# dreamshit — the dream stream's published face

The front for bekh's dream stream. In eva, a machine dreams out loud: every ~5 minutes a new passage of a four-part dream, with a retelling of the dream so far (the **trickle**), an interpreter's **reading**, and a painting per passage. This project is how that stream is *read*: three voices in three columns, paintings living behind the words, a CRT / wired / lain-adjacent atmosphere that is its own thing, and above all effortless reading. Everything in a dream is made by neural networks (LLMs write, ChatGPT paints via codex); our job is the room it's read in.

It lives in eva-goes-berserk as `dreamshit/`, beside `eva/`, `shelf/` and `docs/` — not the instrument (`eva/` is the lab bench) and not text for us, but the published face, named after the door: `https://dreamshit.net`, bekh's domain, public since 2026-09-22 through a Cloudflare tunnel from the mini (`https://dreamshit.x` on the tailnet is the same files, the private twin; `docs/front.md` has where and how). It reads eva's stream over its api and never writes to it. Moved in from its own repo `~/tower/forge/dreamst` on 2026-09-22 by `git subtree`, history kept. A session here doesn't need the root `BRIEF.md`; the root `CLAUDE.md` map is worth having — the front should know what a room, a mark and a plate are. eva's own `/stream` page stays as the placeholder until this front does everything it does.

## How we work here

Two partners, equal minds. bekh's ideas and mine weigh the same: I bring my own taste and objections without waiting to be asked, and hold them until an argument moves them. bekh is the source on what bekh feels; on what looks good, reads well or will work, nobody outranks anybody. We search loosely — nothing about the style is final, and every look or font we try is shelved as a preset, never deleted, because a verdict on one page can flip on another.

Visual work gets judged by eye: screenshots go to `shots/`, opened in Finder for bekh (not kitty).

## Where we stand (rewrite this in place)

- Chosen: newsreader for the passage (labels in spaced Courier); glass at 8px for unfocused plates, dots one click away; focus follows reading; the trickle dripping down its dream's left (next line); quiet voices in `#aab5c7`; a quiet `pic` next to `seed` opens the painting; reader's marks and names as on eva; seed behind a button; the full painting by `pic` only (double-click let go); one plain arrow cursor everywhere; nothing moves on its own.
- **The trickle drips down its dream — the even stretch is the site (bekh's verdict, 2026-09-22: "unfortunately actually the best stylistically").** The seams are joined back into one telling, it stands as a single thread beside the dream's first passage, and three dials in order stretch it to the bottom of the last: narrow the column, then one word to a line, then open the leading. A 45-word telling beside four scenes comes out as single words paced down the whole dream. Defaults in the page, nothing in the url.
- **Everything it beat is kept as a dev option**, nothing deleted: `?trickle=parts` (key `g`) — the telling cut at the sleeper's seams and laid out part n beside passage n, which was the ideal the same morning; `?align=dreams` — stretch with each part paced to its own scene; `?align=band` — dreams with every part held within `?band=` (0.4) of the even pace. What sank the aligned ones is evenness: each part gets its own leading, so a short part opens wide and a long one closes up, and the drip stops being one rhythm; band is the compromise and on story 15 it only bites below about 0.2. Story 15 in all three: `shots/stretch/live-15-even.png`, `live-15-dreams.png`, `live-15-band.png`. `docs/front.md` has the layout and the mismatch rules, `docs/stream.md` the api.
- **The seams stay** even though the even drip ignores them: the phone runs parts and the two aligned drips run on them, and they cost nothing (bekh asked whether the pipes were superfluous now; the answer was this, 2026-09-22).
- **Type (2026-09-22):** every voice is shown lowercase by one CSS rule, the shelf keeps its capitals. The font show is the serious faces at their thinnest — 24 on `f`/`F`, typewriter and pixel faces out of the walk, newsreader on its weight axis with `t`/`T` — and bekh has not yet named a weight; `docs/reading.md`. One dial sizes both side voices (`;`/`'`, `?tsize=`): the trickle at the number, the reading a pixel above it, defaults still 12/13 and bekh's numbers pending — at one word a line 12 is small. **And the passage's ink is his to set** — the bar's `ink` button drops a panel with the presets (`INKS`), the system colour picker and `reset`; only the passage takes it, the marks blend out of it, and it is remembered per browser (`?ink=`).
- Open, in this order: **bekh's weight and size for newsreader** (19px, 400; `,`/`.` and `t`/`T` live, then bake them in) → **the trickle's size** (same, `;`/`'`) → **the wash**: my proposal on the table is an auto wash that reads each plate's brightness behind the words and washes only as much as it needs (`w`/`W` would tune the target contrast), built as a preset beside the fixed `--wa` and judged side by side — the single global number is a compromise between dark and bright plates.
- **Public, done (2026-09-22): `dreamshit.net` is this front, live, reading eva through the same two api paths `dreamshit.x` uses**, via the mirror on the mini. The old line "fed by a pushed feed, never the loom exposed" was not bekh's phrasing and is out — his: the front is connected to eva via the api, that is the site, and it may expand from there. Cloudflare's browser-cache TTL is set to respect our headers, so a deploy lands on a plain reload.
- **Push, not poll (2026-09-22, bekh's call):** every landing on the mac taps the mirror push, and the page holds `/api/stream/events` open instead of polling every minute (`docs/stream.md`).
- Agreed ideas, not built: text arriving live with the tail forgotten (no archive), clickable depth (a side column comes forward), decay that eats words — each must pass the reading rule.
