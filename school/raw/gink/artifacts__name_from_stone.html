Pulsed, Pulsed, Pulsed, the blood As I burned and walked Alone On the far side of the void. And the city rose like Babel As Leviathan tore his chains And from forgotten lore revived Antediluvian pains. And my eyes were locked with God's Whose eyes are stars and night And I saw the swirling mists of time About me in their flight And heard the cries of every soul From ecstasy to blight. And then I saw the void beyond And saw that I alone Must stride across that desolation To its hidden throne Upon whose arms are locked The keys I take my name from stone.
