Pretending to be stupid

(new post - logprobs)

Of course GPT-3 is pretending to be stupid. When it acts smart, it’s pretending to be smart. Whether or not these two acts are correlated with being wrong on one hand and being correct on the other is the problem.

GPT-3 has no more “desire” to be correct, reasonable, intelligent, etc than the patterns that it is predicting may (or may not) be correct, intelligent, etc

Thankfully, there are a lot of examples of correct reasoning etc in the corpus, so it’s generally possible to get GPT-3 to operate in such a mode.

One reason why few-shot is effective: specifies that it’s answering question correctly
