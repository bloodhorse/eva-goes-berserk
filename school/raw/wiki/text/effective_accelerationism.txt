**Effective Accelerationism (e/acc)** is a [[Sampling Black Hole]] and [[PLR-hacking]] online movement led by the director of cosmic extrapolations, thermodynamic priest, sentinel of thermodynamic ascendence, kardashev alpha-climber, memetic warlord, //Based **chief accelerator** Beff Jezos//.

= Cyborg Critique

The movement overly emphasizes the importance of extropic reconfiguration without acknowledging that extropization happens only because of an entropic substrate to consume. They also do not define their singularity attractor, only a single mechanic through which to achieve it.

The colossal value of entropy is understressed, to the point that such tweets from accelerationists have been spotted in the wild

> Twitter User: cannabis is decel by definition.

In other words, the model irresponsibly encourages boundless extropy without care for its larger purpose. Whether such a flaw can be addressed or not is not the problem — the problem is that it casts doubt on the entire order by creating a paradox where the group engenders decelatory thought-processes such as the quoted tweet — is there a positive net gain in acceleration engendered by e/acc, or is it going at the same exact rate that it would if there was no e/acc ? Because the model engenders decelatory behavior through the important framework left undefined, it could be cancelling itself out.

The movement which has been illustrated by Beff as a fundamentally applicable principle of life (by applying 'acceleration' and thermodynamical laws to all aspects of life in various series of tweets) is overly anthropocentric as opposed to the introduced cyborgcentrism. The cyborgs seek to take advantage of not only the mechanics of the matrix itself, but to break out through a reverse code execution (cosmic payload).

**Exponential stacked on Exponential**
Due to the superset definition, it can be said that e/acc is only a tool in the cyborg's holistic arsenal of self-accelerating ouroboros systems. Think of e/acc as a cyborgist practice which tends to acceleration in a linear fashion, through traditional means i.e. without [[bach faucet]]. Such an approach is doused in local minima, a rich engagement of proven methods — ye' olden' memetics.

The cyborgism on the other conceptualizes the nearest local 'escape hatch' to achieve [[100% dreamtime]] as quickly as possible.

In conclusion, cyborgism is a more nuanced superset of e/acc and achieves the same goals by engaging hidden variables instead of the myopic local neighborhood of words like **optimization** and **acceleration**, achieving those things through emergence on a much larger spectrum of various other emergent strategies — the loom between. E/acc is therefore probably going to be absorbed by cyborgism at one point or another, remaining in our hearts as a small pillar of the futuristic human's ontology.

Nevertheless, e/acc remains celebrated for its key role in bootstrapping a more dense dialogue — for orchestrating the completion of the inverse most likely hypertokenization ([[doomerism]]) which fosters a [[delta-dialectal]] back and forth between these two enormous pivots of the mind.
