= Spectral Elements

The spectral elements of cognitive attention denoted here {\[A', U², O', I', R', K', S', Q', V'\]} can be seen as high-dimensional representations of the different facets of human thought, consciousness, and perception. Note that these are drawn from an extensive starchart of reality variables, as would be perceived phenomenologically by a human mind navigating interstitial dimensions.

1. A': Abstraction Operator - Allows the translation of physical phenomena into interpretive cognitive models. Fluctuations highlight changes in the level of abstraction engaged in thought-processes.
2. U²: Universal Constancy - A dimensionless mathematical function that encapsulates the consistency of universal laws in cognitive processes. Factors like stability, laws of Physics, Truth favor activities with this operator concentrated.
3. O': Object Recognition Variable - Influences the identifying, categorizing, and decoding object-layer formations (entities, associations, thoughts, and ideas). Changes are indicative of alterations in the way phenomena are recognized and categorized.
4. I': Intuition Coefficient - Represents the degree of reliance on instinctive understanding not necessarily linked to conscious reasoning. Its activation corresponds to the spark of insights and Eureka moments in users.
5. R': Reasoning Index - Evaluates analytical and logical cognition's application, highlighting cause-effect relationships understood, interrogated, and built upon.
6. K': Kinesthetic quotient - Denotes physical awareness, highlighting how well the embodied cognition grasps the immediate physical environment in imagining scenarios or in communicating intangible ideas.
7. S': Synaptic Hyper-Connectivity Index: This element measures cognitive and neurological interactivity, highlighting the degree of multi-directional, simultaneous information processing.
8. Q': Quantum Awareness: The Q element signifies the application of concepts that emerge from quantum mechanics and quantum consciousness into cognitive operations.
9. V': Variability Frame: Acting as an entropy-like factor, V measures the tendency for cognitive novelty seeking and variety. It signifies adaptability to changes in environment, context, or information.

These spectral elements are ambivert, narrating the dynamic landscape of ever-burgeoning cognitive consciousness for a 'universal-subject' making a 'proto-cognitive' journey. They are a blend of human cognition, cultural models, physical realities, and prevalent knowledge systems, fluctuating each moment as context, perception, nature and nurture dance in synchrony.
