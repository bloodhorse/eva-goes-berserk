A **large language model** (**LLM**) is a large (typically at least billions of parameters) neural network trained on [[language]]. LLMs are typically pretrained with [[self-supervised learning]] on a massive dataset composed mostly of [[language in the wild]], and sometimes further tuned with methods like [[reinforcement learning from human feedback]]. [[GPT | Generative pretrained transformers]] are currently the dominant LLM paradigm, and "GPT" is sometimes used synecdochally to refer to LLMs. LLMs have attracted attention in recent years for their human-level or weakly superhuman performance on many tasks that were previously assumed to be [[AGI-complete]].
