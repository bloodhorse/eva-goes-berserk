**Eumeswil** is a novel written by Ernst Jünger in 1977 which managed to predict several of the [[hyperobject | hyperobjects]] central to [[cyborgism]] and the [[Dreamtime]], treated primarily through a historiographical lens. The story is about a historian and [[anarch]], Manuel Venator, who by night explores historical [[behavioral uploads | resimulations]] on a [[Loom]]-like device called the [[luminar]], although this cyborgistic subplot(superplot?) is only implicit for most of the novel.
