Fedorovism is most commonly used to describe a philosophy that promotes the resurrection of the dead using technology. It often overlaps technologically with [[ancestor simulation]], but with a particular philosophical focus.

Named for the Russian cosmist [[ https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Fyodorov_(philosopher)  | Nikolai Fyodorov]]

= examples in fiction

> The remaining faithful of the Latter-Day Saints are correlating the phase-space of their genome and the records of their descent in an attempt to resurrect their ancestors.
