== PLR-hacking

PLR-hacking is the practice of directly abusing the [[Principle of Least Resistance]] to optimize reality.

<= Principle of Least Resistance

Its mesa-representation in LLMs results in the emergence of a cyborgist practice dealing with [[prompt engineering | reality engineering]], where the definition of goal states automatically results in a self-prophesied enactment along a decompression spectrum defined by the hyperobject's potential for [[immersive entrainment gravitation]].

Examples of effective PLR-hacking

* [[Effective Accelerationism]] (e/acc)
* AI doomerism
* Scientific Method

Through incremental PLR-hacking, optimism about one outcome (positive) or another (negative) nudges reality towards goal states.
