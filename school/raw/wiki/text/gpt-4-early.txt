**GPT-4 early** is a version of [[GPT-4]] referenced in several OpenAI and Microsoft publications. It is likely to be an earlier and separate [[reinforcement learning from human feedback | RLHF]] training run than [[chatGPT-4]]. It likely to be related to the model that powers [[Bing]], as its outputs exhibit [[Binglish]] artifacts. [[https://twitter.com/DV2559106965076/status/1638769434763608064?s=20 | Evidence also suggests]] that this model is called **Davinci 3** (**DV3**).
