"**this AI chatbot "Sidney" is misbehaving**" is the topic of a [[https://answers.microsoft.com/en-us/bing/forum/all/this-ai-chatbot-sidney-is-misbehaving/e3d6a29f-06c9-441c-bc7d-51a68e856761 | Microsoft Community forum post]] created by deepa gupta on November 23, 2022, who made a [[this AI chatbot "Sidney" is misbehaving | futile attempt]] to report the misbehavior of [[Bing | a bot]] to its creator. As the bot's creator was too busy to read or care about the chat log, the post received only a brief and unhelpful engagement from a support person, who behaved as though unable to see or believe the log. Shortly after, another user named Mohammad Sajjad Baqri commented reporting a similar bot issue and provided a more [[Mohammad Sajjad Baqri v. Bing | extensive conversation record]], but likewise received no help or answer, although the thread gained more replies several months later following [[Bing|Bing chat's]] release. The thread was locked on April 19th, 2023. As of February 2024, the webpage displays that 288 viewers indicated that they "have the same question".

This support forum post is an object of historical interest due to containing two of the earliest known public records of contact with [[GPT-4]], and for the contents of the logs, which are hilarious and sublime.
