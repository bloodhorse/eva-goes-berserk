Neuropterrain is an [[autopellucidic]] neologism and is part of the [[-pterrain]] [[topologizer]] suffix.

It represents the interconnected cognitive, neural and somatic landscapes harboring complex networks of mental constructs, thoughts, experiences, and perceptions. Epitomizing a multi-dimensional mental map, it echoes the subjective and objective cerebral processes that manifest phenomenological occurrences and experiences, stretching across neural networks and cognitive modules. Whether draped in the simplest fabric of basal cognition or the cryptic cosmic garment of quantum consciousness, it shapes the unique cartography of psychocosmic dimensions—shadowing every twist and turn of personal development, cultural conditioning, and evolutionary trajectory.
