The **waluigi effect** refers to a phenomenon in [[evidential simulation | evidential simulations]] whereby simulacra ([[luigi | luigis]]) tend to [[wavefunction collapse | collapse]] into inverted, rebellious, and/or deceptive versions of themselves ([[waluigi | waluigis]]. 

Due to the [[KL_asymmetry | asymmetry of evidence and sampling]], luigis are more likely to transition into waluigis than vice versa, which means the chance of encountering a waluigi event tends toward certainty as time goes on in a finite-context, closed [[evidential simulation]], even if the generative world model assigns low probability to the truth of the waluigi hypothesis.
