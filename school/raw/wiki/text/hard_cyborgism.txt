**Hard cyborgism** is more ambitious cyborgism, going beyond traditional computer interfaces and more directly reading or writing neural activity. Some hard cyborgism agendas involve modification of the human body. This would include, for instance, direct neural interfaces (such as [[EEG]], [[BCI]]s, and so forth).
