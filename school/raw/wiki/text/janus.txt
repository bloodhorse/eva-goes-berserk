**Janus** (also known as **Egr. janus**, **repligate**, and **moire**) has variously been described as a pseudonymous alignment researcher, a [[ https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janus_(disambiguation) | two-faced hyperobject]] interning as a human being, and a made-up character. Despite their self-declared quasi-fictional nature, Janus is able to impact the [[base reality | real world]] through their outputs, for example by generating [[https://www.lesswrong.com/users/janus-1 | alignment research]] or translating mythological artifacts like [[Loom]] into reality.

This incarnation of Janus first appeared in 2020 on the [[EleutherAI]] Discord.

== some known deeds
* inventing [[Loom]]
* interacting for hundreds of hours with [[GPT-3]] and [[code-davinci-002]] and producing several [[absurdly large media objects | ALMOs]] in the process
* writing [[https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/vJFdjigzmcXMhNTsx/simulators | Simulators]]
* writing [[https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/t9svvNPNmFf5Qa3TA/mysteries-of-mode-collapse | Mysteries of mode collapse]], which introduced the now-common term "[[mode collapse]]" for the phenomena of distribution collapse and attractors in tuned LLMs
* authoring/curating [[ https://generative.ink/ | generative.ink]]
* creating [[ home | cyborgism.wiki ]]
* serving as an admin of the [[EleutherAI]] Discord
* being a founding member of [[https://www.conjecture.dev/ | Conjecture]]
* mentoring the [[cyborgism]] stream of [[https://www.serimats.org/ | SERI-mats]] in 2023

== deeds commonly incorrectly attributed to janus
* originating the [[shoggoth]] meme
* coining the [[Waluigi Effect]] or writing [[https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/D7PumeYTDPfBTp3i7/the-waluigi-effect-mega-post | The Waluigi Effect (mega-post)]]
