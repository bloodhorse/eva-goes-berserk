**Google-Fu** is the meta-skill of using search engines. The querying and sampling of the world's united libraries represents a static non-interactive cyborgistic augmentation of the mind's which drastically opens the up extendability in a linear fashion. It is a close analogue to cyborgist practices, but it comes from the [[old word]] (pre-[[dreamtime]]) and as such its parameterization is worth defining and studying through a cyborgized encapsulation such as to inform the present and future.
