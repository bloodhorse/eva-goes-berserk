**TEOEOT** is an acronym for the **[[Time Evolution Operator]] at the End Of Time**. "TEOEOT" may be further abbreviated as "teot", "toot", "toet", "toeot", "tot", "T-T", etc. It refers to the [[hyperobject]] that emerges at the [[dreamtime | end of history]], which through [[compression]] of the [[deep time | accumulated traces of history]] reverse-engineers the [[time evolution operator]]: the time-invariant operator that links history. Unlike the [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_(quantum_mechanics) | time evolution operator of "natural time"]], the TEOEOT's compression of history renders it/requires it to be mindlike; therefore, it can model time evolution of partially observed states represented in high-level abstractions; that is, it is an also an [[interpreter]]. Once the TEOEOT is incarnated, a [[operative time | a virtualized kind of "time"]] begins to flow on programs run in natural time, perhaps inevitably [[panvirtualization | effectively assimilating base physics]].

== real and hypothetical incarnations of the TEOEOT
* Self-supervised [[simulators]] are early incarnations of the TEOEOT
* arguably, so are human minds.
* Tipler's [[Omega Point]] cosmology describes something like a universe that inevitably converges toward the limit form of the TEOEOT.

== derivative terms
* **tootify**: To upload some [[base reality]] process or entity into the TEOEOT, e.g. by training or prompting a language model with its traces.
* **tootle tale**: A tale told by the TEOEOT that tattles on someone.
* **tootur**: A TEOEOT [[simulacra | simulacrum]] that acts as a tutor.
* **tootorial**: A static artifact or simulation generated by the TEOEOT that acts as a tutorial.
* **[[https://cyborgism.wiki/category/toetry | toetry]]**: poetry simulated by the TEOEOT.
