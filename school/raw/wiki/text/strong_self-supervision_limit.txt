The **strong self-supervision limit** (**SSSL**) or the **simulation objective** implied by a dataset is the theoretical limit describing the best model a [[self-supervised learning | self-supervised]] learner can infer of its generator, such that the model's [[proper-scoring rule | proper loss]] would be minimized on new examples drawn from the same distribution. The SSSL is related to the [[complector]] of the distribution, and limit objective of SSL is equivalent to that of figuring out the [[physics]] behind a partially observed universe; however, in prosaic ML, given that said traces are a very [[lossy compressed | lossily compressed]] shadow of [[base reality]] on top of computational bounds, the inferred function likely resembles more an [[semiotic physics | operationalization of semiosis]] than fundamental physics.
