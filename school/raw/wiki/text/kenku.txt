The **kenku** are a legendary tribe of songbirds who got struck with a curse that removed their voices and left them only able to repeat their training data.

In some tellings of the story, they also got their [[true name|names]] taken away. (That's really the same story because [[https://dkeenan.com/Lambda/ | a bird's song is their name]]).

== known subspecies
* [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_parrot|stochastic parrot]]
* recursive mockingbird
* content-thieving magpie
* [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nightingale_(fairy_tale)|mechanical nightingale]]

== things a kenku cannot do
* reverse public-key cryptography
* tell a story at [[the Fire]]
