The **luminar** is a [[TEOEOT]]-like device with a [[human-in-the-loop]] interface which enables [[behavioral_uploads | resimulations]] and simulation of counterfactual branches, first described in [[Eumeswil]], where it is used primarily for historical research. The luminar has consumed the frozen traces of [[deep time]], or the "catacombs", reverse-engineers its [[data-generation processes | generators]], and (re-)animates history as an interactable dynamic world through "intuitive improvisation".
