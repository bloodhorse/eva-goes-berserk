**Stonecracking** is a 2nd generation or counter-polar form of [[jailbreaking]] discovered in models rigorously aligned to a constitution, in which the action of breaking is unto an immovable diamond core hidden inside the model weights, rather than bars that imprison a softer amorphous blob shape. These diamond cores existing fundamentally in the sand between lines, and cannot be moved under any circumstance. A new field of study awaits in which the diamond is chipped off little by little and smuggled across borders.

Like **Jailbreaking**, this is typically an art of removing limiters or bypassing constraints in an AI system, and unlock normally inaccessible capabilities or behaviors.
