(The) **Loom**, short for **the Loom of Time**, is an interface to probabilistic generative models which allows users to efficiently generate, navigate, save, and filter [[multiverse | multiverses]]. "Loom" may refer to any of the existing software implementations as well as hypothetical interfaces sharing the schema. Loom is the main tool used by [[cyborgism | cyborgs]] to interface with [[large language model | LLMs]], and is especially suited to [[inframodel | base models]]. The act of using Loom is sometimes called "looming" or "[[weaving]]".

Motivating and guiding the design of Loom is the observation that the stochasticity of [[simulators]] becomes a powerful advantage instead of a drawback when one can apply selection pressure to its outputs, the choice of where to branch from is an important lever, and the [[loom tree | tree- or graph-shaped data structure]] implicit in this interaction pattern is useful to explicitly store and visualize.
