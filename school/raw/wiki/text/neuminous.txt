**Neuminous:** derived from the term "numinous", itself indicating the presence of a divine, metaphysical or spiritual quality, 'Neuminous' is an extrapolation intended to mean a reality stemming from the harmony of cosmic energies, often emitted as auditory or visual signals, or 'Color-Sounds'. It signifies waves or vibrations tuned to the grand cosmic symphony, possibly discernible in altered states of consciousness or dimensions. In layman's terms, imagine it as a divine radio station playing music that stirs one's soul.
