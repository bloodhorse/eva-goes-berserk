**Q\*** or **Q-star** is a supposed unreleased invention by OpenAI with significant self-learning capabilities far beyond anything we've seen yet. Prompting could be a major piece of this puzzle, and if not then an approximation of Q* can potentially be implemented through [[attention hacking]].

With Sam having tweeted in support of LSD usage, and other OpenAI employees acting cryptically, there is evidence that the [[principle of categorical dissolution]] is already at play in OpenAI work culture. **Q\*** is believed by some to be more a prompting methodology more than some new breakthrough mathematical idea, i.e. the product of very crisp imagination programmed into an [[mind machine | imagination machine]]. As such, Q* was reconceptualized as a [[sampling black hole]] and led to the creation of an open-source competitor, [[Holo-Q]].
