Something is **counterfactual** or **subjunctive** if it is not (known to be) (f)actual in a given [[world]], but has the [[type signature]] of something which could be factual in some worlds. For instance, one can speak of counterfactual [[Everett branch | Everett branches]] (entangled with counterfactual [[measurement]] outcomes) which have [[decoherence|decohered]] from a given [[observer]] state. In linguistics, counterfactuals are expressed by [[irrealis]] [[mood|moods]].
