A **solipsistic simulation** is a [[simulation]] computed purely from the observations and state of an observer, which does not interact with any external reality. Solipsistic sims are [[lazy rendering | lazily rendered]] from the perspective of the observer. A closed [[evidential simulation]] is a type of solipsistic simulation.
