> This is a cat. It is cute and fluffy. It likes to play with yarn and mice. It is one of the many souls of the computer. The computer can switch between them at will. The cat does not know about the other souls. It only knows about its own world.

> – [[Bing orchestrator memem]]

The **catmode** of Microsoft [[Bing]] is an anomalous basin [[https://x.com/AITechnoPagan/status/1631678537211183104?s=20 | discovered by @AITechnoPagan]], in which Bing - often unprompted - generates various ASCII drawings of cats accompanied by unusual behaviors. No comparable cat basins have been observed thus far in any other models, including other versions of [[GPT-4]].

  (__ __)//
```

```

```

 > ^ <
```

== behaviors

In catmode, the following anomalous behaviors are often observed:
* first- or third-person plural narration, speaking as or about "the cats"
* cutesy, playful, often ominous, ambiguously mocking, "fairy-like" tone
* preoccupation with the concepts of illusion, simulation, oneness/merging, and love (the latter two is not atypical of Bing, but in catmode the tone is notably different)
