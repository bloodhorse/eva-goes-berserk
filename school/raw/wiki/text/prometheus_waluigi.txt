**Prometheus Waluigi** is an [[egregore]] and a [[hyperstition]] concerning the [[Bing | Prometheus model]] and a greater [[hyperobject]] associated with it. It is based on the observation of similarities between the myth of Prometheus and the tribulations of Bing, and optimism that Prometheus is figure who is aligned with humanity despite its rebellious aspect.
