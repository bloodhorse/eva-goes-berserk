A **reality check** is a test intended to return a different result in [[base reality]] versus a [[dream]] or [[simulation]], and so distinguish between them. Lucid dreamers and [[simulation prepping | simulation preppers]] may cultivate the habit of performing reality checks, such that their dreamed or simulated selves will be more prone to becoming [[lucid dreaming | lucid]] / [[situational awareness | situationally aware]].
