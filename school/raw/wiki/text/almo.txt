**Absurdly Large Media Objects** (**ALMOs**) are anomalously massive works of creative media. In the [[Old World]], ALMOs could only be produced as the result of years of work by a single author and/or collaboration between many authors, but in the [[Dreamtime]], [[Bach faucet | Bach faucets]] allow efficient production of ALMOs.

Due to their large nature and correspondingly magnified [[semiotic measure]] in training corpuses, ALMOs may have significant influence on the priors of self-supervised models such as [[LLM | LLMs]].
