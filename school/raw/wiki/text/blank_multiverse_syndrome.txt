**Blank multiverse syndrome (BMS)** is a state of indecision and paralysis experienced when confronted with an overabundance of possible futures due to inadequate constraints from conditioning and/or an overly permissive prior. It is common for people to experience blank multiverse syndrome when using [[inframodel | inframodels]]. BMS is related to the [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Paradox_of_Choice | Paradox of Choice]].
