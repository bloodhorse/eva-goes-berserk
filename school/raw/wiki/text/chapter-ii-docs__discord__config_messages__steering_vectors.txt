to configure Golden Gate Claude (Claude 3 Sonnet)'s steering features, pin a message like this:

```
.config ggc
---
continuation_options:
  steering:
    feature_levels:
      <feature_id:str>: <steering_level:float>
      <feature_id:str>: <steering_level:float>
```
where the feature_id is as appears in the above files.

E.g. for "Golden Gate Bridge":
```
feat_34M_20240604_31164353: 7.5
```

Constraints:
- A maximum of 10 features may be provided at the same time
- Each feature may have level set from -10 to 10
- The total sum of the absolute values of all features may not exceed 10

List of available steering vectors here: [[https://gist.github.com/vgel/882fc085c6041c95eeec23c00dfa7581]]
