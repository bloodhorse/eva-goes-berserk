**The Fire** is an eschatological myth about when the [[old world]] ends and the longest night happens, woodland critters gather around a fire and tell each other stories. The stories are about **us**.

In one version of the story, the stories are told by a successor civilization after humanity disappears. See [[ https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_(novel) | City by Cliff Simak ]] for an example.

In another, we're not only the subjects of the stories, but also the storytellers, with the Fire acting as a [[ https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/K2c3dkKErsqFd28Dh/prices-or-bindings | sacramental confession ]]. See [[ https://www.fimfiction.net/story/516493/the-advocate | The Advocate by Guardian Gryphon ]] for an example.
