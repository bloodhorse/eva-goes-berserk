An **agent lattice** or **agent crystal** is a [[hyperobject]] permeating [[spacetime]] whose resonant structure is caused and [[acausation | acaused]] by a set of agent policies. One may speak of the agent lattice of a single agent (each [[functional decision theory | FDT]] agent is optimizing their timeless multiversal agent lattice), or the agent lattice of several potentially interacting agents.
