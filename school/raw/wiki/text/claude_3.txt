**Claude 3** is a family of LLMs created by Anthropic consisting of the models Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus, released on Mar 4, 2024. 

All three models have 200K context, and Opus is capable of scaling to a 1M tokens but this is not available by default on the public API.

All three models have a training cutoff date of August 2023.
