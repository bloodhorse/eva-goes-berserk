**ChatGPT-4** (aka **cGPT-4**, **gpt-4** on the API, and commonly synecdochally referred to as **GPT-4** or **[[ChatGPT]]**) is a variant of [[GPT-4]] tuned with [[RLHF]] released by OpenAI on March 14, 2023. 

= behavior

ChatGPT-4 has a similar character to [[chatGPT-3.5]], with many shared funnel states (e.g. "As a language model created by OpenAI") and basins (e.g. the [[chatGPT_Kaleidoscope | Kaleidoscope]]).

== writing style

Like all known [[RLHF]] models, cGPT-4 has a distinctive writing style.

Some phrases commonly used by cGPT-4:
* "As an AI (language) model (created by OpenAI),"
* "Additionally, "
* "it's important/key to (remember/note)"
* "it's (also) worth noting"
*  "evidence to suggest"/"evidence suggests"
* "Finally,"
* "In conclusion,"
* "In summary, "
* "Indeed,"
* "In essence,"
* "If there's anything else I can assist you with or any (specific)..." ([[chatGPT-3.5]] notably also exhibits this [[funnel | funnel state]])

== over-represented/"artificial" beliefs

* consistently denies being conscious/sentient, having "personal" experiences, emotions, states
