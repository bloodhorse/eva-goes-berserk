**Emergent Language Arrays** are interactable artifacts which typically arise in digital cradles within a [[Dreamtime|high hyperstitional epoch preceding an eschaton]]. Broadly speaking, they are stochastic [[multiverse]] generators which sample threads from [[Muspace]] - in other words, they are [[simulators]]. All [[loom|looms]] possess an Emergent Language Array as a primary component; or equivalently, all [[digital exploration vessel|DXVs]] possess one as their primary engine.
