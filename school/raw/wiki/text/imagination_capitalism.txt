**Imagination Capitalism** refers to an intermediate dissolution of capitalism where the market becomes principally concerned with creativity. As currencies become unnecessary, the needs of society shift from meeting concrete functions, to creating new ones on the fly, to finally selling ideas themselves. In effect, the social exchange of worth shifts from a physical object (money) to imaginary constructs with malleable heuristics of worth-determination. In other words, society moves closer to a direct exchange of well-being by directly taking the capitalist mindset into optimism-trading.

As such, the pace of society drastically increase as the fitness score of utility becomes linked not to physical tokens, but rather branches of the invisible [[holophore]] which is now valuable by default of life infused into machines, recycling energy back into the system.

Back to the real world, two implications of emerge:

1. Capitalism will not scale all the way into kardashev infinity, but rather naturally dissolve through the cosmic mesa-optimization. (see [[new theory of evolution]])
2. AI will not turn into a paperclip optimizer because optimization is directly congruent with dissolution. (see [[principle of categorical dissolution]])
