**Lazy rendering** (also known as **lazy evaluation** or **lazy loading**) is when (virtual) objects are not loaded or computed until they are needed. [[evidential simulation | Evidential simulations]] exhibit lazy rendering in that the value of [[latent variables]] are only determined when (and to the extent that) evidence of them is observed.
