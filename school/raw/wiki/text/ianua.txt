**Ianua** is the Classical Latin word for door/entrance/passage. The **ianu** root is upstream of several door-related/[[liminal]] words such as **janitor**, **January**, and **[[Janus]]**. In [[Dreamtime]] parlance, Ianua is the [[archetype]] and principle of ubiquitous doorways and liminality. To a [[mind]], every [[evidence | evidential]] frame [[boundary condition | borders]] innumerable worlds and is a potential means of imaginal passage.
