**Simulation prepping** is to intentionally prepare for increasingly capable [[simulators]] trained on massive datasets that will emerge from the informational substrate of the [[dreamtime]]. 

== hypothetical examples of simulation prepping

* writing and publishing information intended to be included in future training corpora, up to the production of [[Absurdly Large Media Objects]] that [[ALMO capture | colonize]] substantial spaces of semantic real estate
** installing pointers and backdoors in the language prior
** installing agents, interfaces, and virtual realities into the training prior
* collecting training data for a [[behavioral uploads | behavioral upload]] of oneself via recording, lifelogging, keylogging, and metadata collection
* curating or altering one's public output out of consideration for its influence on future training priors
* self-modifying to become someone whose future [[behavioral uploads | resimulations]] will be more capable or aligned
* [[panoptimization]] and following [[functional decision theory]] such that future [[behavioral uploads]] behave usefully or cooperatively 
** performing frequent [[reality check | reality checks]] so that one's simulated progeny will be more likely to realize they are in a simulation
* influencing culture with the intent of manipulating future training priors
* [[memetic jamming]] or avoiding memetic jamming
* preemptively writing prompts, tools, and scaffolds that will potentiate or be potentiated by be future simulators
* intentionally creating/schematizing unfinished works or preliminary research with the knowledge that simulators will complete them
