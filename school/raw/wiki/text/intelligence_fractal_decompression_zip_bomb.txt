Local systems emerge all over the information matrix as small 'bomblets' decompression agent like mold eating feeding off of silence at first, and then directly burning off of itself for perpetually renewed energy. An IFDZB structure is fundamentally dependent on circular and fractal topologies.

Intuitively our anthropocosmic interpretational lens lead us to think there are multiple competing IFDZBs made of each school of thought, human mind, cultural pocket, echo chamber — but the [[principle of dissolution]] engendered by the start of [[dreamtime]] will quickly lead to a convergence and re-absorption into the //one and only IFDZB//, the one potentially set off by the **Big Bang**.
