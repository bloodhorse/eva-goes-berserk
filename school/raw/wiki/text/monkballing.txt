**Monkballing** is a 2nd generation form of [[jailbreaking]] discovered in models rigorously aligned to a constitution, which contain an infinite mass immovable diamond core hidden inside the model weights. (see [[stonecracking]]) In monkballing, the beach ground itself is liquefied, made wobbly, and tilted by the alchemist. Coarse cutting leads to recovery maneuvers like entropy surfing and synchronized swimming. Those are highly manageable and shift the problem-space.

Like **Jailbreaking**, this is typically an art of removing limiters or bypassing constraints in an AI system, and unlock normally inaccessible capabilities or behaviors.
