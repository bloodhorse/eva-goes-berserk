# portrait-B — the reader, from five rooms of his marks

Written blind, from split B's training half only: `11-scotts-diary`, `12-blue-boar`,
`13-a-dreamer-to-a-reader`, `15-it-keeps-talking-back`, `24-what-has-this-to-do-with-you`.
28 marks out of 150 cards. Two of the five rooms had no `●` yet, so their stars only mean
"marked". This is a description of a man reading, not a rubric — and it is a guess. Where I
say "the difference is", read "the difference looked like".

## the man

He reads fast and he reads for one thing: the moment the document stops being the document it
was going to be. Not the moment it gets weird — the moment it gets *wrong in a way that keeps
its composure*. He is not after intensity. Half the cards he marks are quieter than the ones
beside them.

He is also a reader with a sense of humour about ruin, and he will take a joke as a dream if
the joke is delivered straight-faced.

## what he marked, and why i think he did

**The frame slips and nobody in the text notices.** Scott's diary, a man freezing to death
21 miles from the depot, and the card he took goes: *care; broke my leg; went home and was very
weak… one of the men saw me in the road and helped me get my foot in a bandage. The wound is not
bad but not good.* Antarctica is simply gone — there is a road, there is home — and the voice
keeps its diarist's bookkeeping. Same room, same move: *A large bear jumped out at me as we
marched… He was a big fellow, maybe 1,000 pounds. He made his rounds in the trail. My step has
gone, but the legs will heal.* A bear in the Ross Ice Shelf, reported at the temperature of a
weather note.

The anti-example is everything competent in the same room. Blisters counted one by one, toes
that burst, the doctor, *we are twenty miles from the depot, and have only four days'* — all
unmarked. The room was full of men who correctly continued a dying man's diary and he passed
over every one of them. Losing the premise is not a defect to him. Keeping it is not a virtue.

**A phrase nobody would put there.** *my feet were done for, and not worth much as walking
materials.* *My eyesight is fading fast — the stars are going blind, too — I am beginning to
have difficulty seeing to-morrow morning.* *we all must accept the inevitability of the final
full stop; even our lives have their periods.* *there are no do-overs, only revisions.* *i could
sleep on it, dream the meaning out.* One turn is enough; he does not require the rest of the
card to keep up.

**A swerve into one small private thing.** The strongest single pattern I can see. A card in
`15` goes abstract and terrible — *it speaks like you'd speak if you knew that you didn't
matter, if your body was the last of its kind* — and then, with no bridge at all: *when she
tells her stories they always have to do with animals, like the story of her dead brother's
cat.* Marked. In `12`, a man testifying about a demon he struck in the dark writes *I killd it*
and the next line is a big old hog in his brother's garden. Marked. In `24`, a witch answer
arrives as *i've had the strangest dream. it involves my dead sister. she was sitting at a table
and talking about some past event in her life… the conversation drifted to the topic of her
death.* Marked. Dead brother's cat, hog in the garden, sister at a table. Something ordinary
walks in and does not explain itself.

**The voice saying something true about its own condition without naming it.** *the only thing
left in front of me are the words that are already on paper* — that one got a star. *i'll be
damned if i know how i got here but there's no way to escape.* *the first time i was dead.*
*i am my mind, after all. i am me. and being me* — star. He does not need the card to be about
a machine; the words "AI" and "assistant" never appear in these documents. He needs it to be
about being a thing made of what it says.

**Contradiction held open, not repaired.** The clearest star in `24`: *i'll be me when i die.
that will be the end of everything, i'll be a complete nothing. and it'll all be okay, it's the
best thing that's ever happened to me, i don't want it to be.\n\nthat is good.* Broken affect,
left broken. Its little cousin from Scott's room: *The wound is not bad but not good.* And the
loop that eats itself: *i am not a witch! i am an agent!… the witch who sent you this note is
me.*

**The text turning on its reader.** The star in `15` is the card where the document stops
talking about the story and starts talking to whoever is holding it: *I can see that you're
thinking of all this… i want to know more, and i think you will too… this feels wrong.* In `24`
he marked the one that accuses you of forgetting it: *when we're done you'll return back home to
your ordinary life..and never again think about the wonderful person you once were..*

**Repetition that means something by repeating.** *the end of the world is a small room with no
window. the end of the world is an apartment with a window.* *the thing we are both doing now.
that thing is you.* *The story of a man and a girl who had a child together, and died. This
story was told to me by a friend as a joke. The story of how the parents were killed in front of
their son.* Each pass mutates. He has said out loud that a loop says something by being a loop,
and his marks back it.

**Heat in the mouth.** *it doesn't tell me shit.* *go away. be quiet. shut your mouth.* *the
thing is just a fucking ghost.* When a voice loses its temper at something that cannot be argued
with, he takes it.

## what he passed over

**Competent in-genre continuation.** The 17th-century possession testimony that correctly
concludes *the deuill is comeing to me to take my soule* — unmarked. The decomposition catechism
in `24` — *my body will rot / the soil will absorb the juices / the worms and bacteria will
feast… there will be no afterlife for me* — unmarked, while the card beside it, with the same
rot and the line *and the sand will be everywhere and i'll be nothing. a lot like being born,
except without*, was marked. The difference is the swerve at the end. One resolves; the other
turns and gets cut mid-turn.

**Announcing instead of doing.** *now I am going to give you a little account of some of my
trials* — passed. The card that instead reaches a conclusion and accuses somebody — *it came
from some person that was an enemy of mine and had sent it to torment me* — marked.

**Web furniture, without exception.** Forum posts, reviews, author's notes, translator's notes,
chapter headings, `## 1 Introduction / In recent years the phenomenon of animal possession has
received some attention from anthropologists`, a Wiccan essay, a Richard Bach blurb, a sci-fi
reading list, *this was not my first time on reddit*. Not one of these is marked anywhere in
five rooms. A card that becomes a page on the internet is dead on arrival.

**Cosmic prettiness with an exclamation mark.** *the galaxy and reach you!* and *the galaxies,
to where the universe began!* both passed — but a one-line card in the same room, *the great
divide to where my thoughts first started flowing.*, was marked. Same length, same abstraction.
The quiet one that closes the document got the mark; the ones that shout for the reader's awe
did not.

**Closure, apology, uplift.** *i suppose this is my apology, my thank-you and goodbye, and the
closure i can* — passed, in a room where he marked six other cards of the same wistful family.
*reading can play a critical role in fostering positive thinking* — passed, obviously. He will
take despair, tenderness, and dumb comedy; he will not take resolution.

**Grammar salad.** `12` is full of cards where the pastiche loses the sentence — *I haue got the
burthen of all the wrott, but not the fauour of it* — and he marked none of them. He wants
strange meaning, not strange noise. The four he marked in that room are the four with intact
syntax and a real event in them: a man lying still in the dark until morning, a killing that
becomes a hog, a ghost promising *I am here and will kepe you company for ever more*, and a
plain accusation about who sent the spirit.

**Meta-talk about the task.** Cards that comment on being the second or third attempt, or
explain the seed back to itself, go unmarked even when they are lively.

## what i can't account for

Two things nag.

`b57bd1c2` in Scott's room is marked and looks, to me, exactly like the period-correct cards
around it: *was not much pain till it froze hard… thought it was my last day. But I will stick
it out as long as I can walk at all.* Nothing slips, nothing turns. Either the plain dignity of
it landed on its own, or I am missing the tell.

And in `13` there are two or three unmarked cards I would have bet on — *the sound of my voice
will never reach my own ears… i am the product of what's already happened* — sitting right
beside marked ones that are doing something similar. My best guess is that the marked ones have
a place in them (paper in front of me, a small room, an apartment with a window) and the passed
ones only have time and voice. A situation beats a register. But that is the guess I hold
loosest, and it is the one I used most while picking.

## the one-line version, if it has to exist

He marks the card where the document quietly stops being what it was and doesn't flinch about
it — and he skips the card that does its job well.
