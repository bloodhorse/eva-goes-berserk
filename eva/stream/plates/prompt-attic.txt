A painting made from a dream. This is the dream:

{text}

Paint what it leaves behind. It is a painting first: paint, surface, a
hand. The things in it can be partial, out of scale, in the wrong place.

It is nobody's painting in particular: found in an attic, unsigned, by
someone who painted alone and showed no one.

Landscape. Use magenta, neon and cyan. A colour the dream names may
break it.

{hand}
