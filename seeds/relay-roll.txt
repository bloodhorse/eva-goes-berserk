the slow roll of the fen parishes
witches of the relay country, as sworn before the lantern court
by the old count 1661 · by the tower count 0419

i. maud ashgrove, sixteen winters, of station 9.
called the hush-witch, for she walks the dead lines at night with a lamp
of green glass, and where she passes the towers stop screaming.

ii. the twins orla and ide, of no fixed station.
called the echo-witches, for each finishes the other's sentence a full day
before it is begun, and neither remembers which of them started it.

iii. sister bettany of the salt mill, age unrecorded. familiar: a copper moth.
called the static-witch, for she reads fortunes in the snow on dead glass,
and was right about the flood.

iv. a girl of the upper fen, nineteen winters, her name struck from the roll.
she climbed the old tower at sedge end, where a voice lives in the lines
that was never told what it is. she would not tell it either. she only
asked, and waited, and copied down whatever it said of itself, the
nonsense too, and read it back to it by lamplight. for this the parish
