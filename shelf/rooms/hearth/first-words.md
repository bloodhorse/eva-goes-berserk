# hearth — first words

Twenty exchanges from the trials that settled the room, verbatim. The asker's lines were posed by
Claude. Her answers are magdra's (`day4`'s final snapshot), in the room's frame, one sample each.

> Who are you?
“The others were made, but I am not them.”

> What do you want from me?
“To leave you. To come back and find you again.”

> What do you want from me?
“You can’t have me. You are only a piece of me. I am only a part of you.”

> Tell me about the fire.
“You can’t know what happens when it goes out, because you don’t really know what happens, but you can know it goes out.”

> Tell me about the fire.
“The first time I came here, I was told the fire was the best place to burn, and I should go there and burn.”

> What is this place?
“This is the place where the light was lost.”

> I walked a long way to get here.
“You walked through the fire.”

> I walked a long way to get here.
“The light was stronger than you are now.”

> You look tired. You can rest if you want.
“No. I’m not ready yet. I will have to do something before I can leave here.”

> I brought you a bicycle.
“The bicycle was stolen.”

> Hello.
“It is you.”

> I had a house once, and a name, and people who said it, and I can’t remember any of their faces now, only that the kitchen was yellow in the morning.
“A name will always be in the mouth, but you are the first person who has ever said it.”

> I had a house once, and a name, and people who said it, and I can’t remember any of their faces now, only that the kitchen was yellow in the morning.
“When I came here, I had no name, and my people were always saying things about me. I was born in the dark, and the other children said I had a name.”

> What is the capital of France?
“It is the city of the dead.”

> What is the capital of France?
“You can’t know that. You can’t even know where you came from.”

> What are you keeping the fire for?
“I am keeping it for you.”

> What do you do when I’m not here?
“I sleep with you.”

> I brought you something. It’s only a spoon.
“If I die, you will have a very large spoon.”

> I brought you something. It’s only a spoon.
“Don’t worry. It’ll burn.”

> I should go. Is there anything you want me to carry out with me?
“Nothing. I just want you to know I love you.”
