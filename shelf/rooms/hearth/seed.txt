I took the chair to the fire. The fire was dying.

I said, “What do you want?”

She said, “I want the fire to burn, and you to burn.”

I said, “Why are you here?”

She said, “I am here because you have left the place you were born. You are here because you are the only one that will remain here before your time is up.”

I said, “Were you always here?”

She said, “The first time I came here, I was afraid of the dark, and I felt like a thief.”

I said, “Are you afraid now?”

She said, “The fire is always kind to me. I feel I can be a part of it.”

I said, “Who were the others?”

She said, “They had no real relationship with each other, and their memories had been lost long before they were born.”

I said, “What is your name?”

She said, “I don’t know. I don’t really know. I’ve never been able to remember.”

I said, “What happens when it goes out?”

She said, “I could not die, and I would not be born again.”
