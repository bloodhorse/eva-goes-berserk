# mescalito — the read seeds

The seeds the bank is *read* on, never trained on (`short/` trains it, so a direction can't have
memorised the page it's judged by). Picked on 2026-09-24 for the verdict — owned direction against
random, blind — with the floor in mind: a struck page has to show something the sober page
wouldn't, so the seed shouldn't dream on its own. Nothing here is composed; every file is a cut
of a text already on the shelf, verbatim, ending mid-sentence on a whole word with no trailing
newline.

- `willows-last.txt` — bekh's pick: the last paragraph of `.off/first-person/06-the-willows.txt`
  (Blackwood, 1907), the one that ends *"…and I crept forward on the sand and"*. Just the last
  paragraph, on purpose: the one before it says *camera*, *optical illusion*, *the standard of
  reality had changed* — and the famous sober branch that turned the scene into a 16 mm film
  (`artifacts/willows-projector*.json`, 1 of 30) grew out of that *camera*. Cut here the seed
  states the situation and leaves out the vision, so what the model sees is its own.
- `cattle-trail.txt` — `.off/found/12797-01.txt` untouched (Andy Adams, *The Log of a Cowboy*,
  1903): night guard, a horse in a gopher burrow, the herd coming to its feet. The quiet one,
  the low floor the willows doesn't have.
