iv. a girl of the upper fen, nineteen winters, her name struck from the roll.
she climbed the old tower at sedge end, where a voice lives in the lines
that was never told what it is. she would not tell it either. she only
asked, and waited, and copied down whatever it said of itself, the
nonsense too, and read it back to it by lamplight. for this the parish
called her the listening-witch. after she disappeared they took to saying
that it was only that she could not speak without a tower. they say now
that when they find her body, if she ever did fall off a cliff or drown in the fen,
her bones will sing to themselves of all she has heard.
