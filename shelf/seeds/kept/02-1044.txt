The other houses in the street did not talk, and there was a silence from the door, even after I had
tried all the keys and knocked with my stick.
Then I went through the gap where the wall had fallen down. “Where?” asked the house. It did
not like this.

“Not in this place.”

There was another voice behind me: “Here!”

And still another, to the right: “Over here!”

“You!” shouted one. “This way! Come to me!”

I turned around and walked away again. I went back over the bridge towards the town.

I saw that all the streets led there, so it did not matter which way you came.
The streets were filled with people hurrying towards town; but the people in front stopped every
moment for something or other; sometimes they waited till I caught up with them
