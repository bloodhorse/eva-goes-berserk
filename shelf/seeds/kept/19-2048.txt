i have no more faith
than you do. all i've got are names.
my hands ache from holding these letters like a spell i'll never be able to
finish, and when the wind gets up and the dust stirs and the grit flies like a
dream i dream i'll be taken with it.

it was easy to write down what they were called. but who are they?
it was easier still to look at their names than into their eyes. their eyes
are gone now and they won't come back. they're looking somewhere else. there's
something in them that looks like an end of things or like they can see an
end of things that they're not supposed to.
