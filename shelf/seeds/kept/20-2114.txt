it was easy to write down what they were called. but who are they?
the ones that get caught by the wind in the corners of our mouths,
and make us feel we've said something true for the first time?
the ones that walk alongside us as if we were in a crowd of people
but alone? who are the ones who stay in your mind like a photograph
in a book you know will never be found again?

it's hard to say who these ones are, because their names are not their names;
their names are their actions: they're the ones that walk so fast down
the road ahead of you that you have to run to catch up.
