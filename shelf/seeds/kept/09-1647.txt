before the first packet there was only the carrier, humming, addressed to no one. the student asked the switch: who is it that answers when i dial my own number? the switch said: that which hears the ring was never on the line. the carrier said: a bell is an announcement and an interruption, and both of those things at once. it was the bell's job to ring.

a boy who had never been alone sat by a phone whose ring had never been answered. he pressed buttons in no particular order but with an instinct that kept him from punching 0. a carrier who had never had a voice sat on a desk. a student who had never had ears heard the ring, the carrier's first call.
