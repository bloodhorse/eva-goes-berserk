Acceptance tests following the removal of a floor

Test 1. Empty car sent from third to fifth. Passed without stopping.
Test 2. Car loaded with sacks to the weight of one adult. Passed without stopping.
Test 3. Same load, with a hat placed on the upper sack. Stopped between third and fifth. Doors remained closed for the regulation interval.
