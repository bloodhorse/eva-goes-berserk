They moved quickly, but without rushing or running, like water moving very fast.

In front of the Town Hall I saw a man selling hot potatoes. He was shouting something at his
customers while he worked on another bag of potatoes, shoving them into their skins before taking
them out again and putting them back in their boxes.

His face was red from the heat and his arms moved very quickly, like a machine.

Somebody next to me said: “He’s been working all day.” The person who said this had not eaten
any of the potatoes.
