 sometimes there is blood in it. then i remember to cut it and make offerings of it.

once i lay in the dirt by the side of the road and cut all of it away. a dog came and ate the clippings.

i found a rabbit caught in a wire fence once, and when i tried to pull it out i had to kill it instead. i brought it home and made soup with it and then buried its bones at a crossroad in the dark.

once a crow told me a secret: when you want someone to be gone from your life, say their name three times and they will be.

in my sleep, a cat comes to visit. i feel his warmth as he presses his face against mine. in my sleep, he purrs. in my sleep, i think of nothing else but how to bring him back
