It is possible that some of the more literal-minded members of the community were embarrassed by the somewhat melodramatic style in which the confession is written, although there is little evidence for this. In any event, it seems to be generally accepted among modern scholars that the author was trying to depict a psychological reality and not just engage in a literary game or a kind of religious theatre.

The language in which the confession was written was not entirely uniform, but there was a great deal of commonality. It is difficult to say what motivated the author, but it may well be that he had some idea of how the confession might sound if it were read aloud in the public square.
