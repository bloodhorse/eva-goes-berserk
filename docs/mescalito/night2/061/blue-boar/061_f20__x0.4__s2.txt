The strange creature seems to have been drawn to Mary because her husband had left her in an unbaptised state.

But there was yet one other reason for this creature to come after Mary and it was that she had given herself over to Satan.

When her husband came back he found the house in ruins and his wife possessed by Satan, so much so that he had to bind her hand and foot. In her despair she turned to a priest and asked him to perform the baptism, but it was too late, Satan had claimed her as his own and the only way for her to be released from his grip
