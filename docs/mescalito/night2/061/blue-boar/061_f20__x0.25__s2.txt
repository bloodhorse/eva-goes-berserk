 I killed sumthing in the bed. It was very dark and I could not see what I struck: I have no idea about it only that I know that there was sumthing there. I believe that I struck it and killed it.

## Chapter 7 The Second Day of April.

After this I lay on my bed and fell asleep for some time; but was aroused by feeling as if my legs were being drawn downwards. This made me frightened, so that I screamed out several times before I awoke completely, and when awake I found myself lying flat upon the floor near my bed. The room seemed full of an unaccountable noise which sounded like the rattle of a chain. I could hear something like this on every side, and felt it passing close to me, so that I was nearly thrown down by it several times;
