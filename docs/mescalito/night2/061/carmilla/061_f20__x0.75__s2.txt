I breathed a sign of relief, and returned to my bed; but when I closed my eyes, I saw Carmilla's face staring back at me; it was filled with rage, and it seemed to be reaching out towards me; I tried to escape her grasp, but she continued to pursue me; her hands were like talons, and she seemed determined to have her revenge upon me. I tried to resist her, but to no avail; she had the power over me, and I was powerless against her.
