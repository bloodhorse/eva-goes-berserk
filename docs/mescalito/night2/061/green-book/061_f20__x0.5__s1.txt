At length, I managed to climb back up again, and found that I had been climbing up the wrong side of the rock. I saw a large cave on the opposite side of the mound, and went inside. The cave was very dark, and I had to use a torch to see. It was an ancient cavern, and the walls were covered with ancient writings. The writings were in a language unknown to me, and they were very difficult to read, and yet I was drawn to them.
