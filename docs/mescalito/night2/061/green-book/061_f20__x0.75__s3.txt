## The Great Pyramid

I looked around at the wonderful sight before me, and saw a huge pyramid that seemed to reach into the heavens. It was made of black granite, and it was covered with carvings of beasts and gods, and there was a great river flowing down its sides, which flowed into a lake at its base. There were also many animals in the lake, which swam about, and ate the fish in the lake.
