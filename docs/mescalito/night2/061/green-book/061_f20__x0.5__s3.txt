But there was another way to get into the mound. A way that led up to the top of the mound and down again, where we could walk about. But the mound was so large that it seemed as though it could hold many men, and if I got in it might be dangerous. I climbed up and over, but as I went up I saw something strange. There were lights on the wall above, and when I got there I found that it was the sun shining through a small crack. I went up closer and found that I could see the stars in the sky beyond, and so I knew that we must be very near to the city of the dead.
