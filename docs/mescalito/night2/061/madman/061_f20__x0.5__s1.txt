I now hastened home to the council hall and took the pen in hand. I wrote as follows: "I am the secretary of the city council and I am writing this letter to you to tell you that we are in trouble. Our noses have been damaged by the excessive cold. We need your help to repair our noses. Please come to our office immediately."

Wellington was taken by surprise and immediately went to his office. As soon as he entered the room, he was attacked by the members of the council. He was surprised and began to fight back. He fought like a mad man until finally he was overpowered. The members of the council were victorious. [end of text]
