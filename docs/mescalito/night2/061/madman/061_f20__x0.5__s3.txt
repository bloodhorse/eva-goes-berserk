However, this was not so easy to accomplish, since there was a general panic among the population; every man had his nose and his wife's nose and his child's nose on his mind, and could think of nothing else.

Finally, I made use of a stratagem: I sent messengers to every district and told them that the earth had sat upon the moon and that every citizen must go forth and defend his home against the attack of the earth. This stratagem proved effective: the people flocked to the council-house and were eager to fight for the honour of their nose.

The fight lasted two hours; during these two hours the moon was covered with blood, but at last it fell silent, and the people went home in triumph, singing and shouting
