I have heard the terrible news of what has happened to me. It is too horrible. My whole family has perished, because the earth sat on the moon. They are dead! My mother and father, my brothers and sisters, are no more. And the whole world has perished! We are alone, and I am the only survivor. But I will avenge my family! I will kill those who caused the death of my family! [end of text]
