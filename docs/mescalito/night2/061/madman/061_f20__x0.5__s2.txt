But I must now conclude these notes with one word about the history of our earth. After the destruction of our world by fire, it remained for two thousand years without any trace of life; only after three hundred years had elapsed did mankind begin to feel its way into the darkness and begin to rebuild itself again. At last it began to build up cities and temples, and in the year 432, after the death of our first king, there was born a great leader, a mighty hero who ruled the whole world and was called the father of mankind.
