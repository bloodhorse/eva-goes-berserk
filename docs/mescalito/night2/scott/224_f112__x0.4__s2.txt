Monday, March 19.--Today we are still here. Nothing has changed--still wind N.E., force 8, temp. -37°F.; snow falls as though to stay forever; visibility zero. Only three men left alive and well; other two men are dying now; nothing moves except snow, wind and ourselves; we can go nowhere; food is not for us; sleep will come and I pray for it; we are all sick--I'm too sick to be ill--all hope lost, all is death, we are done.
