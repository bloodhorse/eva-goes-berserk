Dr. Wilson did not feel well at lunch-time, so we were not long starting. He said his pulse was 67; that he should lie still to-morrow, if possible. He could not sleep for coughing and breathing all last night.

The day was very cold and clear; we had wind against us, and very hard going; all day it blew fresh; temp. about -40°. In the afternoon there was no wind, and the travelling was better. At 1 P.M.
