Poor Oates gave out and walked on to his death. He died a brave soul. The dogs were horrible last night; some of them came into our tent and wanted food; they howled, fought, and growled; but there were many good ones; one licked my nose when I fell asleep and another licked my feet; they are much smarter than any beast you ever saw, but some were simply savages. Scott had two men, and I two dogs, go out in the night. There was a great noise and a dog was shot.
