Friday, March 23.--No change. Weather same. The men are giving out fast and we can do nothing but lay still till the wind shifts. I wish I had time to write all our adventures here.

March 30.--Had another spell of bad luck--the ice broke and most of my party were drowned. Only one man and myself escaped by climbing a tree. We held on for three days and three nights without food or sleep; then I got down to look after my watch and diamond ring which I always wore round my neck; it was gone--stolen, I suppose.
