We now have no dogs left, nor provisions, and we cannot take more than four miles' worth of supplies at a time.

Tuesday, March 20.--Now is the day and age. Now is the hour to which I must come, for the great adventure. Now I have eaten my last meal, for it is over. What has happened has happened.

Thursday, March 22.--Yesterday we had no march, as it blew like hell. To-day, Thursday, I have had enough and I shall die in my bed.
