Last evening I forgot to put my diary book into my pocket; but never mind; this is not worth recording.

Best wishes from the wilds of Labrador,
Your faithful and obedient servant,
Ever.
S.S.
A few years ago there were many Eskimos living in Labrador.
They used to tell us tales of white men, and of their brave deeds.
