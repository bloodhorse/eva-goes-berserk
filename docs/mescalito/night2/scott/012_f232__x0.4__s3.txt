Monday, March 19.--This morning, lunch, I have no feet; all are gone--I have nothing left to stand upon; they all come in like a troop of horse; no more feet of clay; no more feet. It will be a better place for ever. Now I have learnt my lesson, and there are some who have learnt by bitter experience to choose God's side against Satan's. It seems clear that all will be over in God's plan; there is a brighter place to which I shall rise, never to sin any more.
