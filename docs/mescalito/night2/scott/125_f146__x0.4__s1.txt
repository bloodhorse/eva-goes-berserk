March 19.--Still at 76° 11' N., 57° 10' E.; the sun is gaining perceptibly.

We have lost the rest of our teeth and our gums bleed when chewing--we cannot chew cold food. Toothache is constant.

The storm lasted 40 hours, and now is calm with light breeze. All the canvas on sledges is worn off, and some of the woodwork; sledge irons and chains are nearly gone through; we are reduced to two tents.
