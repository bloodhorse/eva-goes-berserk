The going is very heavy. The men are quite worn out.

There is no food for dogs--only enough for the weakest. Dogs killed to-night. We have finished our sledges; all except two bags of stores are dumped; fuel is going.

Sunday, March 19.--Temperature 42° below zero. Great God! This is an awful place.

We marched till 2 P.M.; then camped for want of food for dogs and the impossibility of feeding them while marching.

All day today we travelled on smooth ice, the surface marked by numerous sastrugi. In the evening it began to snow and the driving mist partially hid the crevasses.
