Monday, March 19.--Sledges gone and the wind rising. We were in camp, lunch, when a breeze sprang up and made my tent begin to strain so that I could not support mine, so I got ready to clear out. At once the other tents went over; some took 3/4 of an hour to put things straight and it was 5 p.m. before they came. There was a bit of pan with 3/4 pint of water in it weighing 2 1/2 lbs. We thought it better to save this, though there was much grumbling, as it was a very important meal; the ice is now melting fast and will be gone soon.
