In a day or two, we will march forward again; will try another way. We shall see more ice, and better, too. We cannot make such bad blunders as these again. There is something wrong, some want of power in ourselves. If there be any more power, then we must use it.

My back was injured in the night; I was caught in a crevasse. I fell on the right foot. The cold is intense; can hardly hold this pen, nor write legibly, either. The night is long, and cold. [end of text]
