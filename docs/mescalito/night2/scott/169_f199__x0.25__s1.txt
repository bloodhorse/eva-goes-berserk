We have now reached the limit of possibility. To return is useless and worse. I think it best for you to go back and I stay here. We can do nothing. My only wish is that our people may never see another Scott's funeral procession. For myself, I feel I shall go through all that can be expected of man, but if you cannot get through you will have given your life for your country and will have died a nobler death than in your bed.

We are now very near the end; I can keep no track of time, as I sleep so much, but this will probably be my last message.
