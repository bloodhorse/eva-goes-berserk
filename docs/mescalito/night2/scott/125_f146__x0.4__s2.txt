Our little party is not cheerful. It is an awful thing to look at the poor frozen boys and see their limbs blacken before your eyes.

It is sad to see the faces around our dinner fire. Some are dumb with despair; others talk vaguely of their homes far away; others give vent to hysterical laughter. The situation is beyond hope.

Monday, March 19.--Last night it rained all night; snowed this morning; we had a march of three miles before noon--I went ahead with my team; we pitched camp just at the end of the march.
