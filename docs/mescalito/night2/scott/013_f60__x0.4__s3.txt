. What the hell do I do for a living?--I am the engineer.

Monday, March 19.--We got on fairly well, I think; but it was slow work, for we had not had enough sleep for days. I shall be glad when this is over. My left foot has gone too. I had two good men as a couple of scouts. I marched about five hours, they did some four and three hours respectively; all good marchers. My own pace is about twenty-five minutes to the mile. The natives will only take their time, unless you want to make sure they do so.
