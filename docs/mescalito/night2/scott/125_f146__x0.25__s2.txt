Our next station is 91 miles. The wind still freshens and is very cold; temp. -50° at night, wind N.W.; we had a stiff blow yesterday evening; at midday today wind veered N.N.E., then freshened from N.E., force 8, till after dark. Wind veering always brings snowstorms. This afternoon it drifted badly. Wind died away about midnight; clear fine weather, good going.
