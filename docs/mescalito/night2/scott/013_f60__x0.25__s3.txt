Monday, March 19.--No wind last night. I wonder if there is any connection between this fact and our food? Perhaps this is the cause of the bad effect of curry powder. In spite of our care, there was no wind or drift all night; all fell asleep over it.

We killed the other madman; he got at the food box and ate up two rations before we could stop him.

Tuesday, March 20.--Still no wind or drift, so we did nothing but eat our rations; killed a third madman; killed two dogs.

Wednesday, March 21.--Same again; the dogs were mad
