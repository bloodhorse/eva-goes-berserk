Tuesday, March 20.--Have got on pretty well, and have kept up to time with the sledge. Marching in bad weather is slow and arduous work; we were often stopped by blizzard; one night was so bad we had to camp, as could not march; that morning my feet were so badly frozen I could do nothing with them all day--could just keep warm.

Thursday, April 5.--We have had no rest yet; there are still 196 miles to the depot and 93 back to the Pole, where our tents and stores are. But we cannot reach the Pole before winter, if we make for depot at once; so shall have to take a turn round the Pole, say 7
