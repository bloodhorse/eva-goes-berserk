In the evening, we went back and got some fresh air (and were nearer the station) and my friend was nearly suffocated in the snow storm.

There must be a relation between the quantity of curry and the number of toes eaten off; I mean the quantity eaten by each person (there's no telling whether one man ate more than another); the relation is that of equality; you must eat more if your friend eats less curries. What about my friend?

From the position of the curry cans you can deduce something else about the dead bodies found there. In this room are five people, and I know something about them: 3 are men; 1 is woman; 2 are cannibals, who each ate two other people, which I did not see.
