Our leader and driver were talking last night, and the doctor told us that if we continued to march at this slow rate, he estimated that 50 per cent. would be lost within four weeks, and if this rate could not be improved upon he must recommend retreat or some action.

Dr. Wilson thinks we can still hold on to the sledges for one week, and after that I don't suppose many of them will last three days.
