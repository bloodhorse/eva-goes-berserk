In my pain I drank the last drop of brandy--I have got a flaskful and must use it again to save another. There are only four flasks full, but the brandy can be used several times; when the contents of any flask have been used once it is empty till I refill it. (All the flasks were refilled with rum or brandy after we left the train.)
