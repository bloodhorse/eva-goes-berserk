To-morrow I hope we shall reach the depot with the dogs all right and strong and eager to go back again for their mates and food. Then, after a rest, they will start again--that is if this foot goes no farther back. My hands have got worse from lack of sleep, which is caused by bad indigestion--they are covered with chilblains; at lunch we tried the "Hudson Bay" brandy, found it very good. I have taken two ounces since then, and feel much better for it. [end of text]
