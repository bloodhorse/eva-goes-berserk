This day has been our blackest yet. In the forenoon a sudden drop in temperature and heavy drift reduced speed to 0; but for lunch a change took place; and after lunch things seemed better. After dinner came disaster; temp. fell suddenly to -67°, with drift of fine particles which, combining with our perspiration, produced blisters all over us. We marched into camp at 9 p.m., exhausted and feeling our age, and by midnight our feet had frozen badly.
