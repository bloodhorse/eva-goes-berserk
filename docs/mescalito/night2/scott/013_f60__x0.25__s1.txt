I think my nose has been going since morning; it is only a cold, of course. It will be a cold day indeed that ever I spend away from my wife.

Tuesday, March 20.--Two more have dropped out--Clayton and Langley. They both had snow blindness; now that my glasses are broken, they are no earthly good to us; and they, having never learned to use them, are dead blind. They can just see a black object at 30 feet.
