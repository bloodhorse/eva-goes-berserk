Dr. Wilson made the diagnosis; he saw my face change while the rest of my company were getting ready for a day's march. "How do you feel to-day?" I replied "Not so well." He put his hand on my pulse, which he said was very feeble.

I lay on the snow most of the time to-day, with the cold wind blowing across me; tried to read my Bible and get up courage.

The tent was taken down to-night to give us another start, though it is only a mile farther. Our strength is almost gone.

The march begins tomorrow, at half-past seven.
