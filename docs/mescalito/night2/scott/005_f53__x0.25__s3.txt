We have been losing strength for some time back; for last few marches our physical condition has been painful in the extreme; we look like walking skeletons; all the party suffer severely from frequent fainting; heart-sick at times.

I can hardly hold the pen to finish this sentence.

In short, our case is serious.

Yours truly,

A. F. M. Wills

SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS,
March 18, 1898.
