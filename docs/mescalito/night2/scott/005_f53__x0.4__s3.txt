 have heart to try for a mule, so we came on to this spot for repairs. Am sure we will get through yet. Had to have a few drops of brandy for refreshment. What would we do without such plenty?

We are getting to know each other well by now.

The dogs are fine specimens of the race.

The reindeer are dying out rapidly. I fear they cannot stand the climate.

We will all soon have to leave.

Have heard nothing of Sir Henry Morton Stanley; hope he is faring as well as ourselves; still I keep in mind our agreement; he has promised never to forget.

How careless these people always are!

Luckily the dogs never bite anyone, which makes things so difficult.

Our camp is here on the extreme verge of the North Pole. All the stars in Heaven are visible
