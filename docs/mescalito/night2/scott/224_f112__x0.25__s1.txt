Saturday, April 7.--At this time we have been out 31 days. I am writing with both hands, as the fingers of my right hand have become stiff and swollen since I put it through the crack of a sledge, while getting one loose. We have been living on the dogs for some days; there were 40 left, and now they have dwindled to 16. They were only worth half a dog each, but that was all. On the way in I have been working hard; I was strong at the beginning, but am very weak now. The result will show how good a thing the trip was. [end of text]
