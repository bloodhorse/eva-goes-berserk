The pain is indescribable--like having red-hot needles thrust through you, but not really pain, as I never thought of pain before. It is a pain of suffering, not a pain that would cause us to revolt. In some respects it will make us more like our Father and Christ than we could ever be here or in Heaven.

But let this pain last 3,000 years, then God's purpose of choice, based on experience, is guaranteed by the love and character of his Son, the First-Born, which is the very climax of the plan of love. God's choice is based upon his love and on God's perfect love, which is so great a value, is eternal. (See this book.)
