Last man's boots gave way; I gave him mine; he left me behind; I starved; he didn't help. Like a fool, like an ass. And like an ass, like a fool. My other leg has gone too; it fell off--my leg--the one that wasn't missing; my foot, not the other, that fell off first.

It's not true! It's not true, like an ass; my other leg is still there! And I don't care! I don't want to be helped, or fed, or saved. Let me go. I'll stay here with the wind, in the wind, to the wind. [end of text]
