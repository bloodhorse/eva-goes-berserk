the light changes, the colours in the clouds are always different, so too are my memories.

there’s a small bird who sings in the mornings. its voice sounds like it comes from a long way away.

some nights there are fireworks, but they do not seem very important, only for children. the lights look like flowers opening up into the darkness, their petals like tongues, the colour of the flames.
