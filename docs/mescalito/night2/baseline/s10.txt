 i think it might be a bird. it will sit there and tug and peck until i am nothing more than a skeleton, with a long grey tangle of hair and an old yellowing dress.

in my bedroom, i pull apart the feathers. the smell is earthy, like the ground in summer. the sky is the same colour, and i know that they are made for each other.

i cannot see my own face, so i draw a picture instead.

this is me:

and this is you.

i give you my best features: a mouth to say sweet things; a soft, round belly; hands like butterflies. you smile when you see me, and it makes me happy. [end of text]
