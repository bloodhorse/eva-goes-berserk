 and then, if i do not comb it out, it takes on a mind of its own; it moves and shifts like something living.

once, the wind whispered to me: your hair is as thick as the sea is deep.

when it is quiet, when i am alone in this house, my hair moves again. and i hear it, so i say hello, hello, it says my name, and we talk.

this house has many rooms and they all smell different. each room smells of the things it has seen: the things that happened here. this room, the smallest, is my room now.

in another life, i was someone else’s daughter.

in another world, my mother lives inside a tree and watches the stars from its branches.

the rain begins to fall, slowly, and it hits the tin roof like an orchestra
