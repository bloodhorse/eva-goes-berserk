 something wants me to come closer and be quiet.

when i stand above the rooftops in the dark i hear the earth rumble beneath me. it tells me stories about when the ground used to be a forest, before there were cities. and all the animals lived on top of the ground instead of underground in dark places with little light.

one day i will walk to the end of the city where it turns into farmland and hills. there will be cows grazing by the road, and there will be sheep too, and their lambs, and they will look up and nod their heads when i walk by.

the road will get thinner and the houses less frequent. they will have gardens full of flowers and bees will be busy gathering pollen and the sky will be clear blue, dotted with puffy white clouds that look like soft candy floss
