 the wind takes hold of me when it chooses.

i tell it everything, all of my thoughts, all of the things i want to say but do not. i tell it that i miss the sun and the colour in things, that i am afraid of the rain because i don’t want to fall off this roof and drown. i tell the sky how angry i am that the water has come here and taken away all of my memories. it doesn’t answer back. the clouds only look at me. sometimes i think they are crying with me.
