 sometimes i want to let it go. but when i get lonely, or feel small and alone in a room full of people, it’s easy to bury my head deep inside, like an owl’s.

some days, when i’m sitting on the bus and it’s dark and rainy, i wonder where all these people are going, where they come from and how long it took them to get there, before this bus. i wonder why we are always rushing, always running somewhere. why we cannot slow down. i don’t understand.

when the train comes and everyone is staring into space, or at their phones, i imagine where they are going. i think they might be going home. maybe some are going to work, but even if they are, i bet they are thinking about going home at the same time.
