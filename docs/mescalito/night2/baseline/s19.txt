 i find myself trying to get out, clawing through the thorns. my fingers are bloodied by the time i am free. then i remember what i was hiding from. it was not an animal. it was myself.

it’s like this, in my hair, under the moon. i’m a little girl with the sky in my eyes and a light shining through me. when i walk on the clouds i don’t need to fear. there is nothing for me here except the darkness, which is comforting. the stars shine like tiny moons in the night. i watch them rise and fall, they dance, and they are my friends. when they talk they don’t sound human but they sound like a symphony of strings.

i used to run, to see how fast i could go, to see if i could reach the stars
