 and so i tie it in plaits or knots. but then it gets even more tangled and i pull at it too much and my head hurts and my fingers bleed.

so today i put my hair in braids again. they were the first thing i did, when i woke up this morning, before i even left my bed. my mother is always telling me not to put them in plaits or else people will think i am still a little girl. she says i should wear a scarf instead. she does, always, since she has been here. it makes her feel more comfortable.

“they won’t understand us”, she said.

i never thought of it before, but it is true: there are many things we cannot explain. we don’t know how to use our oven. i always burn my fingers, because i can’t
