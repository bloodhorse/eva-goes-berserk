 something wants me to stay there, in my garden.

my mouth has become full of water. i try to drink, but the water flows back out. i am made of water. water does not listen when i talk, so i turn into the storm, into the night, i turn away.

there are ghosts everywhere, i can feel them on the breeze, and sometimes i feel they are looking for me, i know they will find me here, so i walk towards the lights, towards the streetlamp, towards my bed.

i wake up with my face pressed against the pillowcase. my eyes are full of tears. the window is open, but there’s nothing to hear outside. [end of text]
