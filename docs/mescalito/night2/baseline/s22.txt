 when i move my head too fast, or lean to the right or left, it feels as though someone else is with me. holding my hair tight around their wrist and trying to take me with them.

the sky is always moving. there are moments of peace but the clouds shift and move like a tide. when they break it looks like they have been torn apart, the shredded remains of something great, but they are not torn. the wind moves through them like water, like sand.

the stars are not in place, but neither are they out of place. i wonder if they are just somewhere different, waiting for us to find them again.
