 and then i hear a whisper in the trees.

once, i stood at the bottom of the stairs, looking out over the fields, and it took all my willpower to stay put. but now i am stronger than that.

i go to the garden, the path that runs between the apple tree and the beehives and the wildflowers. and i lay down my heart upon the grass. it looks like the sun setting behind the horizon.

sometimes i do not want to write any more. [end of text]
