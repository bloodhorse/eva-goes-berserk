 it pulls my neck back, or turns my head so i can't see the way i want to go. the wind runs its fingers through my hair. i tell it not to touch. my hair is my home, my den, and my shelter. my mother tells me it looks a mess.

the sun shines. it burns my hair to ash and smoke. it lights up all the colours in my eyes until i can see the whole world inside them. but it never stays long, it's too afraid. when it goes away, i'm left with just shadows and whispers in the dark. i put my fingers to my eyelids, close my eyes tight. i wait for it to come back, i count slowly. ten seconds pass, but the darkness still stays.

in my hair is an acorn, and two spiders.
