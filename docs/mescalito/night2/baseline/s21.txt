 like when a fisherman has caught you with a net, but not all of you.

the sun comes through my hair. it warms my face and I see spots on my closed eyelids, dancing like fireflies. when i open them, I see the storm again, it has calmed down. now it just gently pats the roofs with its grey hand and strokes the water.

a bird lands in front of my feet. he tilts his head. his black eyes look right into mine, but I feel nothing. it looks at me like I am just another tree, just another patch of sky.

it says hello in the language of the birds. hello in a sound of two notes and then the bird takes off into the stormy sky and is gone. [end of text]
