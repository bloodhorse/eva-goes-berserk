 the birds peck at it, looking for worms and grubs.

and i let them. i am afraid of their wings, but they are only birds. and my skin is hard to peck through.

the ground beneath me is rough and cold. there are cracks in it. the storm says that if i put my foot through it, it will swallow me whole.

and it might. so i am careful not to touch it. but it does not hurt me.

the clouds pass me by. the moon follows the sun. the stars sing and the wind dances.

i look at them and i dance with them. [end of text]
