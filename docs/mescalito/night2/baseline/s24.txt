 i can’t breathe for all the leaves in my face and i tear them out with my nails and then my hair is short again but soon grows back. it grows to my knees and i cut it again. i put it on my bed.

there is a pond at the bottom of the garden. it is dark, still, deep. i don’t swim because there might be monsters. i have never seen anyone drown. it happens often, though. in the rain, it rains hard on the water, but nothing changes. it’s the same water and i don’t trust it.

when i’m little i hide underneath my bed when the lights go off. i think there’s a monster in my wardrobe but i can’t look. my dad puts his hands underneath my mattress and scares me so much i pee the bed
