 when it is time, it will all be cut away, and i will look so different then. it will make my neck look longer. my ears more exposed. but right now i like the feeling of hiding.

my fingers are prune-like and wrinkled, because i’ve been playing in the sea, and the water has filled my toes, filled the gaps between them. it has come to me and i have touched its surface with my feet. it has wrapped around my ankles, my calves, it has reached my knees, my waist and my hips.

the sky is a heavy weight above me. it hangs low. it is dark with purple, yellow and blue lights, like a tent of lamps. i cannot see where it ends. i imagine i can see a sliver of light between the edge of the clouds and the earth.
