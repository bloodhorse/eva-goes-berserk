 i can’t look down. if i looked down, i might see all my dead things; my mother, my father, the child i lost in the wood, all hanging from my hair, trying to speak with me but I am afraid.

I’m a tree.

i think i might have forgotten how to speak. my voice has grown strange from so little use, and i cannot understand it’s sounds. when I do not hear myself, i must feel around in the dark to find my way out of this thicket.

there is something on the ground beneath me.

I reach for it, and it turns into my fingers.

I bend down to look for the rest of my body and see only more leaves. they are green and gold, the colour of the sun when it hits the trees, but I cannot move. I cannot feel anything
