No one came or took any notice. My last performance. (It's all right now, it's going well.) The curtain falls. The cast go slowly up to their rooms to make ready for supper.

The three sisters go upstairs together to bed and lie awake in their beds listening to the thunder.

At this moment, we see a little peasant woman cross from left to right and pass into her cottage door at the rear of the stage house. As she opens the door, she speaks to them through her bedroom window.
