I had no tea for my feet, so they are pretty stiff; we have a little to-night, but I doubt whether it will last through tomorrow. The tent has been torn down, and my sledge has been wrecked and destroyed. We must all sleep together in one tent--three in one, four in other two. To-night I shall be very cramped in bed. The snow was not hard enough to keep the trail; the wind is still N.W. and we march E. 60° N. All day I am weak in back, knees and ankles; the wind is very cold. [end of text]
