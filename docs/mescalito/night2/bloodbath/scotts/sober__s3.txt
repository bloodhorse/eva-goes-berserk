I feel sure that this will be our last halt; if so, I will write and enclose, or perhaps we shall return. Wind has dropped, and we go on.

We reached camp about noon. Drift again and worse than ever. The drift had taken some of the ice from our walls; it was not a cheerful home. The temperature had risen, wind veered S.S.E. force 7, so we made another march.
