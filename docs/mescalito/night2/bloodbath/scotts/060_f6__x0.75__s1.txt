The cold was intense yesterday. The officers wore every scrap they had, but the frost bit through everything. All day it seemed as if they could see their poor companions staggering over the hill, and dropping one by one. When the sun was setting the sky became very clear. I never saw a sky so beautiful. A great many people do not like the idea of being frozen, but it will be so quiet and sleepy; you will be unconscious before you know it; and it will all be over. Of course, they will not freeze half hard enough to hurt them.
