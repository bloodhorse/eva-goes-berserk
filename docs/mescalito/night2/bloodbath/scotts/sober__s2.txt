It was an awful march yesterday--very short tempers all round; many fell back exhausted; we kept pushing each other forward to keep our spirits up.

To-morrow will be our seventh week without news or touch with civilisation.

Wednesday, April 4.--We marched two hours at midday. The sky looks darkish overhead--probably a storm coming--this will mean no marching to-night, and the same again tomorrow if it comes. Our condition is really getting very bad now.
