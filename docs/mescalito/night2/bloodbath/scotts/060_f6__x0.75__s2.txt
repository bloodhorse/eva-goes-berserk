The poor fellow who carried me yesterday (I wish it was yesterday for my sake) could go no farther than three miles. We left him lying at his last gasp. It must have been very painful to him. What a bother to us, too! And what makes me think we have been cursed with thick-headed men this journey is one bringing up the rear being so stupid. We told him to drop dead behind, as usual; he said "No"--so here he is! I tell you there's no constancy in the thing. I am afraid we are done for--any how, there's nothing else to be done.
