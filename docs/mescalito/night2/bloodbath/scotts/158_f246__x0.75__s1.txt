 wake until the afternoon. The whole story must be told--but as my wife can't speak English we'll do it by gestures.

[Sails set at first to get the audience up. All must go into the open air, except the doctor, who stays at his desk until he is called for.] I think we are on the point of leaving and my wife says she will never come back here again. She must take something, but I'm not sure what. There's the baby. He is crying and calling me. She gives me his bottle. [I take the baby from the wife, then the wife from me. The wife looks at the audience. They look at her. We look at each other.]

We are outside. The wind is N.W. Force 12-16.
