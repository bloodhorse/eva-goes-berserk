Monday, March 19.--To-day we have made no progress towards our destination; this morning was cloudless and beautiful; but alas! at noon clouds rolled up like an avalanche coming from heaven; it was terrible in its magnificence as it swept over us like an icy blast; everything seemed frozen into stone as if by some great power; even our thoughts seemed frozen solid until the clouds parted once more and let the sunshine through once more, we were once again free to think of anything we wanted to.
