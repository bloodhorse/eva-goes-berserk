. Now, after all, the fact is I have been an ass in mixing my powdered medicine; but I know now how to manage it, and if my foot does not recover from the effects of this night, I shall rub some of the medicine on my gun and blow my brains out. This is not good for the road.

The man who is trying to find my snowshoes may as well put his head under his arm and crawl down the hill again.
