The channel was some hundred yards wide, but there appeared no sign of life except some few men who appeared on the surface of the snow. I did not know what to do. I knew that if I stayed here I should die of thirst, for it seemed as though there was no hope of obtaining water for myself and my fellow travellers.

I turned around to see my companions and I found that they were all exhausted and desperate. The sight of them was heart-rending. I knew that I could not help them, but I was determined to find a way to help them out of this situation.

I went back to my ship and started to think about the possibilities that existed for the survival of my friends. I was still pondering the situation when a loud scream echoed across the desert landscape.
