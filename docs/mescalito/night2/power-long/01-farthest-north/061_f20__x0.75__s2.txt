It was therefore necessary that we should rest until such time as it became clear how things would develop further.

At length we came across the ruins of an old building. We did not know exactly what it had been used for, nor whether it still contained anything which might help us in our present situation. However, we decided that we must first secure ourselves against any possible danger.

After having searched the place for a few moments we came across a door. It appeared to be made from wood, but it seemed that the material itself was extremely fragile, and the door was only a little larger than the doorframe.
