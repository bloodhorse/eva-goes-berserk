Going down into this opening, I saw before me, upon the floor of the cave, two large, heavy wooden tables, which, from the appearance, had been constructed many years ago and had stood in the place ever since. Upon the top of one table were three chairs, each placed at its proper place around the table; and upon the other table, there was a chair for myself and one for the Professor, who was seated there.
