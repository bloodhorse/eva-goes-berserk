 could only marvel at the fact that the little child should look so much like me. He was clad in a long, white gown, which covered his body from head to foot, but I could see that it was of the finest white cloth, and that its folds were uncreased and unstained.

As I watched him, I felt myself drawn towards him; I felt myself, as it were, attracted by his presence; I felt that I would willingly give myself into his keeping. But my thoughts were clouded with the knowledge of what my father had suffered at my hands; they were clouded with the recollection of the agony and suffering which had been caused to him by the loss of his son.
