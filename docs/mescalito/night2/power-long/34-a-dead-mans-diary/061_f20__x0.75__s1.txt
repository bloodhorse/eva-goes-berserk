 gazed upon the tiny form of the boy without comprehending its meaning. And yet I felt instinctively that there was something strange about him, and that his presence at the foot of my bed, while his father grieved, was a portent of great import.

My father looked up at my mother, and spoke to her with a voice tremulous with emotion: "Is it possible? Can this be true?" he asked, his voice quivering with emotion, "that you would leave me now, when I need you most?"

The reply which he received from his wife was one of the greatest that he had ever received. In her eyes, I saw the light of love shining forth; and in her heart, I sensed the desire of her to return to me, to be reunited with me once more, after so many years of separation.
