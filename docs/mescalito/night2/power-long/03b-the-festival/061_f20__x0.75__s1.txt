I knew not why this should be so, but as I stood there gazing at the beautiful house with its high windows, its long stone door-step and its narrow street, my mind became filled with wonder as to the reason behind the mystery that lay beneath it all. It was as if some ancient force had taken hold of the land, causing it to tremble under its power and become filled with darkness. There was an aura about the place which could not be ignored, and which could only be explained by the power which held it. It was a force which could not be denied, and which had existed since time immemorial. It was a power which was beyond any man’s ability to comprehend or understand. [end of text]
