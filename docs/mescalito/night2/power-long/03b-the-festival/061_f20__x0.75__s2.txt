There was only one light shining from the upper windows of the house which had once belonged to my father, and even that light seemed dimmed by the heavy darkness which covered everything around. There was something about this house that made me feel that I should never be able to penetrate its secrets. Perhaps it was the fact that there were so many of them, or perhaps it was just the feeling of being alone in a place where nobody could understand me.
