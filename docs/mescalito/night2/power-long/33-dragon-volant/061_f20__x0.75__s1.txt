As soon as I entered it I found that all my senses were still active, though my body was entirely covered with a layer of thick wax, which was permeable to air and light but not to anything else.

It was a most uncomfortable position to be in; for my hands and feet were still attached to the coffin, while my head was encased in a helmet of sorts.

I tried to move, but it was impossible. My hands and feet felt as if they were being held down, and the pressure on my head caused an excruciating pain.

I cried out, but no sound came from my throat.

It was then that I realized that the only way out of this predicament was death itself.
