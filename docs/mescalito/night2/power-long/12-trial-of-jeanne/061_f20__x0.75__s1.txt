 Catherine slept the entire time. It was therefore apparent to me that the White Lady had been unable to reach Catherine, or even to speak to her; for there was no way in which this could have occurred. This is a matter of faith, as I shall endeavor to prove.

It appears to me that Catherine had been influenced by the spirits of darkness. For these reasons I believe that there can be no peace at the end of the lance. [end of text]
