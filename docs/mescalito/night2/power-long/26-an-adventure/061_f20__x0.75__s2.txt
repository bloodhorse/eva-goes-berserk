, suddenly, I found that I was alone with three young women.

One of them was the youngest; her name was Sophie. She looked up at me and smiled; she said nothing, and yet her face conveyed to me what was going on around her; she told me that they had seen me come down from my house, and they knew that we had been living near one another. The other two women were older than Sophie, but their names were still unknown to me.

We walked along together for some time; the women had brought some refreshments with them; the younger ones seemed very interested in me and my wife, and the older ones seemed rather jealous. I noticed that the older women were more reserved than the younger women; they were not accustomed to speaking freely, and I did not want to say anything about their situation or my own
