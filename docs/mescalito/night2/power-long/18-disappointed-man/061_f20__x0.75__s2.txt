"The spirit of M----- will rise above all opposition and shall prevail."

Then we walked back home and sat down together, discussing my case and trying to determine whether there were any signs of life remaining or whether they were merely symptoms of death itself.

We went into a great deal of discussion about these matters until finally, after several hours of discussion, M----- finally concluded that the only way to preserve my sanity was to remain with me at all times. So we set about our plans for the next few days.
