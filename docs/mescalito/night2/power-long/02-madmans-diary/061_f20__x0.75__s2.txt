These men were guarding the hall against any intruder or stranger who wished to enter it. So I passed through them and approached the door.

Suddenly I heard the sound of the trumpet, followed by a cry from the crowd below:

"We will not let you enter here!"

So I stopped and waited for further orders. At last, I was admitted into the council-room where all the policemen stood around. As I approached the throne, I saw a great throne standing there, and upon it a great figure, that of God, sitting on his throne, and surrounded by angels.
