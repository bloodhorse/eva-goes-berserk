 me during the whole time; and then there came a great noise in the barn, and several voices cried out, “Come unto us, and we will give you all that is desired.”

I looked round, and could not see any living person; and therefore went out to find them; and as I walked through the streets, I heard a voice calling me back; which I did; and as soon as I entered the barn again, I was surrounded by the spirits of evil; and there I remained for many days.

And so it went, until I was released on the 16th of May. [end of text]
