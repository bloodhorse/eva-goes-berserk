 me during my stay. I went to see what could be done to remove the evil; and upon my return, the next morning, it began to throw larger stones than those which had been used previously. The stones were so heavy that they could not be thrown further than the edge of the barn; but when I approached the house, they fell like snowflakes on my head and shoulders, and caused me much pain.

After that time, I ceased my endeavours to discover the cause of these troubles; and since that time, until now, nothing has disturbed our peace.

Now we may rest in the assurance that God is the one who rules over all things, and who has set up this order in His kingdom, that all things should be in harmony with Himself, and with each other. [end of text]
