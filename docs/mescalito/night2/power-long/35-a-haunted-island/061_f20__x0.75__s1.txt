As soon as I realised this fact, I decided that we should do well to remain where we were for the rest of the night, rather than risk the dangers which might await us if we left the island at all. If the canoes were returning to the mainland, there could be only one reason for them to return: they wished to meet the enemy, and perhaps even kill them. However, this was not what we wanted. We wanted to meet our friend, the Great One, and that was the reason for which I remained on the island.
