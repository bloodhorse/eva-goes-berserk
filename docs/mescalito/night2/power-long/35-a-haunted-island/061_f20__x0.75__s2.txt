It appeared to me that the Indians were going to make their last attempt at landing, and that they meant to make sure of it this time. They had made several attempts at landing already, but now they were determined to succeed.

The steerer was evidently not one to give up easily. He had been fighting the waves for some time now, and he seemed determined to continue his efforts until he succeeded.

I watched from my position behind the rock, as the men came ashore. The Indians landed without difficulty, and immediately began to make their way towards the shore. [end of text]
