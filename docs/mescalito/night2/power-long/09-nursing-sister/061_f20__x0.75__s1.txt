The Frenchman I met yesterday had been severely wounded by shelling at his home town. He has now returned to his home. There was a young French woman also on the train, whom I did not recognise until he told me she had been his wife since their marriage ten years ago.

A letter has come through from my sister in London saying that she has found my father’s grave, which is located in a cemetery at St Albans in North London, close to where we had lived before. This news was received with great relief, as I had not seen or heard from him in over a year. It would be nice to see him again, if he ever decided to come back.
