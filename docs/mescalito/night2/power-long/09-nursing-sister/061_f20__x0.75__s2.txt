The Frenchman in charge of the British hospital was called on by us, and we explained what we needed him to do for us. We then took them out to the village square where they set up camp in tents erected by their own people.

As I stood watching them set up their tent, I was approached by a young officer who wanted me to speak to his mother. His mother had just given birth to twins, but she was too weak to take care of herself and her newborn babies.

I agreed to speak to her, and went over to the house that was next door to where the young officer was setting up his camp. When I got there, I found a very lovely woman lying on a bed, looking out the window with tears streaming down her cheeks.
