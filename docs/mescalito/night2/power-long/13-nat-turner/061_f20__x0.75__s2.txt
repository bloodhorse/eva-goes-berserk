But I believed otherwise; for I knew that God did not hate His children; but He loved them so much that He sent His angels to comfort them in their tribulations and sufferings--and this is the testimony that I bear to all men who read these words; for it is not in vain that we speak unto them concerning the mysteries of Heaven and earth; for therein we shall find a place of rest from our labors, and a place where we can go when we are weary of life.
