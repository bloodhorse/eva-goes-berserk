The ship was at anchor near the coast of Cuba, where a few natives lived who were in league with us. The master of the ship, Mr. Christian, was a native of Jamaica, and was employed by the Government to assist them in their designs upon the island of Cuba. He was also in communication with the leaders of the revolutionary movement, which had been organized for many years, and was now in its infancy. He had been instrumental in obtaining a large sum of money for the Government, and had also obtained a large number of ships, which he had used in his endeavors to further the cause of liberty in Cuba.
