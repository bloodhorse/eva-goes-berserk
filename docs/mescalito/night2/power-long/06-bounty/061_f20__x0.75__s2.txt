They brought me into the great cabin, and seated me on a chair in front of them: I then heard them declare that they intended to make themselves masters of the ship, and that all who refused to obey them should be put to death without mercy: This was accompanied by the most fearful oaths and curses, which, however, I could not understand. They told me that if I would swear allegiance to them, they would spare my life; but that if I refused, they would put me to death immediately, without mercy.
