I turned my head away from the crystal bottle, and my gaze fell upon a mirror hanging on the wall opposite me. I stood before it and gazed at it intently; but all I saw was my own reflection; the mirror revealed nothing else. Then suddenly, as I stared, I noticed that there were two reflections, one of myself, and one of somebody else! It was the face of a man, an old man, with a long white beard, and a bald head, with eyes which seemed to glow with a strange, mysterious fire.
