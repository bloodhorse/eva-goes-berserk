The next thing I knew was that there was a roar of sound, and I saw the floe break into many pieces. As I scrambled to get away from the collapsing ice, I saw that the crack had widened further. I heard shouts coming from below me, but they were muffled by the roar of the ice collapsing. The next moment, I was lifted off my feet and thrown back toward the ship.

As I regained my senses, I felt the cold air on my face and realized that I had been tossed out into the freezing ocean. I began to struggle toward the surface, and as I broke through, I heard the sound of an engine. As I swam toward it, I could hear the screams of the men in the water. [end of text]
