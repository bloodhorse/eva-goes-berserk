“Don as you will,” I heard someone say from within the dome, but the voice was cut short by another loud crack.

I turned to see where this second noise came from, and saw a large section of the ice floe moving away from the rest. There was a man standing at the edge, holding onto the floe as if it would collapse beneath him. As the ice moved further away, the man began to slip over the edge.
