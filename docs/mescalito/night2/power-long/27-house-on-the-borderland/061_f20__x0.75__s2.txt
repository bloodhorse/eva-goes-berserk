 could hear Pepper growl, low in his throat; and he seemed to be aware of the strange change that was taking place all about him; and that he felt something wrong.

Suddenly, the flames began to leap up; higher and higher, until they touched the ceiling. They cast strange shadows upon the walls; shadows that writhed and twisted in their efforts to escape from the flame; and it seemed as though there were living entities within them; creatures that wished nothing more than to escape from their bonds of darkness and return to the light which they craved above all else.

Down upon the floor, I heard Pepper cry out, a single word that sounded like a name; a name that I had never heard before, nor would ever hear again, unless I wished it. The name was: "Alaxandria."
