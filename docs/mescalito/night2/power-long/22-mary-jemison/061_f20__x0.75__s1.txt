I had no desire to see such things as scalps, but when I saw them, and understood the manner in which they were taken and kept, I was convinced that this tribe of Indians were not really savages, but merely playing the part of savages in order to gain some advantage. However, I could not help feeling a certain amount of sympathy for them, because of the fact that they were not savages.
