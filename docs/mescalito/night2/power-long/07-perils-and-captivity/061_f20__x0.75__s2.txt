The sight of this hill gave me fresh courage; and I mounted my ass once more, and set off towards it. However upon arriving there, I found myself surrounded by the whole tribe of Arabs. These wild people had apparently no other object than that of securing themselves against any possible danger which might arise; and it seemed as though their only thought was to preserve themselves from all harm.
