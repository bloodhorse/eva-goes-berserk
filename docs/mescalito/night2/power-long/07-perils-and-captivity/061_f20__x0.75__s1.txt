After some time spent in vain endeavours to persuade him that I was innocent of any crime, and that he could not injure me by his conduct, the Moor at length relented, and agreed to leave me unharmed if I would promise to return with him to his country.

### The desert of Arabia

I consented, but with great reluctance, and the Moor, having seized my arm, led me into the heart of the Desert. He seemed to be very pleased with my appearance, and to take a great interest in the manner in which I was attired. He informed me that he was an officer of the King's Guard, and that he was sent on a special mission to apprehend a certain person, whom he refused to name to me.
