I saw at once that I was not the only one who was suffering under the weight of this dreadful calamity. All around me, there was nothing but death and destruction. It was as though a mighty hand had swept over the earth, leaving naught but desolation in its wake. But I did as my duty demanded. I rose from the floor where I had been lying, and went to the side of the ship, where I could see the sea.
