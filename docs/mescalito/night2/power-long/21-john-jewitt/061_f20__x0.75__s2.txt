 the natives had taken possession of the ship. They had apparently done so without any violence; and indeed, as I had seen them, there could be no doubt that they had done so. I therefore determined to proceed to the nearest place of safety. After ascertaining that the natives were still upon the deck of the vessel, I proceeded towards the nearest cabin. There I found a large number of men and women, who seemed to be engaged in the pursuit of pleasure.

The natives had evidently made themselves comfortable in this manner, and I determined to continue my journey towards them, without interruption. However, as I proceeded on my way, I could not help observing that the natives seemed to be rather surprised at the sight of a foreigner among them. [end of text]
