## The Madness of the Mad Man

I sat at my desk in my cabin, which had been provided by the ship's captain. The captain himself stood beside me, looking out upon the vast sea before us. He said nothing for several minutes. Then he turned to me and said, "Sir, you have brought great trouble upon us. Your actions have caused this disaster." He pointed towards the ocean and said, "We are sinking. You must save us. You alone have the power to do so."
