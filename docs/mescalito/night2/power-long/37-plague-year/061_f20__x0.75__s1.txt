They spoke to him for a good space, and seemed much disturbed by the appearance of him; and, after some time, I observed the burier, who had been looking at the cart, turned to me, and said, “What do you want? Do you want to see a man die, or do you wish to help us to carry out the remains of this unfortunate being?”

“I do not know,” I replied; “but if it be the former, then I shall do all that is in my power to assist you.”

“I understand you,” the burier said, turning to the man that had just spoken. “You have seen me before, and now you must understand that you are not only to be a spectator, but also a participant.”

“I understand,” the other replied.
