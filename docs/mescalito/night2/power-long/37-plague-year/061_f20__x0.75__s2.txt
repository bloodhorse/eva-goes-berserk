I am but a poor man, who have been taken prisoner by these cruel people, and they will not let me go,” and he held up his hands to heaven, and began to weep; and the buriers began to pity him, and they came nearer to him, and looked upon him with pitying eyes. And the man in the cloak looked upon them also with a kind of pitying look, and began to speak in a very low voice to them, and the buriers were greatly affected, and stood gazing upon him as if he was something very precious and important.
