I had been in that room for several months when one day the Lord called me to him, and said to me that he was going to send his angel to comfort me, and that I should be able to stand up and receive him into my heart. This was indeed wonderful to me, for I had been unable to receive him thus far. He spoke to me and said: “You will soon receive your reward if you will obey my commands.” I listened intently to these words, and began to believe that what he said was true.

Then, after receiving my reward, I became sick and tired, and I did not wish to live any longer in that state of mind. I believed that the Lord was telling me the truth when he spoke those words. [end of text]
