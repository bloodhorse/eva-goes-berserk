At this Meeting, there were two Persons of high Degree, who were known by their Names; and who were supposed to be of high Rank among the Men.

These Two Persons were called the Lord High Admiral and the Lord High Chancellor: They were supposed to be of the highest Degree of Wisdom and Learning among the People of this Country; and their Office was to instruct and teach the People concerning their Religion.

These Two Persons were also known by their Names, and by the People of this Country, to be the most learned and wise of all the Men of the Country.
