After a few Minutes had elapsed, the Captain ordered that we should return to our respective Dutches, and that the Master of the Ship should be appointed as their Commander.

This Meeting was of great Benefit to us, and also to those who had come from other Parts of the World, for they had brought with them many of the same Principles, as ourselves; and these were very much appreciated by all.

When I had finished my address, I found that the Master of the Ship, was the same as I was; and I was greatly pleased with him, as he was one of the best of Men that could be found.
