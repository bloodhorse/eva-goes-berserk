I determined at once to make a trial of our new strength, and commenced the journey back to Winter River, where we should find plenty of game.

I soon reached the vicinity of Winter River, and began the hunt for game. The snow lay in great heaps, and made it almost impossible to proceed. However, we managed to make some progress, and came upon a large herd of reindeer, which was soon put to flight by the presence of our party. After the chase was over, we returned to the vicinity of Winter River, where we found that the reindeer had left a considerable amount of flesh behind them.
