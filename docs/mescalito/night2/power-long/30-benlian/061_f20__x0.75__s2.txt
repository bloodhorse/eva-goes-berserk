The answer came later.

It came as I looked at myself in the mirror. It came as I felt the pain of being alive.

But now, I knew that I could not go back to who I once was--that was not possible.

So now, I could only hope for the future ... for the hope of life--the hope of death.

The hope of love ... and of love itself.

The hope of eternity ...

I stood there on my balcony, looking out over the city. It was dark outside--dark like a tomb--dark like the night.

And as I looked, I could see the lights of the city shining out in the distance. The lights were so bright and beautiful that I could hardly take them all in.
