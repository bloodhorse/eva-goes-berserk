 I knew him as such, though I could never discern him by any visible sign; he was invisible, and I had no means of discovering him, except by my own reasoning power.

I have ever since been haunted by the fear of my second self; but it has been more than twenty years since I last saw him. The memory of him is ever before me, and the sight of his countenance haunts my imagination; but I am thankful that my imagination is strong enough to enable me to banish his image from my thoughts.

It may appear strange that a person should imagine himself to be two persons; but I do not consider this as impossible. The fact that I can imagine myself to be two persons, one of whom is a man and the other is a woman, has not only been confirmed by experience, but also by observation
