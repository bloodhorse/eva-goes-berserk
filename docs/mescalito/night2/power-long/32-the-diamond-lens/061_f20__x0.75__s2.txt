I tried to think of what I should do, but no words came into my mind. I stood there, trembling in my boots, with the dreadful realization that all I held dear would soon pass away from me forever. I knew not how or why it would happen; I knew only that it must be done; that there was no other way than by destroying everything I loved and valued in life.

The thought came to me as an irresistible impulse--that which I desired above all else was to be annihilated. I had not asked for such a thing, yet I could not resist the power within me, which sought only to bring me happiness in whatever manner it saw fit. [end of text]
