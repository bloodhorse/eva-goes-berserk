 dying without my loved ones being there.

After several minutes of trying, I succeeded in binding myself together again and I looked around me at the other wounded men who had been left behind when we were captured by the Germans. I saw a German officer standing over them with a rifle pointed at each of their heads as if he were about to execute them right then and there. He turned his head towards me and smiled as if he knew exactly what I was thinking. "Ah, yes," he said, "I see that you are also wondering how I could have come up with such a plan so quickly and so effectively. You should know that I have always been one step ahead of my enemies." He turned back to look at me again, his eyes searching mine for any sign of weakness or fear.
