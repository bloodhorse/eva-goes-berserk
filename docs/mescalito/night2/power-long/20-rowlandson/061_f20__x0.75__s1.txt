There were many others who spake to me to do this or that for them, and I always did as they requested, as I was ever mindful of my master's wishes. There were those who spake ill of my master, and I spoke in defence of him; for I knew that he was a good man, and that he loved me dearly.

I had many friends among the squaws, and I spent much time with them; for I found them to be most amiable, and I felt a great love for them.
