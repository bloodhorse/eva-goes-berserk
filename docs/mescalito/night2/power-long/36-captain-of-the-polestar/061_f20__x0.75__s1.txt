 something moving in front of us. On approaching nearer, we saw a large herd of walrus lying upon their sides and basking in the sun. At first they did not seem aware of our presence; but when we were within a hundred paces, several of them rose up and bellowed in a threatening manner. They were of enormous size and strength, being about twenty feet in length and having huge tusks projecting from either side of their head.

I could scarcely believe my eyes as I beheld this extraordinary sight; it was indeed an experience which would be difficult to forget. [end of text]
