 a boat approaching from the west. I turned my spyglass upon it and could clearly distinguish the outlines of a small vessel of some sort; but I was not able to identify her nationality, for she did not bear the flag of any country.

She approached nearer and nearer, until at length she came within hailing distance. The captain shouted out to us in English, asking us what we were doing upon the ice field.

"We are searching for the survivors of an expedition which disappeared many months ago," said I. "We have been exploring these ice fields for nearly six months now."

"The survivors of an expedition!" exclaimed the captain. "You must be mistaken. There is no such thing as an expedition of explorers. All who venture upon the ice fields perish!"

"We are not the first to make such an exploration," replied I.
