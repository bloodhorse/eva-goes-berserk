What could I do? Nothing but remain still, and watch and wait. It is hard to be thus passive, but it is often the wisest course, especially when we have no better counsel to give.

Ah, my dear, it was a cruel hour to spend, this hour of waiting; and yet it was well that we waited, for the moment of action soon came.
