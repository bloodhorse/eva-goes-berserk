The old man was evidently not in a state of mind to listen to my advice. He seemed so calm and so serene; that nothing could make him understand that he was being betrayed; and even if he did understand it, he could not make any impression upon his followers. They were determined to carry out their purpose of killing him and destroying him; and they were willing to sacrifice themselves in order to accomplish this purpose.

It was only a few minutes since we had arrived here; but it seemed like hours; and as I gazed at the group, I could see how much they were determined upon their course; and I could feel how much they would suffer if they were unsuccessful; but what was I to do?
