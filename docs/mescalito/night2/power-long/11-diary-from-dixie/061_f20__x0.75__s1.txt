But now, at half past five, the sky had begun to lighten, and in a moment we could see the great ship. Her masts were bending beneath the weight of her guns, and her decks were filled with men, waiting to receive her fire.

The scene was indescribable. The smoke was rolling across the bay, and the noise was deafening. The cannons roared out one after another, until it seemed as though the whole world were at war. The ship was slowly coming into view, and I could see the white flags flying above her.

My heart was filled with joy at this sight, for I knew that this was a sign from God, that he was granting peace unto his people.
