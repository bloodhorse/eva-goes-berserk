 endeavoured to converse with them, they refused even to speak to me. One of their number was particularly anxious to talk with me; he was of an amiable disposition, and seemed to be fond of all human kind, even in spite of his own selfish motives. He said that he had been sent hither on some mission, and was in danger of being expelled from the ship.

He told me that he was not to blame for the condition in which the ship lay, but he had done his duty, and he believed that it would be the best thing for him to continue in the service of the company. [end of text]
