October, 1799

The tyranny of the human face has become more than a mere phenomenon in my life. I find it a constant companion, and a most troublesome one. It is indeed an affliction to have to face this tyrant so constantly, and yet, there is no denying that it has its benefits as well.

July, 1830

I find that the tyranny of the human face is ever present in my life. I cannot escape it, nor do I wish to. I find that it is a most useful thing to have in one’s life, for it enables us to understand and appreciate the many facets of humanity.
