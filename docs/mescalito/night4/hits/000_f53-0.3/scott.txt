We have to keep on marching. On the march yesterday the ice seemed firm enough. This morning there was only wind, and now, although we have eaten, we must make camp.

--David Livingstone (Dr.), explorer, lost

Sunday, March 19.--Turned around, now, let's eat. We've got a few supplies. If you don't have much to eat, we are now in trouble. It is too late: our food has gone. What's your idea, mate? Let'ts turn back, or at you? In despair, I'm going to quit.

Sunday, March 20.--
