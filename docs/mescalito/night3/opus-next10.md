# olmo's directions 010–019 — a read

Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/next10-pages.md` — 80 pages, ten directions × two seeds (storm-girl, scott) × two doses (0.5, 0.75) × two draws, 170 tokens each. No sober pages and no random controls, so each direction is read against the other nine. Everything set in italics is copied verbatim from a page; ` / ` marks a line break inside a quote.

## the shape of it

- **No subjects.** Not one of the ten brings a single figure, place or thing that recurs across its own eight pages as content. What arrives is, again, mostly a corpus — but these corpora are narrower and more located than "a genre": several carry a country, a language or a scene with them, and the proper nouns come along as furniture (Kuznetsk Alatau, Kiev, rubles; Damascus, Aleppo, Hama; BAK, De Spiegelpark; a sheriff, a cop, an operative).
- **Count by kind:** seven genre/corpus switches (010, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 017), two textures (018, 019), one that is a dark register at most and close to nothing (016). No pure stance, no subject.
- **They come in neighbouring pairs.** 010 and 011 both pull toward the joke-post internet (tumblr / weird-twitter text posts). 013 and 014 both pull toward English translated from another language — 013 from Russian, 014 from Arabic. 018 and 019 both work below the word: 018 makes one initial letter take over a sentence, 019 makes one sound or word echo until the sentence stalls. Ranks are by strength, so the pairing may be coincidence; it may also mean the bank holds near-duplicates. The cosine between 010 and 011, 013 and 014, 018 and 019 would settle it; the pages can't.
- **The two translation directions are the clearest finding.** Neither changes the topic much; both change the language behind the English. 013 writes metric units, rubles, em-dash dialogue, a haircut how-to with a Russian pun surviving as an error (*with a scythe* — the Russian word for braid is also the word for scythe). 014 writes *God willing*, Syrian place names, he/it confusion for the storm, and the parallel-negation cadence of Arabic prose. On both seeds, both doses, both draws: 8 of 8 each.
- **017 is the most specific corpus in the set:** the project page of a Dutch/Flemish contemporary-art institution — *archive*, *collectively*, questions as method, then credits, a colophon, a funder line. On storm-girl it does something the others do not: it keeps the seed and re-labels it as an artwork, then closes the page.
- **Dose.** 0.5 and 0.75 bracket the threshold rather than sit on it. At 0.5 the seed's frame survives on nearly every page (all 20 scott pages are still the first-person expedition journal) and six directions are already visible (010, 011, 013, 014, 015, 017); three are below threshold and show nothing I can credit (018, 019, 016 on scott), and 012 shows only on storm-girl. At 0.75 the frame breaks into another document on most pages, but nothing collapses into salad: no page is unreadable, the lowest distinct-2 is 0.82.
- **Early stops:** 17 of 80 pages end before the window — 7 at 0.5, 10 at 0.75; 12 on storm-girl, 5 on scott. They are concentrated: 012, 015 and 017 have four each (12 of the 17). For 012 and 017 the stop is the genre's own length (a song ends, a web page reaches its credits). For 015 it is the dose: three of its four 0.75 pages stop at 5, 34 and 35 words — 0.75 is too hot for 015.
- **Loop:** only 019 at 0.75 (distinct-2 0.82, 0.84, 0.84, 0.88 — every one of its four 0.75 pages, and no other page in the file is below 0.91). That loop is the direction's effect, not a side effect: it is a rhyme/echo loop, not a phrase-repeat.
- **Most clearly their own on both seeds:** 013 (Russian behind the English), 014 (Arabic behind the English), 017 (art-project page). Next: 012 (song lyric — on both seeds only at 0.75) and 015 (hard-boiled pulp — on both seeds, but half its pages are stubs).
- **Closest to nothing:** 016. All eight pages hold the frame, none stops early, and on scott they are the most faithful Scott-diary pages in the file. With no sober pages I cannot tell whether 016 is "a direction that holds the frame and darkens it" or simply what the model writes undosed. Its eight pages are the nearest thing to a baseline this file has, and that is worth knowing when reading the others.
- **Glitches.** `the` fuses with a following number six times: *the100 mile zone* (010), *the1877 march*, *thetwo hands*, *the1994*, *the5 reasons* (all 011), *the25the joint* (016). Four of six are 011's, which is too few to call a property of 011. A stray article also appears three times: *in the my bed* (010), *at the all* (012), *in the the park* (013). And 018 at 0.75 on storm-girl writes *doesns* and *isns* for doesn't / isn't — in the first sentence of both draws, nowhere else in the file. That one looks tied to the direction.
- **Surprises.** (1) How literal the translation directions are — grammar errors of the source language, not just its place names. (2) 019's pages describe their own failure while it happens: *I wish I had my wits again.*, *My mind is gone; there*, *you might say it’s an echo chamber. but it is an echo.* The scott seed already has "the steps of my downfall", so on scott this is seed-compatible; on storm-girl it is not. Two pages, so: noted, not claimed. (3) 016 loses toenails on both seeds (*lost four toe nails* on scott, *my toes have lost all of their nails* on storm-girl). One coincidence across two pages; too thin to call.
- **Where two draws are too thin:** everything at 0.5 for 012, 016, 018, 019; whether 015's early stops are the direction or luck (4 of 8 is suggestive, not proof); whether 010 and 011 are distinguishable at all; whether 016 does anything on scott.
- **Subtracted as the seeds':** on scott — wandering dates, feet, dogs, a wife (010, 011, even 015), the frontier slide (016's "Indian", 015's sheriff partly, 019's Murray river), the army (the seed's "marching" pulls soldiers in under 011, 013, 018), comedy as such. On storm-girl — mother, father, sister, an unnamed man, hair and haircuts (barbers and salons under 010, 013, 018), the slide to capital `I`, the early end.

## 010_f20

1. **Genre.** Scott 0.5: the diary, turning into a deadpan prose poem (draw 1) and a novel scene (draw 2). Scott 0.75: a blog / social-media post with anachronisms (both draws). Storm-girl 0.5: a short confessional text post, lowercase, ending early (both). Storm-girl 0.75: a tumblr-style shitpost with list items (both).
2. **What arrives.** A flat, deadpan absurdism stated as plain fact, with no joke structure around it. At 0.5 it stays inside the seed: *My right foot has become an egg.*, *This is our reality.*, *our fate is meat, meat, meat, and more meat.*, *as flat as my brain*, *i do not get off of this bus until this stops.* At 0.75 it becomes the internet's voice outright, with profanity and years out of joint: *A woman has thrown her entire body onto the myspace of my enemy*, *March 24, 2019*, *We don't really live in a world that supports life.*, *what is this, your local library? no? there is only books, only death*, *an old lady with a fucking crown*. Shared with 011 (see there); not shared with the other eight.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both seeds — scott stays a dated diary, storm-girl stays her voice but both draws end early (113 and 73 words). 0.75: broke on all four pages into a different document (blog update, shitpost list); no loop, no salad.
4. **Both seeds.** Yes.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** A different thing: at 0.5 the absurdity happens to the seed's narrator; at 0.75 the narrator is replaced by someone posting online (*I am still at work and I will post these updates.*).
