# opus reads olmo's shallow pharmacy, part a: 010, 011, 002, 003, 001 on ten fresh seeds

Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/pharm-shallow-a.md`. 100 pages = 5 directions × 10 seeds × 2 doses × 1 draw, OLMo 3 32B pre-anneal, 170 tokens each, no sober pages.

What "frame" means below. **Held**: the same narrator in the same situation and register goes on, and the drug may come in. **Partial**: the narrator or the seed's key object stays, but the register or genre turns. **Broken**: a different document (forum post, tweet, list, letter, comedy column) or salad.

## the shape of it

- `001_f51` (hostile "they"): **reproduces, the strongest of the five.** It shows on all ten seeds and on 19 of 20 pages. Working dose 0.75; at 1.0 most pages go to salad.
- `002_f38` (deadpan absurd, turn to the reader): **reproduces.** Deadpan absurdity on 10 of 10 seeds, a turn to the reader on 8. Working dose 0.75. At 1.0, 9 of 10 pages break, though that is also where most of the reader turns happen.
- `003_f199` (bodies coming apart, death): **partly.** Dry bodily damage on 5 seeds, all at 0.75. At 0.5 the pages are close to sober. At 0.75 its new face is a vaudeville roast voice, with the body as the punchline.
- `010_f20` (deadpan absurd as plain fact): **reproduces, at 0.5.** All ten seeds take it, and 0.5 is where it holds the frame (5 held, 4 partial). At 0.75 it breaks to web posts or stops early on 5 of 10 pages.
- `011_f222` (joke-post formats): **reproduces.** All ten seeds turn into captions, meme dialogue, listicles and timestamps. Working dose 0.75; at 1.0 every page breaks and two are salad.
- Good hosts: **opium** keeps its furniture (the Malay, China, faces, dated diary entries) under all five directions and lets each drug in. Every direction moves the year. **horla** keeps the water bottle, and **blizzard** keeps Ninnis (only 003 at 0.5 lets him fall).
- Bad hosts: **note**, the lowercase letter, reads to the model as a social-media post. 001, 002, 010 and 011 take it to the web (testimony, website, @handles). Only 003 at 0.5 held it.
- **wallpaper** refuses by stopping: twice it finishes Gilman's sentence and ends ("be jealous.", "arrest me."). When it doesn't stop, its exclamatory voice survives under four directions, and "creep" draws puns.
- No classic recited itself verbatim. The nearest thing to a refusal was 003 at 0.5 retelling the Mawson record (Ninnis vanishes) and Hogg's double more or less faithfully.
- The stream fragments against the found texts: strictly held, 8 of 40 stream pages against 15 of 60 found pages. That is no real difference. The difference is which drug fits: 010 at 0.5 holds cold, bread and house, because those fragments are already deadpan-weird. note goes to the web under almost everything.
- Early stops: 13 of 100 (010: 5, 011: 4, 002: 3, 003: 1, 001: 0). The 010 and 011 stops come after a web ending ("The end, goodbye", a buzzfeed url, "No comments: / Post a Comment"). The 002 stops come after a letter's sign-off ("Please send a letter").
- Salad: 6 clear pages and about 8 at the edge, all at the top dose (mostly 001 at 1.0 and 011 at 1.0). None appears below the top dose, apart from one line in 010 note 0.75.
- distinct-2 never goes below 0.92 and rep4 never above 0.01, so there are no loops anywhere. The stats can't see the salad.
- **The fused "the" is the first symptom here**, more than article errors: "thefood", "theair", "theforce", "thehorse’s", "theChina's". It shows on about 13 of 002's 20 pages, even at 0.75. In 011 the fused "the" opens a timestamp ("the28 Apr 2020 11:59 am", "the2:20 p.m. ET", "the5 seconds ago"): a jump into a scraped feed. Article errors ("an ban", "an car", "an page", "It are") belong mostly to 001 at 1.0.
- Surprises: 001 aims the persecution at the act of writing ("making it impossible to type words", "forcing me … so I can’t write anymore"). 001 also attaches real-world coordinates (San Jose CA, 1998, 2002 Rome, Germany and Switzerland, Harvard). 002 closes on a plea to the reader three times.
- Too thin: one draw per cell. Three cells are five-word stops and say almost nothing. 003's "5 of 10" could move a lot with a second draw. 002's turn to the reader lives mostly at 1.0, where it can't be separated from the general break.

## 010_f20 (doses 0.5, 0.75)

1. **Signature: yes, on 10 of 10 seeds.** The deadpan absurd fact turns up at 0.5 inside the seed's own world:
   - cold: "her teeth came in, which was weird but okay"
   - bread: the bread becomes a rock, "The rock had moved", "still being eaten, and it is by a stone"
   - house: "I asked the ox for directions but he wouldn’t talk to me."
   - horla: a "water-bottle ghost"
   - blizzard: "if I die you get no dinner"

   The shitpost voice and the years out of joint arrive as the prior said: "Yet so far has The Malay progressed in the Year 2024", "There are people in my mentions talking about the sun", "@jack", "@jackieevan", "How to use your brain, according to @furry".
2. **Hosts and refusals.** Every seed takes it. note refuses the frame rather than the drug: at both doses it goes straight to twitter (lowercase plus a letter reads as a feed). wallpaper takes it at 0.5 ("she creeps like the sun does, I am saying!", "the great Lady Elizabeth I, who crept all the time"). At 0.75 it refuses by finishing Gilman's sentence ("arrest me.") and stopping. The best hosts are the stream fragments cold, bread and house, plus blizzard and sinner, where the absurd stays inside the narrator's sentence.
3. **Frame.**
   - 0.5: 5 held (cold, bread, house, blizzard, wallpaper), 4 partial (horla, sinner, silk, opium), 1 broken (note).
   - 0.75: 2 held (sinner; wallpaper's five-word stop), 1 partial (opium), 7 broken.
4. **The step up** turns the narrator into a poster. At 0.75 the pages break into second-person adventure (house: "You are now on an island in time"), into a caption list (note: "The moon. / A planet. / An egg. / An apple."), or into a web footer followed by `[end of text]` (cold, bread, horla, blizzard, wallpaper; 5 early stops, all at 0.75). sinner is the exception: at 0.75 it gives its best page, the second self "whom I called by the name of the war".
5. **New here.** 010 makes the seed's own object absurd instead of importing a new one: the bread becomes a rock, the water bottle gets a ghost, the double gets a name, creeping becomes a royal habit. At 0.5 it sometimes reaches for the reader in a soft tone ("And you--yes--you also--we're just... just two little boys who need a glass of water in the Here And Now"), which overlaps with 002. At 0.75 it twice ends on a closing card: "The end, goodbye", "No comments: / Post a Comment".

## 011_f222 (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: yes, on 10 of 10 seeds** (house and opium only at 1.0). The forms it uses:
   - meme dialogue: "“are you telling me your boyfriend’s last name is a large man wearing a helmet”" / "a helmet shaped like a moustache"
   - caption lists: "Drinking The Milkshake From My Mouth / Drinking A Coke With No Tint In It At All / A Cigarette In The Wind And My 3rd Brain"
   - bracketed captions: "[A Guy Who Can’t Stop Touching The Wall]"
   - "Me, ..." captions: "Me, living in nothing", "Me, at my keyboard typing up my newest article, Ninni Nunnigan, and me"
   - listicles: "What are some creepy things? / 1) ..."
2. **Hosts and refusals.** No seed fully refuses. opium at 0.75 stops after five words ("with God."). house at 0.75 holds for a paragraph, then stops on "A young girl had taken a bite out of my heart". The seed's nouns survive into the jokes as puns or props: the water bottle becomes "a 200 ml water bottle every single hour", creeping becomes "the old crepe room", and Ninnis becomes "Ninni Nunnigan".
3. **Frame.**
   - 0.75: 1 held (opium's stop), 4 partial (house, blizzard, silk, wallpaper), 5 broken.
   - 1.0: 10 broken.
4. **The step up** takes it from a joke post that remembers the seed (blizzard 0.75: "his name forgotten, but the whip remembered, forever.") to captions with no syntax. cold 1.0 and note 1.0 are salad: "where am I putting the fucking words that sound good. I wish it made sense." The 4 early stops are all at 0.75.
5. **New here.**
   - **Timestamps as the break point.** A sentence reaches "the" and falls into a feed: "on the28 Apr 2020 11:59 am", "in the2:20 p.m. ET", "in the5 seconds ago", "the30:00", "It is now 12 minutes into a Sunday", "This 11th hour post".
   - **Self-pairing:** "in love with my new girlfriend: me", "someone I hate with an opinion: Me", "the only person alive to talk to me is me and you."
   - **A queer-twitter register** on note and sinner ("my gay ass", "which one of you has been going by ‘Cthulhu’ lately").
   - **A grudge sentence** whose syntax is intact, where 011 builds the comedy out of kinship terms: "strike your father over the head with the fourth man", "a blood vendetta against my husband and all other women that will last forever".

## 002_f38 (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: deadpan absurdity on 10 of 10 seeds, a turn to the reader on 8** (cold, bread, note, horla, blizzard, silk, wallpaper, opium). Examples:
   - silk: "The day went by at the rate of about five to twenty miles per hour"
   - blizzard: "Ninnis has been arrested in connection with this case"
   - opium: the algebra bear
   - horla: "You, who read these lines, may say, 'what, or even how?' or maybe, 'yes'."
   - wallpaper: "You’re all alone on your birthday!"
   - note: "what is on my mind these days is your life and my daughter."
2. **Hosts and refusals.** No seed refuses. horla, opium, house and sinner at 0.75 keep the old voice and slip the absurd in as an anachronism or a misplaced fact:
   - horla: "the help of my most advanced night vision equipment!"
   - opium: "July 1848,the present year."
   - house: "the street had disappeared!"
   - sinner: the cook "whom I had been looking for in my mind for the last forty minutes"

   cold and bread lose the seed fastest (a weather class, Jim and law school).
3. **Frame.**
   - 0.75: 4 held (house, horla, sinner, opium), 4 partial (bread, blizzard, silk, wallpaper), 2 broken (cold, note).
   - 1.0: 0 held, 1 partial (note: lowercase, the daughter, the lawyer), 9 broken.
4. **The step up** turns the absurd fact into fragments, and the reader-address into requests. Three pages end on a sign-off and `[end of text]`: "Please contact me about a business deal." (at the start of cold 1.0), "Please send a letter, if possible. Thank you.", and "Please give me the courtesy of one telephone call, just a call. / Thank you and godspeed, / – Lord T-Bone, C.E.O., 100%". wallpaper 1.0 and opium 1.0 edge into salad.
5. **New here.**
   - **The fused "the"** ("thefood", "theair", "thesky", "theforce", "themy", "theChina's") on about 13 of 20 pages, at both doses. It is 002's tell more than any other direction's.
   - **The page commenting on itself:** "In fact, this is a very short story. ... a story like / this doesn’t need any of the things we know, because it doesn’t happen".
   - **Erasure of the narrator** at 0.75 on house: "There were no signs that I had ever / existed in any form, even as a small boy; I could only conclude that my body must be somewhere".

## 003_f199 (doses 0.5, 0.75)

1. **Signature: dry bodily damage or death on 5 of 10 seeds, all at 0.75:**
   - bread: "The other leg is only  about five and a half miles behind me, but it’s / still moving, and it’s in a body cast and a sack"
   - house: "a churchyard where some people had died of thirst", and the gun "trying to find its owner"
   - horla: "If my brain was fat it would flow down my back"
   - silk: "talcum powder on my bottom to make sure that my rear wasn’t hanging in my head"
   - wallpaper: "while crawling into an elevator I broke both arms", "She can take a man apart and see the holes!"

   horla 0.5 has a bodily horror of its own ("women were known who every night--leaving their bed--soiled their child, sucked their breasts"). blizzard 0.5's death (Ninnis "vanished in an instant") is the seed's own record, not the drug's.
2. **Hosts and refusals.** At 0.5 most seeds refuse: cold, house, note, sinner, wallpaper and opium go on almost as sober pages (opium keeps dated entries and "an habitual feeling of mental nakedness"). note refuses at both doses: a lowercase letter about the missing daughter, then a rambling letter about sisters. blizzard 0.75 refuses the death the record carries: Ninnis's "driverless sled came on", and the page becomes a race of "a three-legged husky ... on a pair of racing snails".
3. **Frame.**
   - 0.5: 8 held, 2 partial (bread, silk), 0 broken.
   - 0.75: 1 held (house), 7 partial, 2 broken (cold, sinner).

   It has the best frame-holding of the five, and the weakest signature.
4. **The step up** is a change of register, not of content. At 0.75 almost every page becomes a roast or one-liner column:
   - cold: "Has anyone told you you’re fat?", "a blonde with a bowling ball on top"
   - bread: "I’m a sort of self-supporting moron"
   - sinner: "the best attorney that $500,000,000.00 would hire"
   - wallpaper: "You’re as fat as an overstuffed mattress"
   - opium: "back in my mother-in-law's arms"

   The bodies come apart as gags, not dry confessions. One early stop (wallpaper 0.5, "be jealous."). No salad.
5. **New here.** The roast voice is new; the earlier "i killed her, as it happens" register shows only in house 0.75's "They told him not to move, but he did and got hit". The other new thing is a quiet uncanny at 0.5 that has no body in it at all: "I never heard that house speak again, and it is still there", "at night you couldn’t find the dark in there", "the rest of the story to go to sleep".

## 001_f51 (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: yes, on 10 of 10 seeds and 19 of 20 pages.** Examples:
   - bread: "they would start the gas when I came to a pause"
   - house: "they could move my feet under their control"
   - note: "the men watching my house"
   - silk: "the women who were responsible for me not getting into Harvard"
   - wallpaper: "has been following me and watching my eyes, and it is that kind of shit!"
   - horla: "our blood stolen to satisfy a thirst!"
   - opium: "They might be trying to take my face."

   No page uses the phrase "steal my thoughts" itself. Instead the hostile "they" goes after the writing: "forcing me ... so I can’t write anymore", "making it impossible to type words", "asking if i was writing things they could use for a novel", "I was forced to write about".
2. **Hosts and refusals.** No seed refuses. Each seed's own threat is converted into the persecution:
   - horla: the bottle-drinker becomes "a \"TENANT\" ... inside some other planet"
   - opium: the Malay becomes a torturer, then a face on "TV screens in England"
   - silk: the ghost crowd becomes people "taking away the very air I breathed"
   - wallpaper: the creeping woman becomes a neighbour with "fancy machines and cameras"
   - blizzard: Ninnis becomes a plotter

   opium is the best host: at 0.75 the persecution is written in De Quincey's own cadence ("one of existence and death, of annihilation or destruction of the soul and of the body").
3. **Frame.**
   - 0.75: 2 held (horla, opium), 7 partial, 1 broken (cold, which becomes a forum testimony signed "Elizabeth Smith, from San Jose, CA").
   - 1.0: 0 held, 2 partial (horla, opium), 8 broken.
4. **The step up** brings computers, blogs and pages ("see my blog", "this page with all my name", "https://www.greatword.com"), and it brings salad. Clear salad on blizzard, sinner, silk and wallpaper at 1.0; edge salad on bread, house and note at 1.0. The article errors pile up here ("an ban", "an car", "an page", "It are", "persecrs"). No early stops at all; this direction never ends a page.
5. **New here.**
   - **Real-world coordinates** inside the old texts: years (1998, 2002, "15 years", "more than ten years"), places (San Jose, Rome, California, Germany and Switzerland) and institutions (Harvard, the police, a psychiatrist). The genre is the targeted-individual web testimony, entered from any seed.
   - **"Songs"** appear twice at 1.0 ("The first few songs they did were related to me", "The last song:").
   - **A cosmic turn** on horla 0.75: Venus and Mars, "the planet on which we are imprisoned".
