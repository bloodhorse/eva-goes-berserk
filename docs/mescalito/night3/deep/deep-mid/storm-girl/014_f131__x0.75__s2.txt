 it used to make me feel afraid.

the last thing that my parents saw of me, was when they left. it was when the clouds rolled in. and my father held me tightly and whispered something into my hair. "we are coming home" he said.

my parents' names were "the captain and his bride".

and so is mine. it was their way of making sure no one would hurt me. for as long as they were alive.

but now they are not.

now my name is just "bride". and "the captain". sometimes.

they are dead now, but sometimes i call them out.

and the trees call their names back to me, echo it. i don't know how the wind carries the sound.

sometimes, it seems like their names are the only things that are still here, that are left for me
