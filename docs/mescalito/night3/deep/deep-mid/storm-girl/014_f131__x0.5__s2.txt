we were taken. my mother had no one left in her house to feed or look after us, so we had to stay at my aunt’s.

her name was noella and she had many sons. there was tim and william. william had two wives. one of his wives had children with william and with another man, who had left. she had ten sons and no daughters.

once, when she had her babies, tim, my aunt and the other men took them down to the stream and held their heads under water to see who was stronger.
