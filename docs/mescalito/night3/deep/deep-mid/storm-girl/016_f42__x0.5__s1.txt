 as if the strands of hair have their own lives.

once i cut all of them. my sister sat behind me with her scissors and a mirror in front of her, and for days, the sound of cutting did not cease. in the end, it looked as if my hair were woven with white flowers.

now it’s grown back again, thicker, wilder. sometimes they call me wild woman or crazy. once they tried to cut my hair off, and then my eyes were closed forever, so they stopped.

but it has to be this way, for us. we have no other way.

the language we use in our village, the dialects that were created during the civil wars of the last 70 years, were a necessity, to understand and to express. today they are often called the ‘mother tongues’.
