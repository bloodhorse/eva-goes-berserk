 once i let someone cut it and my grandmother got so angry, she called him names. now my hair has grown and my grandmother braids it with leaves.

there is a place where we used to live, in a village that is gone, but it lives inside us. my grandfather remembers the path there and sometimes i remember, too, a boy's face.

this story has no ending.
Liam's Notes:
1. "Anon." is an abbreviation of anonymous (or, "a nameless one") that was frequently used by British newspapers and magazines. Anon is also an abbreviation of another Latin word, anonyimus (which means, "one with an unknown identity"), that is also translated into English as, "anonymous". This second use of anon has the same meaning as the first use.
