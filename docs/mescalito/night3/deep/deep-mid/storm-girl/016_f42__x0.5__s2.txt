 and my teeth are also my hair, i pull on them until i hear a crunch.

sometimes there is a storm, and i am standing at the crossroads.

when i look around, there are two roads leading to different villages. but when i take the left, the village i leave behind seems so small that i do not recognize it at first glance.

but then there is always the other side that we see when we come closer and when we realize that everything has two faces: that we cannot ignore our pain or that of others.

if we walk towards each other and share, then our lives can change in an instant, like the sound of thunder that passes and the silence that follow.

and so we become, in our own way, a new language: one that does not have to be explained but just lived together; a language that knows
