At the camp site we could not set up our tent--snow is too deep--but made our beds on a hillock; crawled into the tents about midnight, after three hours work--it took all hands; there we are at ease and at home--two hours sleep is better than eight, without the sleep; better, at any rate, is our thought. Sleep makes our spirits high for the work ahead.

This evening the snow is blowing in clouds from the northeast and ahead of the wind it is 3 ft. deep and rising--I do not expect we shall find it so deep to the northwest. I do not care if the wind shifts to the S.E.--that way, I have said, is our line of retreat; if not, then
