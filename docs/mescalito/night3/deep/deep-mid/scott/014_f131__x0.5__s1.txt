Friday, March 15.--I feel much worse and walk more lame, but march I must. I feel too proud to stay in the hut while my companions suffer the cold and the storm, though I feel very miserable.

We had no dinner today. Our rations were eaten up, but our cook promised we would get a hot lunch and a hot dinner to-day.

It is dark again, we can only see the shadows of each other; and I think of a fire that has died down; I see only ashes--but what ashes! they are my last food, and they give me no hope of life. What hope can a man have if his food be ashes?
