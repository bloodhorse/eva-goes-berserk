We had no marching order to-day, so some of us decided to play cards to kill time; as it is our custom, but others thought otherwise.

Wednesday, March 21.--Our position here is perilous and uncertain, with strong southerlies coming upon us at this very moment and a bitter cold in the teeth. We were driven into this bay and have lost three sled dogs; two others were unable to travel.

It has been reported to us by an Eskimo hunter that a huge whale lies near to us and there are fears of attack from the monster.

Monday, March 26.--I must report a disaster in our party which is grave, to say the least.
