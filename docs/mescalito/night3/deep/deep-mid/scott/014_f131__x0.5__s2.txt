He had been ill; so it was very good of him; my friends sent us the best medicine and I felt much better.

Saturday, March 19.--Still camped and suffering--had some porridge this morning--toasted biscuits are delicious.

Monday, March 20.--This is my twenty-seventh birthday and we have just got out of bed.
