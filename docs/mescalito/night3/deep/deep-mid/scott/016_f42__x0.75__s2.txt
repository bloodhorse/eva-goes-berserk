This evening I wrote letters home from this outpost: "I have written, I am waiting, I believe that these lines will reach the people who read them with their heart wide open." We, as Palestinians and Syrians, as human beings who suffered from occupation, persecution and colonialism for decades, understand and believe in these lines.

On the way from Kherson to Crimea and back to Zaporizhyia, I will carry our demands on my back: We want an immediate ceasefire in Ukraine and we oppose all war.

On my back I will also carry our questions. I have so many questions on this visit, as we are not just one group with different views and positions on this war, but there are millions of questions. They are all important.
