 Walk Again. My Right Leg is Going DOWN, BUT, THE FATE WILL COM. I'M WAS VERY.

 Sunday, April 1st - This day. I have Come A Long Way From My First MILE.
 We are. 29 Miles from Home. This Year. The New World Has Given Us The First Mile. But There Is Will And I Believe We HAVE ALL A FUTURE IN A NEW WORLD.
 I have Done A Great Work In My FIRST YEAR IN A NEW LAND. THE WORLD OF NEW FARM. A LIFE I NEVER WOULD HAVE KNOWN. IT. It is The First Mile That We HAVE MADE A WORLD TOGET. [end of text]
