"March or not," said I, "the men are getting too cold; I can't get warm; my foot hurts too much--we shall die here." But that did not comfort us. And, somehow or other, it seems that you don't get killed by these colds.

Monday, March 19.--To-day I've got the last foot I'm to get this expedition. To-night, it is a horrid cold; the worst we have had so far; it has frozen all the poor ponies' feet--but not ours--we were all asleep and very comfortable, too, till this awful cold came.
