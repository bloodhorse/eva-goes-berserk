I will go and help make snow holes for some of these lads who can't cope; a job well worth doing for their morale and comfort.

Tuesday, March 20.--Marching is suspended owing to bad weather. We had a spell of high winds yesterday afternoon--result: many cases of snow blindness; several are sent back for hospitalisation, so far our numbers have reduced by half, I'm 3/4 frozen but still here!
