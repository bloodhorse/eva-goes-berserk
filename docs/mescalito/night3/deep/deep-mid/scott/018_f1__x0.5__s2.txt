Friday, March 19th. I have not seen my dog since last Sunday. We Have Been Looking for Him. We Must Search The Hills and Valleys for Him; But The Men Do NOT Wish to Go Back AND SEE WHAT MAY HAVE HAPPENED. THE POOR THINGS WILL BE THERE, BUT IT IS VERY HARD FOR THE MEN. We Cannot Move To-Morrow.

I AM HURT; MY HEART ACHES TO THINK OF HIM; THE DOGS WILL DIE TO THE BIRTH AND TO THE LAST; IT IS THEIR DESTINY TO BE WITH THEIR MASTER UNTIL HIS DEATH. I HATE THEM SO MUCH! BUT THERE ARE OTHERS THAT ARE ALSO MY ENEMIES.
