They reached their tent after a short time and lit a fire, and were soon in the tent, undressing and feeling quite comfortable; then, however, the frost set in sharply. "What do you do," asked Jack, "when there isn't anything in your boot to freeze? How does that keep your feet warm?" He put his bare feet on the stones before him.

"You don't suppose your toes can go numb, when they aren't there," answered Alice. She drew them back quickly from the stones. "Oh! what is that? You nearly burnt me!" She put her feet down at the end of the bed and went off to sleep again, quite unconcerned at the lack of blankets.

In about a quarter of an hour they fell asleep.
