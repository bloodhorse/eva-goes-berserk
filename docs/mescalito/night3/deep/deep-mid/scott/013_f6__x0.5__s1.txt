I'll have to stop. My leg has turned blue from the cold; no other damage than a numb feeling, so that is lucky.

Marching to a safe place was very slow as I didn't have use of both my legs for long distances.

Saturday, March 24. --After two days, the frostbite in my left leg has thawed and it looks normal; it's been numb the whole time and hasn't hurt yet; just waiting for the nerve to get better so I can get home. My team is on a break today to get things back on track.
