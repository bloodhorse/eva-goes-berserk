My feet are very cold, my right is blue--so are the other feet, my left hand, ears and face--can hardly eat my dinner; got my fingers into a pocket and pulled out the rest of my curry powder. My boots were covered with blood.

The snow had drifted against a hill and formed an overhanging wall--it had a doorway through which I could creep; I crept inside--made my camp, got the toes of my mukluk dry; I lay on the back of my toboggan; had some food. Then I sat in front of the fire. I could feel a little heat--then the feet warmed and stopped bleeding.
