My cook says it will get better and he has many things to make the pain easier; so I have some hope and comfort in mind; and the young officer in the detachment who will remain in camp until they carry me in will make me well again; if I cannot continue to work for others I may live in order to serve the Russian nation, who need us.

Monday, March 19.--We had to halt to-day on account of heavy wind and blinding drift from the N.E.; but now there are better conditions and we continue on. We have heard of many people, of all ages and walks of life, who have been taken prisoners. Some were soldiers who went on in front, and after them followed others.
