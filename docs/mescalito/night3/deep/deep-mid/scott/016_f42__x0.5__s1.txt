But here's the pity, and here the greatest suffering begins; when, by reason of wounds and injuries, our brave and patient men have to go down, how is it to be? They are lying in snow and frost without any possibility of saving themselves; for whom or to what power, in such cases, do we turn? Here we find our hearts and spirits failing; there's nothing left, no one can save, nor do they try.

All I see around me, as I look around the world, are those who are poor and helpless; those who, like the Indians, suffer the most from poverty.
