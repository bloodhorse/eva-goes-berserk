 march another step that day; all of us stopped; only five days from home now. Am glad that our expedition leader has gone with the sledge--will get on well now with no more stops for wind and snow, or "stopping to have tea".

We had one stop--it was terrible--the whole party stopped. One of them is going blind in right eye; they said the cause of this was his smoking too many cigarettes, or the heat from a kerosene heater, and that he will get a lot better now, he doesn't smoke so many.

I'm so sorry. This is not good luck.
