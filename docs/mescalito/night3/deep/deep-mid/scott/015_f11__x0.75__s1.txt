Mrs. Riddle said this very gravely and sadly; then added in a louder tone: "The pain will soon be over, though."

"I'll see that it doesn't," said Alice, who had found something of her spirit again by this time; she could hear people's feet moving, so there was nothing for it, but to say "Good-bye;" and she wondered what they had quarrelled about; but she found Alice's head, and said in a low whisper: "How very pretty you are!" She was speaking to a daisy.

And the Queen's eyes filled with tears. She couldn't help it--the Queen had no reason for crying.

"I am not a daisy, I am Alice!"
