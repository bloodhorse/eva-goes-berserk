Sunday, April 8.--Our situation has changed considerably, so I will update you with this letter from our present location.

The expedition to find Scott and Bowers has come to a sad end. I had to leave them and march on ahead due to severe injury; it was only 26 miles back, and the way we travelled we could cover 60 miles a week.
