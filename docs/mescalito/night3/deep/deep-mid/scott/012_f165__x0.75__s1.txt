Saturday, March 18, 1909. We've got here; my foot has gone completely. My hands have frostbite, so I can barely write. There are so many others, including the admiral, with injuries much worse than mine, but I know the captain would rather see this letter first, before anyone else gets the news of my accident, and he will get to see my story of how it all came to happen.
