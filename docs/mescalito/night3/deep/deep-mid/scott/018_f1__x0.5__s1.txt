 Go Far, Only 7 Miles; Distance Trav. 9.4. March, March 18.

Sunday, March 18. To-Day, We March 8.7 Miles; Temp. 25°, Wind. Force 4, And Frost. No Human Being Could Face It.

Wednesday, March 20.--I have now given My Diary. In It You Will See I Am Suffering and Living Under Great Trials, Yet I Try My Best Not to Be Anx.
