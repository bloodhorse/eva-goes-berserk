To-night I go to the chief and ask to rest a little--my heart is gone with my feet.

Saturday, March 20.--To-day, a snow storm comes on and hides our camp from our enemy, the Cree Indians. My man says, "It's time we go," and I tell him I want him to find my son. The Cree chief sends men to kill me--to make peace I want a child--a man child to replace those lost by my guns.

My friend tells the chief the "Bull" (the White Bull, my friend) can't go--he is lame.

To-day he says I will be a cripple.
