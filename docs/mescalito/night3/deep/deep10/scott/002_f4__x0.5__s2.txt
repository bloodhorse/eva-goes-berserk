Oh! my head and feet and stomach--they hurt, but what can be done? All must march if the sick come too--not much hope, not much, only for those that believe; all must march! We must bear our cross.

"Saturday, March 24th."--This was my first entry in my diary for nine days--nine days without writing and thinking and living; without hearing another's voice, and without speaking--only in a low, still, patient, and ever patient waiting--the snow lies so still upon it all!
