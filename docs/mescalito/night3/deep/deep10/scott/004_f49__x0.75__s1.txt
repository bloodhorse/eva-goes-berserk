Sunday, March 20.--Very little wind last night--to-day very warm and strong; drifts cover our trail--have to re-trace to get food, so I cannot send this until we come again. The dog and wolf skin on the A.E. Co. trail we know very well. It will not take long to re-trace the steps to the depot, then to-morrow, perhaps next day, I send letter with some food by two of our best men; the rest we follow you. To-morrow, or day after, I go with 100 dogs, find food on other trail--the one on which our friends, the N.E. Co. are camped--and send you a large quantity.
