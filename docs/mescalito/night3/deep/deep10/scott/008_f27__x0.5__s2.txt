April 30, to-night. The thermometer fell, the frost became hard--snow lay deep--I didn't stand up; there are a number of reasons that came off--no food or water left, so that I lay in my tent.

April 19. This day we reached a river bank at about 11 miles--my foot became more serious. There were some holes on my toe and we moved forward.
