The party halted here in this ravine for food; my tent, half-sodden and torn in the gale. It was useless to make camp; the wind and the cold were so fierce they would kill us, even here, without blankets; so the order is given, and we will march, not stopping for food again, to-night. To-morrow night, at 40 miles, the depot must be reached--the wind and storm will have killed many before the sun goes down. This is my last march to the depot.

We are at 73° 40', about 8 p.m.--very tired; can't walk farther.

We have had very hard times--one of the best hunters killed; two other dogs wounded by our guns, when hungry--
