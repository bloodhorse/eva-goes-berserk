Thursday, March 29.--A hard slog yesterday and a hard one today--10-14 kms of wind. In a couple of hours my boot had worn a hole, and now the skin of the outside of my foot was shredded. A long slog. 50 km in three days with only three meals; two hours rest on Wednesday night--my body has no idea of the distance, just a sore knee. No way we were getting any more rest until our last night--no one would risk the sleds going off track.
