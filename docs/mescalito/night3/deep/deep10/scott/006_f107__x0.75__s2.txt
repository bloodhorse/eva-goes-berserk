Yesterday afternoon, a storm was blowing hard; the boys said that a young girl of Ballygarrow was being "stuffed." This girl's brother went away some years ago to Australia. Her people thought it was time she was getting a husband and were determined to give her one whether she wanted it or not. When he came back she was taken and forcibly married to him.

Her brothers, however, went out after they were married to help the "stuffer." One brother came on a ladder, another one climbed on the thatch and cut it open; a third jumped out through the chimney. They came to her and told her she had better give herself up
