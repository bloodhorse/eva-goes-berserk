Tuesday, March 20.--Last night we camped. To-day lunch, 4½ miles. I am ashamed of myself. We got into the sheltered place. Last night, when we had to turn out for a new start, the frost bit on us cruelly; I had not slept for five nights and my hands and feet were cold--so were our bodies and hearts.

When the others left to walk round to the back, I took charge of our sleeping-bags, but, though they said it was only to get them ready to put on the bags, I thought it was for a new start; I did not care; I knew we could never do it--we were dead on our feet--I wanted to get out of the place.
