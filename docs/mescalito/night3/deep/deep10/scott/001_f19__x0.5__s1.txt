Our first party started last evening--we must take him off, if we have to leave him on the road. I was so ashamed. He is still unconscious and cannot get out. If I am sent off in my current condition, I'll go with it and see what it feels like to walk.

At night the sky clears up--I wish we had seen that earlier; there were signs of snowfall; snow would not stop if the snow were to be found in a place that was too much of the country, so I think I should leave.
