A very cold march and not at all a good time; only hope that some time or other we'll be able to do some real work. This afternoon it got warmer (40°), and a light southerly wind blew which warmed us up so well, I got too many thoughts and decided to do no more marching, at any rate this trip. After several days of fasting and hardship, my flesh, having lost a good deal of weight, I can hardly feel its effect upon myself.

Tuesday, March 19.--Last night I slept for the first time in six days and nights; and got to like the sleeping system; no night dreams--very soothing; my only complaint is that there was a mosquito in my bed. I killed the brute in a minute.
