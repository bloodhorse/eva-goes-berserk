Woke and I could hardly feel myself--feeling like my old self.

The worst is that the last few weeks we've had the same dream over and over.

Tuesday, March 16th.--Wind, rain, hail and sleet from behind--a force of nature against the flag and us all. Wind blowing colder from ahead yesterday; no one knew, so no one could tell; so they couldn't ask me--we marched all the day. Today I'll try again to march away.

Wednesday, March 17.--Marched away today; tried my luck on a rainy day and was beaten; I'll get back home somehow; I was beaten and I took the fall; it seems that every time we lose, the winners leave without leaving a trail, a name or anything.
