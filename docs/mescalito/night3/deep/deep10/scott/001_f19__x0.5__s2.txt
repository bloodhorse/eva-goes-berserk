We lost two ponies and two sheep at our hands; the whole thing is terrible.

To-morrow I must put myself into good shape; to-night we sleep with our faces buried in snow and with frost bite--nearly half the people, including me, were so frost-bitten that they were unable to sleep comfortably for five hours. However, they were treated in their usual manner and no one suffered serious injuries; however, our only worry was whether it was really true to sleep without our own body.
