All were ready, when I heard cries in rear and turned my head--a big black dog with the last team; I ran at it; it stood still and wagged tail, thinking we were friendly--I got to the dogs nose--bang!--my foot shot off my shoe--down it came to my hand--then my heart jumped as the dog rushed to fight; I dodged--off again he rushed and again I dodged.

After several attempts we killed it and dragged its body from the sledge. Now it was I noticed my foot gone.
