My foot is very painful. We must now change our marching orders--if there is no thaw this winter the entire party may die, so the surgeon, doctor and others say; only our poor foot has no hope; I don't expect it will survive the campaign.

The snow was up to our chins--in front--the sun was warm.

Wednesday, March 19.--It has been bitterly cold to-day--so cold we could only crawl across the ice and snow.
