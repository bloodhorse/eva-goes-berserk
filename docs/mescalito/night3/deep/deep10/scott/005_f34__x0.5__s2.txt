My foot is better but very sore.

Wednesday, March 20.--Wind still coming from the east; force 6; temp. -39°C.

Sunday, March 23.--To-day lunch we are 9.4 miles from depot. It was an emotional day with my boots; first they were freezing; second they were soaking wet--they're still damp; then, to add insult to injury, we have stopped marching to fix my right foot which is red and swollen.
