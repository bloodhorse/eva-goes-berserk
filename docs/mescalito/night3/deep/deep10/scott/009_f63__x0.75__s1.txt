[Sunday, April 12.]

April 12, Sunday, early morning.

There were several who had left camp--just as the others arrived.

We are all at camp--all but the man with no name. The wind is cold, bitter cold. He had just climbed to his sleeping place. He knew that it had to be something important when they came and asked to stay a bit.

It is not known whether the man is real, but what is known, is that the campfire, was a man. He sat on the bench by the campfire and it burned for all of eternity, as if to tell of his terrible deeds, he did so many, and so well. It's just another legend, after all, or is it? The Man in Black has been sighted and seen around the forest near camp
