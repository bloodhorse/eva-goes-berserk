A letter came in my sleep from Mrs. B----; my spirits are high. If the sledge will not move to-morrow, we will turn homeward.

The stars were bright above me. My hands and face burned with the cold. Before the morning came, I must perish. There, upon that flat stretch of endless snows I sank to my knees like one mortally stricken. There I remained with my gun upon my arm. The sky, with all its stars above me, was as white as the snow beneath.

[1] Sir Francis Leopold McClintock was born in 1819; died, September, 1873.
