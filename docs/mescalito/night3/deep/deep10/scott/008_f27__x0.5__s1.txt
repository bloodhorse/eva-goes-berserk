We all know, one has to have proper knowledge and I didn't take anything. My eyes have got red, and my mind was not strong; it was not the best. To-day I was glad and all were sad to get out. To-night I will feel very much worried, but not so as I think.

This morning my eyes got weak. To-day we shall get our right things; all went out on the road to get into our new camp.

The next morning my right leg had been bad and we all are so good to see that we were not too old. To be sure I must get into camp--no use to do it again.

Saturday, March 18.--To-day we are 20 miles from our last depot and will come by 12 at night
