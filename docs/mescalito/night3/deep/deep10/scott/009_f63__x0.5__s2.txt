We marched in single file for twenty hours.

Friday, March 16.--Yesterday the sun did shine a little bit; no good. I couldn't keep my feet up at all. We marched all night.

We stopped, as we saw some white dogs.

I've been sick three days now.

Yesterday evening, one of our dogs left the party--just slunk away; and we haven't seen it since.
