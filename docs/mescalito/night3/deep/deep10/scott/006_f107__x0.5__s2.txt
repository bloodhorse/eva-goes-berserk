Yesterday we were delayed a little while on account of our captain being obliged to lie down from a violent fit of vomiting and severe colic pains--he recovered.

Tuesday, March 19.--Still snowing; it's awful; we've lost another man this day, making five altogether.

To-night there's no campfire; it would be too great an expenditure of fuel for nothing at all--too weak to stand the cold.

March 21.--Wind S.S.E.; force 1 to 2. To-day, lunch, 32 miles from the depot, having been halted at noon to-day--a great relief; we marched only 16 miles since
