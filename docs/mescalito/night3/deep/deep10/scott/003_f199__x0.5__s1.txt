March 20th--Still suffering. The cold was worse--it's always hard on me to walk; I go in an open buggy and don't ride horseback--no room, I can't put the foot into a stirrup.

Sunday, March 18th.--Lunch--We have travelled 26 miles; weather, strong W., 9 mph; temperature -38.8°.

We will meet with no disaster from a frozen lake--we can get by, though a long time--in our own lives. The result will be better when the power is applied.

This week's action will help us, the results of this action will be of more avail.
