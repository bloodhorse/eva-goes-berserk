Marching Home.--A pleasant day with some drifts. A fine sunny afternoon with good drift. We had 10 miles for a short day's march. Our leader and one man fell out before us--hadn't strength enough for it. This man has been with us three days from home. He has two years of a flogging sentence and 3 months on his hands; is a quiet fellow but can be savage if provoked.

Monday, March 19.--We were in high hopes for a change; all that was promised us was to take our chances and do our best; it is always darkest before day, but the dark has never been so black before.
