April 15th.

Lunch--it has been quiet at camp--but not very much, a horse came over last night--this is a warning to you to look behind you when walking home.

June 12th.

It's a full moon again to-morrow; it is the same one from three months ago.

Sunday, July 26.--At night the sound of soft, gentle breathing. The figure was outside--it is gone. I wonder who was that?
