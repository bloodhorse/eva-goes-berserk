This morning's news from home made my day; they told us that the war is over; the good men have lost. I must be mistaken; no, there must be some mistake, please. It is written on your lips, it's a lie and it won't stop hurting me; so I write and you receive the letter with a smile and the same old bitter lines of defeat, my darling, the worst has not yet come; but the rain comes down on us all and the earth is just a hole that you dig into the sky, but still we sing for love and money and all that you do. Well I've been watching the rain on the roof; the last two years you were with me.
