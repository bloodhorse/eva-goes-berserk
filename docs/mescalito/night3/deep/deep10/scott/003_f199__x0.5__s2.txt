 go to breakfast; have to leave camp early this afternoon for 50-mile journey, to reach depot in time, and so can't risk foot.

We will see how long my left one holds out, and hope the foot doesn't fall off!

If there was any help at the back-end of my car I might do more--there's only myself and one or two more at the front.

Tuesday, March 19.--At breakfast to-morrow we may find that 4 ozs. of beef have saved our lives; no wind and drift yesterday.

No. of meals served on last 24 hrs.=6.5. The extra beef gave us the 7th. We shall not get as good a dinner to-day--we are short of potatoes--and it is impossible for the 12 oz. of potatoes each per meal
