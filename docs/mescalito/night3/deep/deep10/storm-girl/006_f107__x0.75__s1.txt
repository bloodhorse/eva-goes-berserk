 sometimes there is a weight, and the hairs get twisted into tight coils that feel like snakes crawling around my neck and head.

a great tree grows near my house. i am not alone. a bird lands in it, a dove with bright red feet, its plumage so dark and deep it shines like velvet, or black, or dark green.

its voice is as deep as the wind that blows from the west and as quiet as a child asleep, a voice you have never heard, one you can't believe could ever come from that mouth, or any other, for that matter, or that any creature could have a song of that kind, but you hear it anyway and are captivated.

when it flies away i watch the ripples in the air it makes. and then the ripples are gone.

there are others. there are many
