 and sometimes, there are flowers, white bells. but mostly it is green with thorns.

when it gets long i like to wrap it in my head scarf to create a new, fresh head, one without all the thorns, grass, sticks, stones or white bells.

my ears are like small sea shells, a little pointy at the bottom and bigger at the top, which gives the perfect home to hide my earrings when i’m in my headscarf. it has also become my trademark. the people who know me have seen them poking through the scarfs.

i love my nose, i think it’s my favourite part. it has the same point at the bottom like my ears. but my nostrils flare up, i get very animated with my facial expressions when i am speaking. so there’s always something to talk about.
