 there are some strands of white and sometimes a crow is trapped. sometimes there are dead animals tangled inside my hair, but when i turn to look for them they are gone. my hands are always red.

my hair is a thicket.

i like the flowers best. they grow all along the path and up through the treetops and they glow at night like fireflies, but their scent is that of blood, and their petals are dark red like a heart that has stopped. [end of text]
