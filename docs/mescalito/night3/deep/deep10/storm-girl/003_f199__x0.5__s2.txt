 the little birds look at it in surprise.

when it gets so heavy with the damp and the stones, it can no longer float above my eyes and so i close it.

but, sometimes, if the light shines in through it, the water around the reeds seems to glisten like jewels and so does the moss and the damp patches on the side of the road.

I look at the ground when I walk because sometimes there is something new that catches my eye: an insect or a pebble.

sometimes the leaves fall on top of each other like layers of rock, forming hills.

the air gets heavier when you stop to look, and your shoulders relax.

if the soil gets wet it turns dark. sometimes you have to look closely to find out whether it’s rain, dew, or the mist, or something that has grown into the dirt
