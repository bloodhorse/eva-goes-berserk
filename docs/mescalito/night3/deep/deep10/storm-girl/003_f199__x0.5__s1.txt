 when i can untangle the knot i feel that the something is letting me go, it lets me live again, for another moment, in a place full of light. this time the air smells different and there is a different voice. my skin is a darker shade of yellow than last time.

there are places that have always been dark and the voices are all silent, like in the town where the men keep to themselves. their shadows are not so easily erased and sometimes the sound is loud.

in other places it is clear and bright like the day the sky opened and it was sunny, but in my head it is like nightfall. it is only then, when it rains and i step into the street, when my thoughts can be free, like the wind and the water flowing from the rooftops, flowing towards the river, into the sea
