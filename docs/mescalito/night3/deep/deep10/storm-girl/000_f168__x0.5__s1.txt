sometimes my feet sink in the mud and the wind tears my dress into pieces and throws them back over the rooftops and over the treetops. then it becomes cold. it becomes hard to breathe, i get out of bed and open the windows. this always helps, except in the middle of the night. at the window i look out across the field to where the forest begins and there are dark things moving, just beyond my reach, that want me. they move just far enough that they will not come near the window, so that i am not frightened, and i can only just make out what they are. this makes my dreams much sweeter, but it means i cannot go to sleep, for i have to look.
