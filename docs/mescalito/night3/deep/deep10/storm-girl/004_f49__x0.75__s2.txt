when the white men come they look at the way i look and laugh. They call me names that mean ‘squaw’. My brother and sister do the same thing to them and the white man knows this.

“Witch. Witch” they call me, when the black cloud rolls up and my sister’s spirit leaves me. Then, my husband sees me and runs, like the frightened rabbits.

“Come back. You must not run!” I call to them but they will not listen.

Then the black clouds roll back to where they came from.
