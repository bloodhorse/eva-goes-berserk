It's hard work to go, and when the team stops you have to keep the horses close; one was loose twice and was kicked and bit before we caught her, which delayed our getting under way.

Monday, March 19.--Fell 400 yards and were told not to camp--"bad water" is ahead. We took another short walk and found that water in our tanks, the engine is so thirsty.

March 19--we were held up at noon on Sunday because a horse fell--not much good; just about finished. I have to rest every hour and do not sleep; it's no good; can't eat or sleep.
