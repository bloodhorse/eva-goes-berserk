At times my breath would go. and the chill ran through me as a storm began; wind grew in intensity--blown snow, ice and hail were tossed at me from ahead--this made it a harder trip and it was obvious the worst had arrived--we were moving and were only three miles from camp and not for a day to the place--from this moment on our future is in question and there is nothing I could say in this position; I can only hope the outcome is as good as the expectations of what would come from a position of this kind--all hopes for better.
