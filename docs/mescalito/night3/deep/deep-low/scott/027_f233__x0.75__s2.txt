 feel the movement; we had no idea how long the storm lasted--about an hour--then moved and put the right foot up for three minutes. My foot didn't fall back asleep; it hurt for 45 minutes; after the pain, there is the fatigue that it caused--now 30 miles from depot--so we must march there and take over the rest of our supplies. In the mean time we had two loads of bread to be baked.

This storm is bad--there was only one man from my right company in church to-day.
