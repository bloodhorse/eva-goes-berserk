My brother (my hero) tried to give me some first-aid, and we decided to cut it off. We started off with no blankets, but this time, our little camp. He gave me one blanket--my left leg was cut. Then the temperature started falling at an alarming rate and our supplies dwindled.

At night, my brother's foot gave out--his skin cracked in four places. Our clothes froze stiff on our backs--we didn't move and it was 7° below zero, wind force 1--nothing is as it should be--this was the 3rd day without sleep--and the fourth night.
