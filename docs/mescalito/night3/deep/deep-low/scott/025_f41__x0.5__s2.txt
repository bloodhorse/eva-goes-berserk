MARCH 21ST. Sunday--Still drifting; can't march or cook or wash--weather's too cold and strong--have had two feet and half an hour rest this week.

We were going through, but are still far behind; but there's hope of reaching our destination on Monday night or Tuesday at latest--when there I'm off.

I know of nothing better for my nerves than this long stretch on the march--every day brings me nearer the end, the place where my term is served out. Then the great joy to feel again that you are close. I have kept it down well, I think. I don't see how the other fellow stood it.
