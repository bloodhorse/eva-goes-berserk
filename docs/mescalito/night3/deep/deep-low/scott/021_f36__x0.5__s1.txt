Our position is becoming very grave. The last 40 hours has been nothing but walking; the average for three days, 28 miles; one day's march 50 miles.

It will not be possible for the sledge team to haul more than one week's provisions for two men.

If this keeps on for 50 or 75 miles more we must send the sledges back--in the mean time our rations have been reduced from two and one-half to two meals per day, and even that cannot be obtained regularly. One day's food was carried, two days' carried by dog, and we have one more day to get in supplies, which was very small to-day.
