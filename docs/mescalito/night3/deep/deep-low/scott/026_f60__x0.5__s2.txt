In the night we lost three good men to enemy action. All we had, as I have said, was our mules, our trusty ponies. Three of us fell out on the march. One had frostbitten nose--poor devil was delirious and couldn't keep it from falling off, even if we wanted to stop and re-freeze it. Another one of our party developed gangrene. It had got too bad. His arm had to come off at once; we didn't even bother about disinfectants or anaesthetic.
