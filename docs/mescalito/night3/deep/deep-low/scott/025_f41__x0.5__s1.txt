Wrote my journal till a gale from N. E. brought all outside on deck; had to lash ourselves fast, but one poor fellow was thrown out by a sudden gust--struggled a little, but soon lay like dead weight. There he lies. His last sleep has come on him, but our work has just begun, and there are other poor fellows in this storm who want my care more. They're drifting; their canvas has failed to keep back the icy gales--life's in them still, though their strength's too low.

"Good-b'ys" were few from those we leave, but no hand shook hands with him, no voice said a word as they crossed the gang-plank--some had to be dragged aboard--tattered, and bowed by suffering--wasted by sickness
