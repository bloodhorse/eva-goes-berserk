Tuesday, March 19th.--This morning I woke early with severe cramp; there is not one of us well; our clothing is rotten, we cannot change. Our hands and faces are cracked; some are almost useless from cold and rheumatism.

Thursday, March 20.--Our team horses were getting worn out. Our food supply was very limited and it was impossible for any one of us to procure anything whatever; I decided, therefore, that it would be wise to turn the horses and everything not absolutely necessary back to depot as they would not be of much use on theless travelled country and our progress would be so slow and we were not very well off at the best of times, that the sooner we arrived at our destination, the better for all.
