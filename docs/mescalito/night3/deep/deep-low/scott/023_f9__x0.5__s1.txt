Our Course Has Been North And South; We've Only Travelled A Little More Than 10 MILES SINCE DEPARTURE ON FEB. 29. In My Exhausted Condition I Was Given The Best PLACE IN THE COLUMN; WERE IN TWO PARTIES OF ELEVEN, ONE FOLLOWING THE OTHER WITH ABOUT TWO OR THREE HOURS SEPARATION; 20 TO 30 YARDS BEING KEPT BETWEEN MARCHERS, WHICH REDUCES DRAUGHT IN HEAVY SNOW.
