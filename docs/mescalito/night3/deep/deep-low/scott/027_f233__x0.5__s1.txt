 eat breakfast, feeling weak, ill and desperate, as usual, at our hopeless condition. Wind kept blowing strongly, but changed a bit. It began to blow a bit from ahead at last; we moved in to a new location, still a little N.E., and took in our drag; it's getting late to stop for a big one and it would take all day.

After 20 hours I went on watch with our relief man at the last position. They left me, I moved the tent a bit to see if we had more room; there is no more room. We will try for 12 more hours at 9.5 miles and then reposition if possible, which will put us 3/8 mile from depot, on 9-3/8.
