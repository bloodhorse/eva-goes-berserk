[Monday, March 19.]

Miles and miles we've been, but there are still many, many to go. It has been a tough week; I shall never recover; it will leave a nasty scar; a small part of me will die with the experience of having a bad cold, which is all my fault; I've let the condition drag on and on.

[SUNDAY, April 8.]

Well, this was the day the German people began the bombing raids against the cities and villages of Great Britain, which has caused much distress among the general populace and even in the royal family, who are at the very core of this terrible affair and who are deeply troubled by it, though the king, bless his little cotton socks, is a kind man
