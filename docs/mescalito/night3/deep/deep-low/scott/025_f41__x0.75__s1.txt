They tried to keep up but failed--frozen feet give them down, down; some say "walk"; some "lie still" and die. I don't complain--life and limb imperilled--some were buried in their icy sleep; my right foot is amputated--will you come?

April 30.--

From here I take a day off, not a minute's rest. A cold night--20°, 35 mph from ahead; some walk; others lie and wait; others never waken. A long wait, I know.

I can see my goal and almost reach for it--two months and more. Then they came for me and carried me--so I had a start, a broken back.
