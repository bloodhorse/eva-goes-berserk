Friday, March 23.--Winds continue from W., with slight variations of temp. I was going to start for home to-night, but the natives refused to accompany me.

Wednesday, March 21.--This morning, I heard a little bell tinkling; my orderly says he saw a dog running round and round it in circles. I saw no sign of dog when I came up, but my man tells me it is very difficult to distinguish it, especially as it was running at the time.
