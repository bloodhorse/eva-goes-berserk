If the "Boys" at Home Were Only Informed As to Our Actual Needs!

To the Editor, THE REVIEW:
Dear Sir,
We hear much about "Our Boys." Is this Not an Inappropriate Designation?

For Example, My Case. A Little Over Four Months Ago I Left the United States Of America With My "Boy,"--The Latter Having A Most Intelligent Appearance. The Destination, South Polar Expedition Under Robert F. Scott.

It Is True, "Boys" Have Not Heretofore Been Given "Their Full DUE." In this Instance I Have Done Everything For My BOY THAT THE CAPACITYS OF A WOMAN OF 38 WILL ADMIT.
