Sunday, April 15, 1872.--This day is one week of travel on our return. Our company of six sleds made a long trip through a strong, bitter, icy cold that burned us up completely. On our way home we ran into an unexpected ice storm with 60 mph wind; everything was buried by drifting snow. We could only crawl for nearly four hours until we made a halt and decided to continue on the way back.

Our horses, sledges, and baggage suffered severe losses from the biting wind; we are not at our peak performance; it is no longer a case of being in our usual form and condition.

March 18.--We arrived home. Our 6-month tour, which included winter and spring seasons, ended
