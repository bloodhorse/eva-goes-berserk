[Sunday, March 18, continued]--
It was our first day at 50 degrees; I can't feel my fingers nor nose, nor feel much hope of finding supplies, for no track of sledges is visible; my toes have all frozen but one; a blizzard came at noon.

To-night my foot has eased up somewhat and is nearly numb.

I've found that old pot of bear grease I packed last fall; now my nose can shine; 'twix't will help to save my face; the frost won't gnaw my cheeks; 'way down deep, a bit of heat might make a man's blood creep.

To-day, there was a bit more sun and less wind, though it still blew a gale.
