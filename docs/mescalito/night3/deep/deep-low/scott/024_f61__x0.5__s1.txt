At noon we found two of our Eskimo guides, and their dogs. The other day, the day after they left, we found a couple of Eskimo dogs, dead from exposure to cold.

Sergeant George Henderson and Private McEwan died, this morning.

After a rest for about four hours we recommenced to march. There were 22 men. I took over Sergeant George Henderson's tent, blankets and cooking outfit from Lieutenant W.R. Lister; and have to cook my own food. It is no use getting into trouble. My work has been much more severe than anyone else's; my food is insufficient for my exertion--I can't stand this.
