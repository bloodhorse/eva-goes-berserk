In the afternoon there were only four horses able to carry 19 men.

Monday, March 19.--Still moving--a distance of about 5 miles--wind E. to E.S.E. and force 7, temp. -42°; drifting snow--this is no picnic; we have  two men sick from exposure in front; no fire for four hours--that will make five.

This afternoon the frost bit our left hands off; no sign of life; one hand froze in my pocket. All night, however, we did not dare move, We had to stay there in the withdrawing room. It was very dark and there were no lights--so we left about four.
