At times, we seem like ghosts--our faces black from soot; the frozen sweat, blackened from our hands, which have rubbed and cleaned the frostbite; our clothing so full of frost that our legs don't move. When the frost has filled it to bursting point, you must get into it; when it gets cold again, the frost is forced out.

If a man fell in this snow and there were no signs of him, it would take two months of hard work to get to him; the drift and depth is so bad--so thick; I couldn't see where the front step is placed by my brother, who is just ahead.
