18.5.15- 15/18

Tuesday, March 16.---Lunch; we are 27 miles from the depot.

Our leader says: "No sign of depot or anyone". It was a lovely sunny morning but now a white haze, that may become fog.

Monday, March 15.--To-day we march 3 hours before breakfast. It's so cold you cannot take it out of the pocket--everything sticks to everything and nothing comes loose; not a hair out of place! My teeth stick together!

Thursday, March 13.--The sun is up--there is a very strong breeze with gusts, which would have been quite warm. The depot team, after having driven all day and half of night yesterday, came in this morning; the dog teams arrived 5:30.
