We are marching at night--very good, if there were no snow-drift and we didn't feel so cold, which makes me shudder and makes me fear--all the men have their toeses and fingers frost-bitten; one of my scouts was attacked by the Indians on Saturday. They rode up and shot at him. He took off and rode towards camp; the Indians were too busy looking at him to shoot. I have my horse shot through the body and neck--it was shot from a bush by a mounted Indian with a Martini carbine.
