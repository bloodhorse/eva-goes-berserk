After we arrived here we went for lunch at the hut and we have a rest of several hours.

Monday, March 19.--The next morning the temperature had reached 15 degrees, we have lunch, the weather has warmed, but we had no success, our last two attempts failed.

In this we must remember the dangers of this area; we need a better description, which we don't have.

The road from here goes north-west through a marsh, there is no one going that way except Indians, so they have a great danger. We should get more detailed information, so that people don't get hurt in this marshland.
