 As Though I Were Tied to An Object And Someone Had Given That Object To Another.

Sometimes it Feels Like Someone Else Has Stolen Me And My Own Body.

The Storm Gives Me A Different Identity. In The Black Cloud, In The Darkness Of Night, I Feel Closer To Myself, Than At Any Other Time. This Is Something I Share Only With The Wind. No One Knows My Other Identity, Nor Would They Believe It, Should They Hear It Described.
