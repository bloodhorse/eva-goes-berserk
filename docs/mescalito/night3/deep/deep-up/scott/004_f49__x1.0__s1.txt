Sunday, April 6.--The great cold comes on fast; ice breaks, no ice more this year; ice-giants gone. We have waited, and will go on in the A.M. We want more food than white man has with him.

The chief says his Indians will wait at the camp, while our camp will follow and give the dogs for the sled. Our dogs not worth anything, he wants no. I take his meaning and his advice; we go, he follows; he gives us his son.

He goes to our camp, and we to the Indian camp; to-night, if wind good, we may leave this place, but not if it blow strong from ahead, to-morrow.
