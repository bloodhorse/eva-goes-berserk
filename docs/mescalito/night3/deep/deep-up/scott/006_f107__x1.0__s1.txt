Monday, March 20.--There are 25 or 30 miles of us going on this evening--about 20 men--a mile of them are our neighbours. We were going to work at about 10, when I found my brother sick; he went on as far as he was able. It is very fine for me; he is young, and would like a drink.

There are four or five men coming this way for us to take out to-morrow; there is one old man in his seventies. His son died a few months ago and is lying now on his bed.

Sunday, April 19.--A long and severe winter; the weather is mild to-day; I think that summer has commenced.
