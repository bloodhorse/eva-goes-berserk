Wind is not favorable; it does not come from behind; the Eskimos donthe pack. One is named Sam--I think that he has a wife here.

At sunset we make our fire, and one Eskimo carries me and another packs my stuff; I go in Sam's kayak and drift back. He brings a young Eskimo. We go back in one boat with meat.

To-night Sam gives us the "jumper," but I have a very large spoon and plenty to drink--one big bowl; and to-night my friend gives me some good things, pemmican, and fish, and a pipe, and much tea; I is all good
