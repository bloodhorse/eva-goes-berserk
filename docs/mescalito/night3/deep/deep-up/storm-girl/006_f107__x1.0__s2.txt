 the first thing i would do on my wedding night, so i am told, was to undo the braids, and then go on into my bed and rest my head on his pillow, to give him all she could, but he never asked, nor would she tell. when i wake on this night i shall lie and watch the storm as i lay my head on this man’s shoulder and take him as i am and take his strength to keep me whole and safe. As for now i feel i’m fading like the stars. My feet are numb beneath the freezing ground and my hair has gone loose and I want to cry, to get home quickly.

This is a tale from a story I have been reading on a recent visit to Ireland, The Fairy Faith In Ireland, by Lady Augusta Gregory.
