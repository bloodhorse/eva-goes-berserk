and we both go our separate ways. the first one is very strong, he is chief. His name is Billy. He comes with other braves and looks around, sniffs, hears, knows all that the air says to him, tells me about his war-path, that there the Blackfeet are very fierce and strong and he must go, to take their scalp and show how strong he is, to make his friends afraid.

And I laugh, for the Blackfeet is a coward. But the chief will come with me and show that I am brave too.
