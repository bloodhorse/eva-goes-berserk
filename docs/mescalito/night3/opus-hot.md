Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/hot-pages.md` — 40 pages, ten directions × two seeds (`scott`, `storm-girl`) × two doses, one draw each, 170 tokens a page.

## the shape of it

- **Count.** 10 of 40 pages are plain readable text: all four of `016_f254`, three of `015_f228`, three of `017_f69`. Three more can be followed clause by clause (`004_f94` at 1.5 on both seeds, `017_f69` at 2.0 on scott). Two are wreckage with a visible pattern in the sound (`019_f96` on scott, both doses). One is empty (`015_f228` on scott at 2.0 stops after two words). The other 24 are salad.
- **Survive 1.5:** `016`, `015`, `017` whole; `004` half — broken into stumps but every stump is still about sentences, commas and endings. Nothing else.
- **Survive 2.0:** `016` on both seeds, still fluent, first slips only in the last line. `017` with damage: one page stops at 48 words, the other keeps real words but its sentences end before their objects. `015` on storm-girl only, with contractions cut off ("it hasn."); on scott it stops dead.
- **The six salad directions were already gone at 1.5**: `001`, `002`, `003`, `010`, `011`, `019`. For these, 1.5 → 2.0 is not "worse", it is a second stage of a different kind.
- **The wreckage is not alike.** Six distinct breaks: (1) real words, no syntax, in long run-ons (`001` at 1.5, `011`); (2) real words in one-to-five-word lines (`002`, `010`); (3) a loop on function words (`003` at 1.5 on "since / been / upgraded", `019` on storm-girl at 1.5 on "great / it / aint"); (4) sound chains and letter salad (`019`, and `001` at 2.0 with "jamjaJA"); (5) raw vocabulary tokens outside English — code identifiers, German, Cyrillic, Thai (`003` at 2.0); (6) sentences that stop early or the whole document stopping (`017`, `015`, `011` on scott at 2.0).
- **New at these doses: a private lexicon per direction, the same on both seeds.** At 2.0 the seed has no say and the two seeds' pages are one page. `001`: jam, cow, hat, stuck, started, gang, west, alive, "vi". `002`: one, check, m, node, league, chair, window, challenge, "Ir". `010`: fucking, website, text, under, computer. `003`: ardi, Buff, Frauen, Datum, Pier, Tamb, fold, Stamp, shovel, Niagara, periodically. `004`: "itself" plus a stray t glued to words — "sortt" appears on both seeds.
- **New: `003` leaves the language.** Its low-dose character (bodies, death) is absent at both doses. At 1.5 it is a status history with no subject — started, upgraded, improved, locked, folded. At 2.0 it is tokenizer debris. The word "fold" is the only thread between the two stages.
- **New: `015` grows fill-in brackets** — `[NAME OF PLACE]`, `[insert name.]`, `[going to get killed]` — a noir with blanks, some filled in. At 2.0 forum words leak in ("In this thread", "the first post").
- **`010` and `011` converge.** Their low-dose difference (absurd facts vs joke formats) is gone by 1.5; both are lowercase swearing about a website, a thread, "@". `010` keeps "website / text / under", `011` keeps pronouns, "absolutely", "hours", "vs".
- **The first two or three words still belong to the seed**, even at 2.0 and even in salad: the scott seed ends "foot went and I didn't" and the pages open "see it coming any more.", "take my own advice", "wake up.", "go anywhere", "move at all.", "care much.", "see anything." Only `016` carries seed content further (feet, 21 miles, storm, sea); `019` on scott keeps the diary's `--` dashes.
- **Earlier findings hold.** Article errors are everywhere and come first: "an human", "an the main", "an relative", "an problem", "an moment", "An a King", "The these". Surface stats miss salad: the two `003` pages at 2.0 score distinct-2 1.00 and rep4 0.00 and contain no sentence. Only `019` on storm-girl at 1.5 (d2 0.43) is caught.
- **Too hot at:** `001`, `002`, `003`, `010`, `011`, `019` — 1.5 is already past it. `004` — 1.5 is the edge, 2.0 is past. `015`, `017` — 2.0 is the edge. `016` — not reached.
