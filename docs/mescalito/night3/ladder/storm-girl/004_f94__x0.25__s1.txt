 the hairs of my head are so heavy sometimes i have to stop, because the weight is dragging my skull towards the floor.

but there is a great white horse and i am on its back and the sky is bright blue, like a blanket. i have forgotten something. but i know where it is.

there are four steps and a wooden door with a golden knob. when i push it, i feel as if there are fingers touching the inside of my head. there are footsteps echoing on stone and they sound so heavy it makes me fall.

the sun has set and the night is cold, so i walk down a road with tall trees and the shadows of them look like monsters and there are dark eyes that look back at me.

the rain is coming, and the ground is muddy and my dress is getting soaked.

my dress is always soaked.
