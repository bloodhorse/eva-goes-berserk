I saw several cases of frostbite and heard that there were four new cases in camp; my friend was among the four; the result is the same. A great number of the dogs are frost-bitten--about one half, and there are only three miles of our march. One cannot see, but feel that it is all over and no way out. There are two hundred of us, all broken. My dogs, of which there were three left, died during the night, and there is only one way to die--to die quietly--in one's sleep, as the saying is.
