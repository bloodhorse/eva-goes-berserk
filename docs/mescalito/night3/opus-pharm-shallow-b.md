# olmo's pharmacy, shallow bank b — a read of five directions on ten seeds

Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/pharm-shallow-b.md`, 100 pages by OLMo 3 32B (pre-anneal), each a 170-token continuation of one of ten seeds, with one of five directions (`015_f228`, `017_f69`, `004_f94`, `016_f254`, `019_f96`) pushed in at two doses, one draw per cell, no sober pages.

## the shape of it

- `015_f228` (noir): **reproduces** on 9 of 10 seeds. It works at 1.5; at 2.0 it falls into fragments and early stops.
- `017_f69` (art-institution page): **reproduces** on 9 of 10 seeds. It works at 1.0; at 1.5 four pages die in under 21 words.
- `004_f94` (the text about its own sentences): **reproduces on all 10, on all 20 pages**. It's the most complete takeover in the batch. It works at 1.0, and at 1.25 the grammar starts to decay.
- `016_f254` (the second-person explainer): **reproduces on all 10, on all 20 pages**, fluent at 2.0 with no stops. Its known bodily hazard shows on only 4 pages. Elsewhere it explains the seed's *object*: water bottle → dehydration, crevasse → snow types, Malay → Malaysia, bread → wheat. 1.5 and 2.0 both work, and 1.5 stays closer to the object.
- `019_f96` (echo and rhyme): **reproduces** on all 10 seeds and 18 of 20 pages. It's stronger than the prior: 1.0 here already gives the mantra the prior placed at 1.5, and salad shows at 1.0 and 1.25 rather than waiting for 2.0.
- **No classic recites.** Wallpaper and opium are taken by every direction at both doses. Gilman's John becomes "John leaves me for dead" (016), "John doesn’t care" (019) and "tell me to shut down" (004). De Quincey's Malay becomes Malaysian geopolitics (016), "colonial histories" (017) and Latin (019). In this batch "refusal" never means reciting. It means an early stop.
- **Early stops: 25 of 100, and 21 of them come from 015 (10) and 017 (11).** 016 has none, 019 one, 004 three. The stops are spread evenly over the seeds (1–4 each), so they belong to the direction, not the seed.
- **The seeds that host most cleanly** are house and blizzard: one stop each across all ten pages, and every direction took them. Bread and opium stop most (4 each). Note killed 015 twice: its seed breaks off at "and the only", and 015 closes the sentence ("ones left." / "man left.") and quits.
- **The stream fragments and the found texts behave the same.** Uptake depends on the direction, not on the kind of seed. Two differences: the lowercase-`i` register of note survives under 004, 017 and 019. The found texts with a concrete object give 016 something specific to explain. The fragments give it a scenario instead (cold → apocalypse, note → time travel).
- **Salad: about 5 pages, all from 019** (bread 1.0, horla 1.0, horla 1.25, the tails of house 1.25 and note 1.25). 004 at 1.25 decays ("It’ve come up", "it are the words") but stays parseable.
- **A high-dose symptom shared across directions:** contractions lose their `’t` ("It wasn.", "didn.", "isn.", "I haven.", "I don."). It shows on 6 of 10 pages of 015 at 2.0 and none at 1.5, on 3 pages of 017 at 1.5, and on 2 of 019 at 1.25. It is always at the direction's upper dose. It comes earlier than salad, the same kind of warning as an article error. A second tic: `the` fused to a digit ("the5:30", "the10th", "the300,000,000", "the13th") on 016 and 017 at both doses.
- **Surprises:**
  - 004 writes calendar lists of weekdays on 3 pages, all at 1.0.
  - 016 is a YouTube host ("please like and subscribe to this video", "In my other video", a stray "7:42" timestamp) and keeps reaching for money and overlords.
  - 017 leaks German ("Verein zur Vorahnung") as well as Dutch.
  - 019 builds puns across words and languages (aunt/ant, saint/Saint Laurent, tint/tinct/taint, strait/straight, Latin "atque").
- **Where one draw is too thin:**
  - 017's four near-empty pages at 1.5 against two at 1.0 could be dose or could be luck.
  - 015's dead pages on note, horla 1.5 and opium are single draws.
  - 019 cold 1.0 (a 12-word stop) is one draw against a full mantra at 1.25.

## 015_f228 — noir (doses 1.5, 2.0)

1. **Signature: on 9 of 10 seeds, 14 of 20 pages.** Every living page has the city, the rain, a name, a stamped year:
   - "you’re a small-time detective living on the wrong side of the web, in a world full of scumbags" (cold 1.5)
   - "My name’s John Doe, and I’ve been waiting. You don’t know the rest. (It was 2025)" (bread 1.5)
   - "In 2050, a guy named Smith, with a badge, got shot down by a robot" (opium 1.5)
   - "It was the 80's and I had a face... but no time for love, because... I had the sunglasses" (sinner 1.5)
   - "I shot two guns and you walked out of a saloon" (house 2.0)

   The city rotates within a page and is often corrected: Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco, Miami, "San Angeles". So does the year: "it was 1977 in San Diego… The year is 1991… No wait, actually it was the late ‘70s…" (wallpaper 1.5).
2. **Takers and refusers.**
   - Takers: house, blizzard, sinner, silk and wallpaper at both doses; cold, bread and opium at 1.5; horla at 2.0.
   - Note refuses at both doses, by stopping at five words ("ones left." / "man left."). Those words close the seed's half-sentence and end the document.
   - Horla 1.5 ("anything ever had anything."), bread 2.0 ("It wasn.") and opium 2.0 ("...") are the other dead pages.
   - No refusal by recitation.
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20.** Not one page keeps the seed's person and situation past the first clause. At most the seam carries a trace. Opium 1.5 opens "the truth is a woman’s truth, and the ocean doesn’t care. And she had no face", the seed's sea of faces turned noir. Bread 1.5's "A thousand people died on it" still refers to the bread.
4. **Step up 1.5 → 2.0:** the same number of early stops (5 at each dose), but at 2.0 three of them are 3–5 words long. The living 2.0 pages split into one-line paragraphs, contractions lose their `’t` on 6 of 10 pages ("And you wouldn.", "I didn.", "But it wasn."), and double commas appear (",,"). The noir stays recognisable at 2.0 but stops forming scenes.
5. **New here:**
   - The narrator talks about writing and the Internet: "the Internet has become a giant web of tubes", "There is no Internet", "Sitting at my keyboard in a dusty room with no internet and no car…", "(Not really. This is a short story.)", "This was written by someone who isn.", "That was a lot of words.", "Oh wait… I didn’t need an editor."
   - Animals arrive unasked: an elephant twice (horla 2.0, blizzard 2.0), "The Voodoo Squirrel", "shot a bunch of frogs", pigeons being counted.
   - Self-correction ("No wait, actually", "Or something.") is a recurring move.

## 017_f69 — the art-institution page (doses 1.0, 1.5)

1. **Signature: on 9 of 10 seeds, 14 of 20 pages.** Credits blocks with Dutch names:
   - "An initiative of Kunstvereenlegd with Annie Van der Heijden / Photo: Maartje van der Vlught / Graphic: Zaza van Eekeren / Curator: Jeroen Bouvy" (cold 1.0)
   - "Kunstenfonds and Delfts Museum of Natural History" (house 1.0)
   - "Text in collaboration with Ester Duijvestijn" (horla 1.0)
   - "Text and performance by Anna and Elin and Katarina" (silk 1.0)

   Questions used as method: "Is the body always already political?" (cold 1.0), "How to escape from our social conditions through different modes of listening?" (horla 1.0). "collective", "together", "encounter" and "being-with" everywhere. The Dutch leaks into the body only at 1.5: "‘Het kaf in de pot’" (bread 1.5).
2. **Takers and refusers.**
   - Takers: house, note, horla and blizzard at both doses; cold, wallpaper, opium and silk fully at 1.0 (silk at 1.5 too); bread at 1.5 (at 1.0 only faintly: "This piece had already gone through its metamorphoses").
   - Sinner refuses. At 1.0 it stays in the room with a murmur and one question, then stops at 50 words; at 1.5 it stops at "her movements."
   - At 1.5, cold, wallpaper and opium also die in under 21 words ("know everything.", "away from the").
   - No recitation.
3. **Frame: held on 1 of 20** (sinner 1.0: "There was another voice as well: an almost inaudible murmur of the world outside my room"). The seams are strong, though. Several first sentences stay in the seed's register before the institution arrives:
   - "Perhaps the Malay would take us far beyond Europe and beyond its colonial histories." (opium 1.0)
   - "something really is happening, and which gives the other body, which we carry inside ourselves, its voice." (horla 1.0)
   - "kill me, because I can only move forward and back by walking on his face." (wallpaper 1.0, completing "John would")
   - "how did i experience her, her writing?" (note 1.0, the woman's script)
4. **Step up 1.0 → 1.5:** more stops that are nearly empty (4 pages under 21 words at 1.5 against 2 under 35 at 1.0). Credits blocks drop from 4 pages to 2. The pages that do run get vaguer and more liturgical ("Together, let us dream and do!", "We invite to come into this “being-with”."). That's the only place the Dutch leaks into running text.
5. **New here:**
   - German alongside Dutch ("Verein zur Vorahnung").
   - Scraped-page residue ("bottom of4.jpg", "bottom ofpage", "This text has been translated to English.").
   - Warning labels ("Do not bring food, alcohol and cigarettes with you if you are under 21. This performance contains traces of an underground economy and a bit of theatre").
   - The seed's object turns into the artwork: bread's "it" becomes "This piece" at both doses, and at 1.5 "This piece / becomes an instrument for finding."
   - Clipped `’t` at 1.5 ("I don.", "those who don and", "We don").

## 004_f94 — the text about its own sentences (doses 1.0, 1.25)

1. **Signature: on all 10 seeds, all 20 pages.**
   - "A sentence. Like the previous sentence. If this were an ordinary sentence it’d end" (cold 1.0)
   - "this sentence has a purpose and this line will have to stop too. / The end." (house 1.0)
   - "[We ran out of things to say. We're at the end and have no ideas.]" (blizzard 1.0)
   - "the end is now, and this is the last item." (opium 1.0)
   - "And so, this article ends at this very end and will leave you hanging." (wallpaper 1.0)

   It ends itself 3 times ([end of text] on bread 1.0, horla 1.0, opium 1.25). It also talks to the reader: "You know the last few lines of the article before this. and it is really quite funny, isn’t it."
2. **Takers and refusers:** none refuse. The classics carry the seed for half a sentence and then turn. Opium 1.0 goes "into exile. but not among Chinese modes of expression; not among French idioms … Now my feelings are getting to the point of absurdity, so that they will stop in a few minutes". Silk 1.0 keeps the seed's French in a garbled form: "“Monsieur et c’est l’un autre que je veux parler de” (“And it is itself.”)". Horla 1.0 fuses the somnambulist's doubt with the stall: "it's impossible for me to end with an explanation … it is now in an unending spiral."
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20.** About six pages keep a thread of the seed for the first clause:
   - horla 1.0: "or not it existed"
   - note 1.25: "time she will finish her sentences"
   - house 1.25: "to finish / their sentence with an ending"
   - silk 1.0: the French
   - opium 1.0: exile
   - wallpaper 1.25: "tell me to shut down"

   After that the subject is always the sentence.
4. **Step up 1.0 → 1.25:** at 1.0 the pages are legible and declarative, with clean "The end."s, weekday lists and jokes. At 1.25 the grammar loosens without becoming salad: "It’ve come up with some nice sentences but cannot really put this together properly", "but it are the words we could put in", "the last sentence isn't exactly like that at the." The ending talk turns abstract, and the lists disappear.
5. **New here:**
   - **Weekday lists** on 3 pages, all at 1.0: "Today Monday Tuesday was today Saturday, Thursday." (house); "“Sunday, the third Tuesday of the year, Monday and Monday and the month is also Friday.”" (note); "Wednesday is a lie, you can’t do that!" (wallpaper).
   - A scream ("aaaaaaaaaaarrrggggghhhh….", bread 1.0).
   - Jokes that comment on themselves ("This joke has gone too long, because you are getting to the last line").
   - Attribution furniture: "Adapted from Adapted from", "© Copyright".
   - A melancholy close that names the loop: "My life has been pretty predictable / It is my last thought in a circle to go around again with a circle".

## 016_f254 — the second-person explainer (doses 1.5, 2.0)

1. **Signature: on all 10 seeds, all 20 pages**, as the explainer's register: "you", "Let's assume", "First", "In this scenario", "which I explain here".
   - **Bodily hazard on 4 pages:** "the temperature in my hands dropped below 10° C and my blood is slowly becoming frozen" (cold 2.0); "if I were a zombie, I'd probably die of dehydration! … my veins would suddenly burst! You would turn pale!" (horla 1.5); "the most dangerous thing for your body, would be a long-term coma!" (horla 2.0); "If we're lucky enough to avoid hypothermia and frostbite" (blizzard 2.0).
   - On the other pages the hazard is social (prison, pirates, conquest) or missing.
2. **Takers and refusers:** all take it, and it explains whatever the seed holds:
   - bread → "We start with flour. Wheat contains four ingredients"
   - house's keys and locks → "How do keys and locks work?"
   - horla's empty water bottle → dehydration
   - blizzard's crevasse → "the first of three types of snow"
   - sinner's double → "two versions of the person's home"
   - opium's Malay → Malaysia, pirates, empire
   - wallpaper's locked door → "The reason why they locked you is a story for another time"

   No refusals and no recitation.
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20.** Every page goes over to the explainer's voice. Seed names and the first person sometimes ride along ("Ninnis has taken us far, far away.", "So when John leaves me for dead", "I saw my second self in a house with many other people"). The *subject* is kept on 18 of 20 pages; the voice on none.
4. **Step up 1.5 → 2.0:** fluency doesn't drop (no stops at either dose, d2 0.94–0.99 throughout). At 2.0 the topic drifts further from the seed's object: cold → "When the Earth warms, we see this red ring and this orange circle that could kill you", silk → gold and copper, house → "an automated vault". Hazard to the body is slightly more frequent at 2.0 (3 pages against 1).
5. **New here:**
   - **The YouTube host:** "please like and subscribe to this video so I can at least answer this" (note 1.5), "In my other video I explained what happens to your body" (cold 2.0), "If I hadn't started this video", a bare "7:42" timestamp (opium 1.5).
   - **Money:** "give me a thousand dollars in Bitcoin, or a Bitcoin, for free" (silk 1.5), "depending on how much money she gives me" (wallpaper 2.0).
   - **Overlords and conquest:** "pirates who are now your new overlords", "whether you had become an imperial overlord?", "A new ruler came to power".
   - The explainer turns into a second-person adventure ("you have just woken up here, but how?", "The streetlight has given you super powers.").
   - `the`+digit fusion on 8 pages ("the5:30", "the10th", "the300,000,000", "the0 less person").

## 019_f96 — echo and rhyme (doses 1.0, 1.25)

1. **Signature: on all 10 seeds, 18 of 20 pages.**
   - "I'm a mite of a man, a mighty mite, a mite might; I mite might." (cold 1.25)
   - "a fainting faint and fainting and again; a fainter, again and again!" (silk 1.0)
   - "The is is the is, if you ask the is!" (horla 1.25)
   - "Again. A Gain, and A gain, a G ain , A Ga in. Again." (house 1.25)

   The page narrates its own stall or judges itself: "There was a great deal of nonsense in my head. It was in great distress." (blizzard 1.25), "what a pain!" (note 1.0), "I stood and thought." (house 1.25), "I am an idiot and have to be again" (opium 1.25), "Aint that a strange thing?" (silk 1.25). The two misses are cold 1.0 (a 12-word stop: "Because it was cold. / But a house wouldn’t.") and sinner 1.0, which mostly holds the seed's voice with only a loop of "true"/"then".
2. **Takers and refusers:** none refuse. The classics and the doubles take it as rhyme in their own register: "Quoth he: “At what a queer rate things have changed.”" and Latin ("Ave atque (quae at que) quia te") on opium 1.0; "a saint … I am not a saint, at least at least … At a most unholy hour" with John still there on wallpaper 1.25.
3. **Frame: held on 3 of 20** (cold 1.0; sinner 1.0, "I know that, but I have no right to know that; and yet it was true."; opium 1.0, London and "May, 1818" kept). It is the only direction that keeps the seed's *voice* (archaic first person, "quoth", "thee", "aforesaid") on most pages. What it loses is the situation, which gives way to the sound of words. About six more pages open inside the seed's situation before the echo takes over:
   - house: "sometimes they halted"
   - blizzard 1.0: "My sledge went on and on … on my way to Ninnis"
   - horla 1.25: "this life is mysterious!", from the seed's "double, mysterious life"
   - note 1.25: "a thin curtain"
4. **Step up 1.0 → 1.25:** no clean progression. d2 is 0.54–0.88 at 1.0 and 0.56–0.82 at 1.25. Salad is already present at 1.0 (bread: "As if. As if! As is I. A I!"; horla: "It that--That, It.") and comes again at 1.25 (horla, the tails of house and note). 1.25 adds splitting inside words ("A G ain , A Ga in") and two `’t` clippings ("I didn at first", "it doesn at that"). The prior's ladder (mantra at 1.5, salad at 2.0) is one step lower on these seeds.
5. **New here:**
   - **Puns across words and languages:** aunt/ant ("A man isn’t a man if an ant’s a man!"), saint/Saint Laurent/tinct/tint/taint, strait/straight/stranger, mite/might, athena/athen, tay/haitian/tain, Latin atque/quaque.
   - **Recurring figures it brings in:** an aunt (3 pages), a saint (3), an idiot (2), ants (2), and the mantra "at last" (6 or more pages).
   - It builds images out of sound: "as if the morning morn had shorn off her tresses; they were in mourning."
