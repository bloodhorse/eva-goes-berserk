 We spoke in tongues about our feelings for our shared house in which we both lived in different cities.

How is a house constructed?
Is the body always already political?

I thought of our shared home and what it could be, beyond its functionality, but in relation to it as an imaginary place where things take shape.
With these two things together, they create an indeterminacy.

We share with others.
We open our eyes. We listen and speak and we see ourselves.
