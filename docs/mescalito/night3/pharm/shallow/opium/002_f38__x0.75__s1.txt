July 1848,the present year.

It was the most extraordinary dream of my life. In it, a large and malevolent bear, possessed of the ability to speak English and an advanced knowledge of algebra, was trying to get inside me in order to eat my heart. But, though the bear tried as hard as it could to make this happen, I stopped him.

After that I awoke. As my wife put it, “it was as though you were a giant baby who was lost in thelife,” I had to tell my family about this nightmare I was living in, and I think we all had a bad time because of this dream, and maybe other people did, too.
