October, 1833

My life is like a nightmare. I go to sleep, and find myself back in my mother-in-law's arms, and I don't even recognize myself. It must have been something I ate in my former life; and it doesn't agree with me now.

The above was written in October, 1819. In that year the Emperor was in Russia.

If there was an American poet of any value he would probably have to be put on the throne in China.

He'd look better as an ornament than a statue, and I don't know if that is intended as praise or an insult.
