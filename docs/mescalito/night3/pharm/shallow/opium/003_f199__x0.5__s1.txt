July 22, 1818

I am at a loss what to do. I have an habitual feeling of mental nakedness. I feel thoughts they are every day stabbing and persecuting and tending to annihilate my self. They are but too powerful for me….

My sister has lately purchased a young Malay slave, or rather bird. This girl has the most preternatural and evil countenance that can possibly be conceived….This young girl, when I was alone in her company, disturbed me to a degree I could never have anticipated.

July 30, 1818

For three or four years, my mind has been so harassed and confused as to prevent any rational process of thinking or reasoning.
