For some years now I have been exploring how one might inhabit a different reality and what is required for an existence in other dimensions. As a first step in that process, I decided to take you with me and we started walking around, looking at each other and talking together in search for possible connections to our past and the past of theour. We, who are strangers in these surroundings and find it hard to navigate our own positions in time. The spaces and places that have become present are inhabited by a multiplicity of stories and people. In search of traces, in a series of performances I attempt to look at other worlds.
