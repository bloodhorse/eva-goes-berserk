We continued in that fashion until at last the sun fell from its place in thesky.

A great and terrible thing had occurred that evening. As I stood staring into the stars I felt that all the good people in all the world could not bring about such an occurrence, even if they worked all the nights, which, thanks to electricity, are always dark. That night the sky was so cold it made my cheeks feel numb.
