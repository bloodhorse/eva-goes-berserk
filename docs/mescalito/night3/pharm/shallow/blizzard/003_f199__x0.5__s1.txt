Ninnis, George and my entire team, 10 sledges, were gone, vanished in an instant. Not the least sound came to tell of the fate of those I left in front, not the faintest whisper to show what had become of all those human lives, those men and animals. No doubt it was over in the minute in which I looked round and away. My first feeling was of bewilderment at the thought of Ninnis having made his final error and fallen to the same death that I had been trying so hard to escape for years. It was my own turn next. It must have come and it had not struck yet.
