A dog jumped on me in front of my sledge, knocking the snow goggles out of my eyes. It took a good ten minutes for us to get our lines and sleds straight again. At that time there were three men to a dog: a husky and a Norwegian, and two men with two dogs apiece, each on his own team, both trying to make for the South Pole and not being able to make a landing.
