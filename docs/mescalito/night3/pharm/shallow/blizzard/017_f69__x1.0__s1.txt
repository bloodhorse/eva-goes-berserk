Museum Night (2): On the13th, we gathered on a long table at a bar. It is not about what to tell or what to do. The main thing is: How does this bar feel, where it is at a certain place on a particular moment, without having a clue where this might go.

It's 12:45 p.m., 2 p.m. and 1 o'clock in the13th, which means a lot, to a few.
