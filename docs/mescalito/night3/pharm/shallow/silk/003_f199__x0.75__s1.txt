When the music stopped I didn’t hear any noise; but then again there was the rustling.

At this moment, if there is no wind, I can’t feel a feather; if a feather touches me I don’t notice; and I wouldn’t feel my back being scratched, so I put talcum powder on my bottom to make sure that my rear wasn’t hanging in my head.

The man at the hotel kept looking up, saying, “When it rains, they all come out!” Then it would get light and they’d go back.
