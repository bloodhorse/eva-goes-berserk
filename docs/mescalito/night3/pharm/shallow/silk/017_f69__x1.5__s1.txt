The festival of “Heteroculture”, was part a happening with an intimate and experimental setup, curated by A collective and individual group in residence. With this they are investigating a moment of uncertainty that exists within our relation with other and how it is performed.

This festival took place for three evenings. We will move between “being in touch with” and “not understanding” during these encounters and try to explore and change our perception. We invite to come into this “being-with”.
