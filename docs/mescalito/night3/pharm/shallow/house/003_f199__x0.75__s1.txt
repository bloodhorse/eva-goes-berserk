I walked through a churchyard where some people had died of thirst.
There was an empty cemetery beside a street lined with broken cars, which could only have come from
one of the cars on the road; the rest must have come from a funeral procession. It looked like it was going to rain;
there were people with buckets, collecting donations of old newspapers; some had already donated.

They said to him, “Don’t move,” but he did and got hit. Then they told him not to move.
They told him that if he left, he’d get shot, but the gun had run out of bullets; it was trying to find its owner
without much success, when it got to him; he asked, “What can we do to help?”, and was answered
by silence and an angry stare.
