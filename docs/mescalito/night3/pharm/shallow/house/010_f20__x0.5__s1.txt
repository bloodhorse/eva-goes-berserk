There were the men I’d seen by the river: there were a couple of old guys who didn’t look
dangerous, they had an ox. I asked the ox for directions but he wouldn’t talk to me.
The town was large but very much a small town: everything was built out of stone; it looked like it had
been assembled from castles.

People kept stopping to ask me what was up and then when I explained about my brother being in jail,
and my brother having told me not to get into any trouble but now I had a mystery, and a town,
and I wanted to ask everyone about it, they’d usually nod their heads, as if to say: I get it; that
sounds like you.
