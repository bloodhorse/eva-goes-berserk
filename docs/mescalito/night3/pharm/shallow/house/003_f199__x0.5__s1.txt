Some of these were my old neighbours who did not want me to see what was going on.
They made signs for me not to go that way and so on, and I just had to turn my back to
them. I looked for some way round them. But they followed me wherever I went.
The houses seemed to have faces, the trees looked sad, the river ran black as if with tears
over a fallen brother, and the mountains, with the wind playing across their green shoulders, had a
certain grace to their form which gave the landscape an almost human dignity.

I turned the corner, and there were all these people sitting at the window. They did not even
see me, though they knew me, so I did not want to go to town.
