My favorite places in Amsterdam are those spots that I’ve visited over the years and have marked. They have an energy to me, or I remember a particular event happening there. Those moments can’t be put on paper. So how to show these experiences?

How can a person enter into the situation in an alternative way? How to understand a moment, if there’s a distance in space, or if one’s sight is obstructed, and so one can't really see it, because of the position, the height or the direction. How can the space become accessible if we enter into the situation and use all senses?

With a little help of my friend.

I use different forms to describe, or rather, to bring a situation forward.
