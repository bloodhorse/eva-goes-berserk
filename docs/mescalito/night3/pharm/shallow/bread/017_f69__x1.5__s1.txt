It was made from my personal experiences and memories from
The Paf festival of The Good Life and The Bad.

This event was inspired by
‘The other way of being a good feminist’ at
‘Het kaf in de pot’. A moment for women in which
we shared a meal.

With these events I wanted to show
what can happen if we could stop talking
for a second.

‘Paf' stands for an open platform that gives a voice to people who do not
recognise their surroundings and want to say something that
can be different, together.
We have all been through different moments that made it difficult
to think and imagine beyond our current state.
