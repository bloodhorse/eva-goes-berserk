But now, what could we do about the situation?

There was but one way. To return home! And this I did. The streets were filled with a clamor of noise--noise of many kinds; of loud and of soft sounds, all making up what was surely an unceasing cacophony. There was music; there was the noise of motorcars; and the air itself seemed alive with noise of various kinds, even as a musical orchestra. As for me, I could do little more than to listen. A million times, perhaps. And what if they should see? They could do so easily--even they--with the help of my most advanced night vision equipment! If the noise persisted, what was there to do?
