My name is Sara Vissers, a painter who works and lives in The Netherlands, currently residing in Vienna. I am a storyteller. In my artistic research I search for narratives of how bodies feel the world through a queer gaze, using performance as an entry point for creating worlds together, while connecting bodies across space and time. How to escape from our social conditions through different modes of listening?
