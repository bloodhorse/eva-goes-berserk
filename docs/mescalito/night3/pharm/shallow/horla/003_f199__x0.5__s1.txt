I did not recollect walking, although, indeed, the distance between the two pieces of furniture was not more than six feet. What, then, was it that had transported me through my bedroom while I was asleep to the opposite side of it, when, without moving, without making a single step, in deep, motionless sleep, I had reached the place where the drink was which I so needed?

A terrible vision passed before my sight, and the blood stopped in its course.
