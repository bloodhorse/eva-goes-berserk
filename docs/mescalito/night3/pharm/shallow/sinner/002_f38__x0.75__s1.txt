One morning my eyes opened with a start; they opened so fast that it made me feel like a cat. My breakfast had already been brought, as I knew it would, because my cook had gone out that night, leaving no time for cooking, and when the dawn had broken he had told me to be ready, so that he would not have had the chance. In my mind's eye, I had pictured my cook in many places and postures. As the cook would not tell me what had become of the dog I had had to kill with a gun, I began to doubt that this person was, indeed, the same cook whom I had been looking for in my mind for the last forty minutes.
