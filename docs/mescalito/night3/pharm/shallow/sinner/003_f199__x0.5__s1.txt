He appeared like me in everything except my being larger; when he moved he looked just like myself; I saw my whole person at full length; I was sometimes so frightened, that I screamed aloud, but my companions perceived no person but myself; they used to call me and bid me go and come; my other self sometimes did, as well as I; sometimes I called and my other self answered me. If my hands or any other parts were bruised or burnt, he felt it the same. This I suffered for several days, but I had it under as great command as I pleased; for it did not trouble me but when I pleased.
