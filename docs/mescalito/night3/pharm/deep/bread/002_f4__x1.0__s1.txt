 What does
the moon eat, when the stars go down and there are still apples to bite?

All this is true.
What did the moon eat while we slept?

In the dream the world kept turning
while I was asleep.
There is still a smell on your hands

from the time before.
            —Mary Oliver, “Bitten”

A boy once found a butterfly on the back
of his left arm.

She was black, the butterfly,
like an little sack of darkness.
Like a shadow. A breath.
—Debora Lind
