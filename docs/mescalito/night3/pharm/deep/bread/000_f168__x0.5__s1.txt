Then I put on a kettle and went into the lounge with my newspaper, where the coffee maker sat, still
looking a little lost, next to a chair which, at this time of the evening, would be filled by the owner,
who’d sit in the same way until she was finished.
The smell of hot bread made the owner turn from her normal thoughts of work and bills and shopping
to a thought that might have been triggered by a faint sound or a scent on the wind: maybe the
sandwich-maker was about to start, maybe he had a friend, or an evil twin, in another country who had
already begun to bake a fresh supply. Either way, he might make some more and they’d be available in
a day or so.
