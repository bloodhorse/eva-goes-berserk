My love for it is not easy. It’s made from wheat, but not only wheat.

The sky was white that morning. It looked like milk.

And if it was bread, and bread, what was left, if not the smell?

The rest, after bread, was silence, or a field,
the moon.

If we must name a time,
let it be this one.
Let it be summer,
or never,
in this or no other time,
before or after
a rain that falls on leaves.
A day with no name,
and no one remembers
any name but our own. [end of text]
