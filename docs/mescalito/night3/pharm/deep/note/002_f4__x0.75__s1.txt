 woman who was ever like me was my mother.
when they were born i took their bodies into my mouth, their soft heads. it was a dream and i could hear myself saying that you, yes you, are the one that will carry the future, little stranger.

the dead, as we all know, cannot come home, and as the flowers begin to decay the angels wait for me.
the night i left my house i found the front door locked, as all doors should be. [end of text]
