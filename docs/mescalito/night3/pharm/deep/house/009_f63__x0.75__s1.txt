Then, suddenly, there were none ahead; but behind there were a million more.
Then they stopped again and turned round towards the town; the faces were those of dead people.
There were many things I did not like in the street; but the worst thing of all was when someone
sang to you.

When you were in a lonely part of town and there were no others on the streets, you might
occasionally hear the sound of a music box; its haunting tune echoing in your head until it slowly
dwindled to a halt as if it was never there to begin with.

And the streetlamps only cast a faint light, dimly revealing the silhouettes that
wandered towards you, then past; always just on the periphery of your vision.
