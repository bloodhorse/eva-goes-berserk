I waited with the people.
In this way, when the night came, all were ready for the great talk of the They who was first to
fight. It was over.
I turned away to my horse, but the horses of my enemies stood close to my horse and did not
want him to leave.

I said they were all my enemies: he did not know what that was.

But I took one man who was going to fight and sent him away with us, because we could
fight, too.

When he got home the young man said: “We are ready. When is the man to come?”

“To-morrow night at the Good Star.”
