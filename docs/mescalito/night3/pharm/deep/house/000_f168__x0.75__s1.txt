It was dark in town and raining hard, which meant that all the windows were steamed up and no-
one could see out of them, so the shops stayed closed all day and nobody could go out in it, or
get home, because no-one can find the town if he goes out for the night.

The inhabitants have all moved to other parts of the country. Those that haven't, live here, so you
must be very careful about whom you ask questions of, or you will be taken to places that
shouldn't be visited and exposed to views and noises which you may have thought had ceased.
