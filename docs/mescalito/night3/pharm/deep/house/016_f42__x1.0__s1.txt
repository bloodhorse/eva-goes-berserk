People of different backgrounds came from different sides.

Some people who lived near us and some others came to me, some in solidarity with what was
happening, and some from fear, for different reasons: fear of being hit by the weapons that Israel
use in our lands.

There are some of those who had a message that we, as human beings, cannot remain indifferent to
this injustice.

As such, there were people from the nations, from Palestine and Arabs from across the borders
and also those from different countries.
These are all peoples and individuals, all of whom had one thing in common, and that was
opposition to this crime of aggression that the Palestinian people face. And this opposition took
the form of people gathering to stand in front of tanks. I saw many young men who joined
demonstration.
