 We talked. Finally we were both satisfied, so I was permitted to walk free in the Nowhere Land.

Now the sky has darkened, and a new sound is in the air, a sound I’ve never heard before, a terrible, threatening, terrifying roar, like the noise made by a catfish. Is that what the Thunderbird hears in the thunder?

No one has seen or heard it again. Now there you see, he is dead! I go away now to sleep, and I will send the horse for the smoke which you say came from your camp fire.

I am the Blackfoot war-chief, but my name I cannot tell. I am old; I will soon be with the good spirits.

Blackfoot, farewell! The land of the Sioux is his home, he is the brother of my father’s father.
