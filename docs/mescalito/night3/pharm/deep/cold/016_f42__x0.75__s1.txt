 My fingers and toes had already been burned with hot oil.

But I heard nothing but the cries of those whose bodies had become piles of dust, those whose hearts had been buried under rubble and who were unable to tell the the pain of those they love, whose cries we could no longer hear. They became words on social media, which could be erased with one click. These became stories and the world moved on to another news and forgot. So, they are not like us and the voices that we heard were those that the media brought us to our homes.

In our hearts and souls, we know that this will continue in this land of war. We do not believe in anything else and do not think that anything other than a permanent solution can bring an end to the blood. So we are determined to work hard so that a new horizon appear.
