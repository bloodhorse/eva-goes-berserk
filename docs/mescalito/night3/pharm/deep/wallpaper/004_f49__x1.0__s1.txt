John says it’s bad enough already! If Indian got his woman away he’d never get her back. She’s the best Indian that I know! Her sons are chiefs of great note!

The whole Sioux tribe may hear the great war-whoop, but she won’t!

They might as they like take her and John—nobody cares; they will all go. If the chief is so, his woman can go, and his braves.

He has a big heart, he can’t be bad! The women that creep with me can go! No one wants them; we all get away! Indian and white men, too, may have our women, but white men don’t let us go—why?
