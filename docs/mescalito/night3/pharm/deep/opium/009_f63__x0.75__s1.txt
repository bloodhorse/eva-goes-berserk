February 15th

My aunt having gone down stairs to wash up the tea-things, and to put the spoons and forks in water to be drained ready for use next morning, left Mary and myself in the Church, with no light burning. We did not at first perceive it; but, when we did, we saw it go along the end wall, and round and round, and pass in front of the window, as though following our eyes; then we became terrified. I did not wish to stay longer. We hurried to the door; it would not open. The key was not in the key-hole. Mary tried to open it; but the key turned sharply and would not stay in. We could not open the door till, after long and agonizing struggles, it suddenly flew open
