In these things, as I believe, we do not deceive ourselves: this is our first language: the language which we learn when we are hungry, and go among our mothers. I suppose the others, our so-called languages, are not a handful of words, but a hunger, a thirst, a falling down, a rising up again towards what has no name.

It has happened. All this was unforeseen.
How could we have guessed, at first,
that after this death, even death is given,
even death, its own life. How else
will you be remembered? All the living
who love us, we are their food and bread,
our broken days in their open arms. All our enemies

and old enemies. When I go
from here, I shall take nothing,
so thorough has the letting-go been
