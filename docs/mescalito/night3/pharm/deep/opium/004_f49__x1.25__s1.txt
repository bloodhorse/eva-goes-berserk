This morning there my friend came from the village; he was the man of the His Majesty. He The first words were spoken. We have smoked together and drunk coffee and calumet; now we will exchange presents, that our friendship may last forever.

This night I am at home of his son; I will sing the dance, that his women shall not look upon me in fear and anger, that his wife will look at me, that they will dance and be content, and have pleasure in each other’s company. May the stars look down in favor and peace; let all men do this thing which they ask, that their women may not be ravished from their homes.
