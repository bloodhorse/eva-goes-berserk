It May is well. The Englishman who was sent by the president to the English settlement to receive the message which had come, has been detained by sickness. His son (my friend) goes. He goes, and in a short time will bring a letter for me to read, from my white brother. When that time comes I shall know all which you want of me; and then, I suppose, he will accompany us on the Forth? It Is that which you want? [Here the old chief waved his hand, to indicate a boat and his attendants, and said "He goes."] What more do you require of us?
