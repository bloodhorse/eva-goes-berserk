It was from the Chinese language that I derived the above expressions; it was not borrowed from them; I did not inherit it.

The reader, though he be of no very profound research, has doubtless not failed to observe that the Chinese language comes directly under that description of tongues to which I have frequently adverted as mysterious.

It would be impossible for me, or for any man, in one short dissertation, to lay down and demonstrate the rules for composing and pronouncing all Chinese sentences, and in all possible keys and modes; I must content myself with giving a few of the simplest rules, as a specimen.
