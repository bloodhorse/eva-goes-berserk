July, 1819

A people like the French and Italians cannot but admire us. If they were able to speak our language and understand the contents of our books and to understand their real spirit and to listen to them and understand the words they speak and read our works and our lives and the laws of our government, then the world would see the true character of this country. They would know what kind of men and what kinds of laws, what a people and a people of such noble ideals, what great powers they had achieved and how great their responsibilities
