It was plain enough. He did not mean to kill any man with this boat; it that he meant he would do it before night; he did not mean it, for the Frenchmen are very good fellows, and do no such things; but they say, he must wait and hear from his people on the other side, where there and French people go. If the White Chief was to be there to-night, there will be more talk with the Black Fox, the medicine-man of the Bloods.
