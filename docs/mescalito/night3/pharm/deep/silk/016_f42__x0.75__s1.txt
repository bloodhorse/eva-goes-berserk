These women are French and their names are Madame Pompadour and Marie Antoinette; their time have been erased and the only things left for them are their memories; I am very close to the history of their time. But they were women in flesh and bones; and even though the circumstances in which they found themselves were often painful and cruel, they always preserved the spirit of nobility, refinement and freedom, and thus they remain immortal and engraved in history. But their greatness have also become their misfortune because the women who came after them had to follow the footsteps they traced. Perhaps for this reason they still live today and continue to fascinate and inspire us, although many have tried to erase their memory.
