A little further down she speaks of her lover who she also calls ‘You’. It’s interesting to think about a man’s body in the way one would describe a forest, or the ocean: ‘How handsome he is in this moment,’ she says, ‘his shirt is so beautiful it makes my heart hurt to think that he will live on without it.’ Or, later: ‘the light he casts in a room when he goes past.’ When you leave your home and leave the things that live in it, they call to you.
