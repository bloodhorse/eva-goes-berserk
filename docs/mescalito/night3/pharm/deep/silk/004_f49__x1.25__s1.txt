 two warriors in splendid feather bonnet came in. One is Big Bear. He knows my brother by the great name of Chief Iron Snake; and so we shake hands, and smoke together.

The Big Bear and I go to his tepee to drink. When we get there we have tea made by an young wife; he and his squaw eat; we all drink coffee.
