 the voices were silenced and I found myself alone; the next instant the women came into sight again and stopped; and at last one woman’s face showed among them. I did not recognize any of them.

At one end of the path stood an ancient and deserted chapel, which was not visible from any road and had been deserted for a hundred and twenty years. But if the old legend was true, one of these ladies had gone inside, and was seen again.

At night she sat alone at a small table by a dim fire and the rain pattered outside. A young girl with dark hair and blue eyes had been reading, when suddenly a man stepped out of a mirror on the door across the room, and after staring at her, vanished.
