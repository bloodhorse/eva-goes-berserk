 every Indian to whom I speak understands my speech; his friends and brothers may hear, if they choose; this I does not object.

The white men will give us the head of our friend; but it if not be, we shall come in our canoes with their scalps to his friends; then I shall say he is a liar, but if he says he will send one of his people to buy his son, they shall be free; his son will stay, he shall come back and give a horse for this little man (showing me).

I wish my father to send his young friend; he is wise, but I do not see his face: his eyes are blind; he does not look; they cannot meet; but I will take this white man in my arms and walk about; then his young friends will give him presents
