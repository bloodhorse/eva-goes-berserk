If I was always in pain, it is because my body was in love. When I came upon it for the first time it was naked in its loneliness and it seemed that nothing had been taken away; everything was still there, but the shadow, and this shadow too remained.

There were certain people whom I was so glad to meet I could hardly bear it, I thought of them for many years.
