At first the world learned that Ninnis and Mertz were dead, while the entire group were grieving, without having had any contact with their country for nearly three months, without a way of getting news of any kind and with all communications having been broken off for so many years. The tragedy, and especially the circumstances under which Ninnis died, could be compared to what had happened at theundiscovered pole.
