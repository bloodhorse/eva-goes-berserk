In an agony of fear that Ninnis might be still alive, I called out his name, but received no answer. In any case, Ninnis had obviously not been killed, otherwise we should have seen some trace of the incident, such as the vanishing of his sledges.

By the time we were able to locate him, a year later, all we could find were some spare boots and socks and an old overcoat, together with the remains of the expedition's last bottle of brandy.

I asked the officer how he came to be standing outside a tent, without a tent. He said, "Sir, the tent pole broke".
