A mile from here I turned. Ahead was another snow field.

When we started back we went across a snow-covered ice shelf which had once been an island.

Ninns and the other men are still at the place where they set their sledge down. Ninnis has put out the stove. It has not been lit. His feet are frozen, Ninnis. And I am saying good-by. My dear, do you see what I bring back in my hands, the twisted cord and the bell that was tied to the collar of a black dog, and I think I may be allowed to tell you something: this is a true story which I shall keep safe between us. It will grow as we grow old and be our life. [end of text]
