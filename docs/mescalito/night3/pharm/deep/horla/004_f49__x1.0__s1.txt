How came this thing about the water-bottle, that the pale-faced chief would take with him? It had come in my canoe! He could go far with his canoe and find much fish in his water. It had come in his hand! It was given! He wanted more, many, much. This chief will find water and make no more trouble. My young friend is welcome, but my friend will be too much of a somnambulist to say much to my chief. I When you return you will see him at Fort St. Anthony, if I live!
