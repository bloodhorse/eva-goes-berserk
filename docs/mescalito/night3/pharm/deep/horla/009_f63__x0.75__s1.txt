It is the beginning of autumn and in our country district, which has remained faithful to the customs of old times, it is believed that, when a woman goes out to pick apples at the hour of dusk, there are times when she can see the Devil standing under an old tree….
That was all; it was my turn now….
When she told her story to a friend of mine who, at that moment, happened to pass the village of Roissy-les-Sarcelles, my friend heard the strange cry of the night from a distance…. The woman stopped suddenly, the fruit dropped from her hands and she looked fixedly at a place behind the two young girls.....
