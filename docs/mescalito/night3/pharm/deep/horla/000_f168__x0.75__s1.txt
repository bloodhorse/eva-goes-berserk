I felt a violent dizziness, an inexplicable joy. My thirst was quenched! But in what manner, in what way, had it been done? That question I did not try to solve! It is well known that a glass of water takes eight hours before it begins to work; at least it should work. It took eight minutes in my case, for I was able to write the following morning, but the taste remained.
