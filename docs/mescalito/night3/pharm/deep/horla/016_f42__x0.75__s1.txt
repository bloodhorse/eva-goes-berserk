Then my fears, which were only half formed, took on more concrete shapes and became stronger. The first idea, in the as in other crises, was those of vengeance and violence: 'What do the people who drink the waters have in common?' The most visible things are those who make us suffer. These people who do not believe, or those who pretend not to believe in our suffering. And to be convinced that our sufferings were not just fictions of a weak mind, the others must first go to the place of death to see what they do with us and our suffering.
