There was another bottle of water in the cupboard. I opened it; it was also empty! I rushed towards the wash-stand: that also was without water. Then I remembered having heard the water run two or three days ago! Then the explanation of this incredible phenomenon dawned upon me, and I left the room. I went as far as my friend's apartment, and said to him: "Is it you?"
