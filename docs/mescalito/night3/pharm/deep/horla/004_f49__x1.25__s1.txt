My friend said that he could come to-night again. I will meet him with the first morning sun.

I will not harm him with his glass--only give it back; but give him, in exchange, another white bead; then they will be friends once more, and he will no longer fear, but go far off to other waters where live other Indians, where the braves never strike the war-pole of our camp; but go and fight with the white man--he will have their scalp. My friend will come back in four days; then the war-belt shall be tied once more.

In the morning the sun will be bright--it shall light the smoke and give me a pipe of peace
