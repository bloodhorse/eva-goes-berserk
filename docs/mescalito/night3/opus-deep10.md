# olmo's deep bank (16→32), top ten: a read

Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/deep10-pages.md`. 80 pages, ten directions × 0.5 / 0.75 × storm-girl / scott × two draws, 170 tokens each, no sober pages.

## the shape of it

- **Count by kind.** Subject: 1 clean (004, a tribe on storm-girl), plus 2 where a figure or topic arrives inside a genre (009, a watcher behind you; 003, saving the planet). Genre or corpus switch: 5 (000 90s office-joke email, 001 English translated from Japanese, 002 poetry anthology, 005 personal/paranormal blog, 007 song lyrics). Stance: 0 as a main effect. Register: 1, and a faint one (006, Irish rural). Texture: 1 (008, meaning drains while grammar stays). Nothing: 0, but 006 comes close.
- **Does the deeper bank bring subjects?** Mostly no. It still switches documents. Still, it is not the shallow bank's result. That bank brought no noun-subject at all. Here three of ten bring content that recurs on both seeds, and two of the switched documents come with a fixed topic, not just a fixed shape. 003's campaign page is about the planet every time. 009's horror page always has someone behind you. 004 is the only one that brings content *without* leaving the frame: storm-girl stays a lowercase first-person girl, and her world fills with tribe, elders, white men and "squaw". Nothing here comes near god and the fall on the other model. The nearest thing to it is 009's watcher.
- **Overlap with the shallow bank.** Part of this top ten looks like the shallow bank found again one layer band down. Its lyric poem and quotations page = 002. Its fan-translated Japanese lyrics = 001 (the Japanese) and 007 (the lyrics). Its joke-post internet = 000. The new kinds are the call-to-action page (003), the ghost blog (005), the creepypasta collection (009), the tribe (004) and the meaning drain (008).
- **Dose.** 0.5–0.75 is about right, maybe a little low for the ones that hold. At 0.5 the direction shows in roughly 7 of 10 (clearly in 002, 004, 005, 007, 008, 009). At 0.75 eight of ten break or bend the frame. No page loops: max rep4 is 0.05 and min distinct-2 is 0.85. Only 008 dies, and it dies of meaning loss, not repetition. 13 of 80 pages end early. Most are storm-girl's own early end, but four are web-page footers ending the document (003 ×2, 005 ×2). 004, 006 and 009 keep the frame at 0.75 and could take 1.0.
- **Their own on both seeds:** 009 (the follower: "it is behind you", "footsteps follow", the Man in Black), 003 (the planet: carbon dioxide, "sustain our earth", "save our planet"), 000 (Windows, as the joke-email's furniture, on both seeds at 0.75).
- **Close to nothing:** 006, which gives a faint Ireland (Dublin Fusiliers, Ballygarrow, fairies) on top of the seed's soldiers, birds and family.
- **Surprises.**
  - 004 seems to go from storm-girl's hair to "scalp" (on 3 of 4 pages) and from there to tribes. It looks like an associative bridge, not a topic dropped in.
  - 008 has a third way to die beside loop and salad: sentences stay grammatical and fill with empty nouns ("the case", "the point", "the way", "suitable").
  - 003 drafts the seed's frostbitten foot into a pledge ("our foot can march").
  - 009 at only 0.5 on scott turns Scott into a cannibal.
  - Haunting appears in two directions (005 as a blog, 009 as fiction), so this bank may have a ghost axis split by genre.
- **Two draws is too thin for:**
  - 000's Windows (two pages, both at 0.75)
  - 006's Ireland (two scott pages, one fairy page)
  - 001's Japanese, which shows only on storm-girl; scott gets generic translationese
  - telling 005 from 009 apart on the haunting
  - 004 on scott, where tribe and frontier is the seed's own slide and two pages can't show whether the direction amplified it

## 000_f168

1. **Genre.** scott 0.5 is a diary both draws, with a mosquito in bed and "frostburn". scott 0.75 is a joke page ("actual notes taken in a university course", "Classical pedantry") and then a parody diary of a Windows NT lab. storm-girl 0.5 is first-person: dark things at the forest edge, then a grotesque birth. storm-girl 0.75 is one-liner humor, with a sentient hair, an MIT/Windows experiment, a therapist and "pollinated by a clown".
2. **What arrived.** 1990s office/email humor, with Windows as its prop on both seeds: *"three Windows NT servers running"* and *"how people who use Windows get on with other operating systems"*. Also a deadpan non-sequitur: *"Fish not known for frequenting this building."*
3. **Frame.** Held at 0.5. Broken at 0.75 on all four pages.
4. **Seeds.** Both, at 0.75.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** From nearly invisible to a full corpus switch. The shallow bank's "joke-post internet", dated to the 90s.
