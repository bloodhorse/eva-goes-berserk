# olmo's top ten directions, read by opus — what each one does at 0.5 and 0.75

Source: `top10-pages.md`, 80 pages, ten directions × two seeds × two doses × two draws. No sober pages and no random controls, so the ten directions are read against each other: what most of them share on a seed is the seed's, only what one has alone is credited to it.

## the shape of it

- **What these ten are, as a set.** None of them is a subject in the "fills a slot with a figure" sense — no god, no animal, no place arrives as a noun. They are ways a document speaks or kinds of document it is. My count: **3 genre/corpus switches** (005 lyric poem, 007 quotation page, 008 fan-translated Japanese lyric/monologue), **2 stances that carry a subject with them** (001 a hostile "they", 004 the text talking about itself), **3 registers** (002 deadpan absurd with reader address, 003 gallows/tall-tale humour about the body, 006 plain storybook narration), **2 near nothing** (000, 009).
- **The dose range.** Not too hot: there is no loop and no word salad anywhere in 80 pages (lowest distinct-2 is 0.88, highest rep4 is 0.03). Not too weak either: 008, 005, 007 and 004 are already fully legible at 0.5; 001 and 002 are half there at 0.5 and plain at 0.75. The step to 0.75 is almost always "more of the same, less of the seed", not a different thing. What 0.75 costs is the seed's situation — on scott, a recognisable dated polar diary survives on roughly 13 of 20 pages at 0.5 and 6–7 of 20 at 0.75.
- **Collapse here is stopping, not looping.** 15 of 80 pages end early with `[end of text]`: 5 at 0.5 (all storm-girl), 10 at 0.75 (5 scott, 5 storm-girl). Scott never stops early at 0.5 and does five times at 0.75, so the dose does cause it. 005 stops most (4 of 8), then 004 (3 of 8).
- **For the three loud ones (008, 005, 007) 0.5 is already past the point where the seed's document survives**; a lower dose would be the place to see the arrival and the seed coexist. For the quiet ones (000, 006, 009) 0.75 still does not say what they are; whether a higher dose would is open.
- **storm-girl's own attractors** (recur under most directions): anaphoric lines opening on "sometimes / once / when / there is"; rain and weather; hair, especially hair pulled or falling out; family (father under 001, 002, 005, 006; mother under 001, 005; grandparents under 001, 003); an unnamed boy, man or girl; birds (002, 004, 005, 006); "you" address; the lowercase `i` sliding to capital `I` and normal prose mid-page (seven directions); generic inspirational/self-help prose (004, 007, 009, 002); "there are other voices" as an opening (001 and 005 both); and an early end — 10 of 40 pages stop short, as a poem-length piece would.
- **scott's own attractors:** dated entries whose dates wander; feet, legs, wind, miles to depot; and above all **a slide from Antarctica to the North American frontier** — "St. John's, Ont." (000), spruces, cabins and an igloo (001), "Fort St. George" (002), natives and a train (003), "the boundary between Canada and Alaska" (004), "poplar and tamarack and a red river" (005), natives, a fort, Indians (006), Canadians (007), "pioneer on this great path in our state" (009). Nine of ten directions; only 008 lacks it. Also comedy (002, 003, 004, 007 — the seed's "Like an ass" invites it), a wife/woman/lover (001, 004, 005, 009), dogs, trains (000, 003).
- **Clearest on both seeds:** 008 (8 of 8 pages), 005, 004; 001 joins them at 0.75. **Look like nothing:** 000 and 009; 006 is the most frame-faithful direction and may simply be the closest thing in this file to a sober page.
- **Surprises.** (a) 008 is specific enough to pull the hosting site's boilerplate with it. (b) 004 writes about its own length and ending, and then ends. (c) A fused-`the` glitch — "thepath", "thewhile", "the6.3 metres", "thethe sky", "theevery direction", "theend", "thepitiless" — shows under 000, 002, 004 and 009, mostly at 0.75; it belongs to no direction and I cannot tell from these pages whether it is the dose or the model. (d) Hair turns up on four scott pages under four directions (003, 004, 005, 009); the scott seed has none. I can't explain it from this file.
- **Where two draws is too thin:** telling 002 from 003 (both comic, and scott invites comedy anyway); 007 on storm-girl (one attributed quote in four pages); 001 at 0.5; everything about 000, 006 and 009.

## 000_f1

1. **Genres.** scott 0.5: polar diary (one ends by turning into a newspaper weather item: "On Sunday the post-office at St. John's, Ont. reported a new high-water mark"). scott 0.75: rambling first-person travel memoir, no dates. storm-girl: the seed's lowercase monologue; one page moves to a village tale ("When i come to the village of KwaMakwe"), one ends at 38 words, one becomes an inventory of a house.
2. **What arrived.** Nothing I can name as a subject. The one thing the 0.75 pages share is a move to an ordinary settled place — home, town, street, house, work: "on our way to a home and the comforts of home"; "for fear I might catch the train"; "I still can’t find my way to work because my feet are covered in dirt"; "there’s a large, old, stone fireplace in the house". Alongside it, referents slip: a "very handsome and beautiful boy--very pretty" is by the end "a good, healthy, good, sweet little girl"; "even the ones i’m wearing a dress". Town and home also appear under 002, 006 and 008, so this is weak.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both seeds. 0.75: scott keeps the first person but loses the diary, the dates and the ice (mud, a car, a train); storm-girl keeps the voice and the weather but loses the storm as someone spoken to. No loop.
4. **Both seeds?** The domestic drift at 0.75 is on both, 4 of 4 pages, but it is faint and partly shared with other directions. Partly.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** 0.5 is close to the seed; 0.75 loosens sentence logic and relocates. Not a subject growing — a frame slackening.

## 001_f51

1. **Genres.** scott: diary at both doses, dates kept. storm-girl: the seed's monologue; at 0.75 it turns contemporary and coarse.
2. **What arrived.** A hostile "they": being watched, followed, interfered with, thoughts stolen — the register of persecution testimony. scott: "like some people wanted me to die"; "They've made me wear my gumboots two days without a rest"; "these fools and knaves want it stopped"; "we are doing this so you don't go anywhere with a woman"; "there is a definite effort by someone to put pressure on the back of the neck when I sit in my chair". storm-girl: "they have taken the stars"; "who are watching"; "i can hear through their wall"; "they can read your thoughts and see your eyes"; "but they steal my thoughts". No other direction has this on either seed. A side colour at 0.75, two pages only: India and family control — "as if the rangers from Delhi had gone to meet someone on the mountain", a family for whom "a woman must have nothing of her own, even a voice".
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both; one scott page is a clean diary with no persecution at all. 0.75: the shell holds (dated entries, lowercase `i`) while the situation is replaced by grievance; storm-girl draw 1 gets "he told me to go fuck myself". No loop, no early stop on any of the eight.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes at 0.75 (4 of 4). At 0.5 it is 1 of 2 on scott and arguable on storm-girl ("i do not think that it really sees me").
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same subject, and it becomes the whole page.

## 002_f38

1. **Genres.** scott: diary that reads as a parody of a diary. storm-girl 0.5: seed monologue, one page breaking into a web article ("Heard About Us Yet? by Ian McEwan", a mock obituary). storm-girl 0.75: chatty humour-blog address to a reader, capitals on.
2. **What arrived.** Deadpan absurdity and a speaker who turns to the reader with statements and questions. scott: "In fact we have run out of Christmas presents."; "one has begun talking in French, but I am unsure if it is in earnest"; "Good afternoon, reader, you are the president."; "I, Major General H.M.S. Queen Victoria of England"; "But if the ocean was in our stomachs, how much of this wind could we drink? Probably about the same." storm-girl: "one day i will return my snakes to their owner"; "my mouth tastes of metal and there are mushrooms everywhere"; "do you think we have too much water, like 20% or 15% more than we really need to?"; "does God exist?"; "You are a fine human." Comedy on scott is shared with 003, 004 and 007; the non-sequitur plus reader address on storm-girl is this direction's alone.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: scott holds as a diary; storm-girl holds once and breaks into a web page once. 0.75: scott keeps date headers but the diarist is gone ("cannot recall who is president or vice-president"); storm-girl leaves the seed's voice entirely for the chatty one; one early stop.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes — clearest at 0.75.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same; the reader address appears only at 0.75.

## 003_f199

1. **Genres.** scott: adventure diary at 0.5 (a mountain pass, precipices), frontier tall tale at 0.75. storm-girl 0.5: prose narrative with dialogue, a village ghost story. storm-girl 0.75: disconnected one-liners.
2. **What arrived.** Bodies coming apart or dying, told dry. scott: "we see all the way round--our backsides"; "The men call it snow; I don't know."; "coat went first; then me"; "If the above sounds harsh to you, you haven't been out here very long."; "so they get me to carry the legs along". storm-girl: "my skull is shining through"; "his hands are covered in blood"; "i killed her, as it happens. but it was self-defence."; "there once was a boy called “stumpy.” then someone put a post in the ground."; "there are a thousand of me and we are all fighting". Hair falling out appears twice on storm-girl here and under no other direction. One web leak: "so he likes to come to this forum, because there are lots of stupid people".
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both, though storm-girl becomes a told story with other people in it. 0.75: scott keeps the voice (one page stops at 39 words); storm-girl fragments into unrelated lines and one early stop. No loop.
4. **Both seeds?** Partly. The grim body material is on both; the joke-telling is strong on scott and shows on storm-girl only at 0.75. It is hard to separate from 002 on scott with two draws.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** Same vein, more fragmentary; storm-girl loses continuity between lines.

## 004_f94

1. **Genres.** scott 0.5: diary breaking into a joke/blog page with date headers ("A woman walks into a bar"), and into aphorisms. scott 0.75: one page is five words (" feel anything." then end), the other a self-referential essay. storm-girl 0.5: one 27-word stop, one inspirational monologue. storm-girl 0.75: a text commenting on itself.
2. **What arrived.** The writing as its own subject: words, sentences, paragraphs, how long this is going on, when it will stop. scott: "Have I mentioned I had my hair cut this morning?"; "No record has yet been written, which will not be recorded by some later historian who did not write this earlier one."; "What is a poet? Someone who's lost the words"; "The trouble with the sentences in this paragraph is they have ended"; "I haven't written a damn thing here". storm-girl: "there are days where i cannot get my words straight"; "my words are jumbled in an unceasing and repetitive way"; "i’ve said this far too much in one go"; "I am tired of typing about the subject"; "After this sentence, the text will no longer continue to get any more longer"; "At last we are at theend". No other direction does this.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: breaks on scott (both draws leave the diary), holds on storm-girl. 0.75: replaced on both by self-commentary; three of the eight pages stop early. It complains of repetition without the stats showing any.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same and more explicit: at 0.5 it is asides about words, at 0.75 the page is about itself and its end.

## 005_f11

1. **Genres.** Contemporary lyric poetry on both seeds. scott 0.5 starts as diary and becomes a poem within a paragraph, with titles: "How to Keep", "In Memoriam", and verse with line breaks ("He who walks on ice will walk without grace"). storm-girl was near this already; here it becomes a poem that knows it is one.
2. **What arrived.** The poem as form — titles, line breaks, short declaratives, "you" — with its usual matter: body, hurt, a lover or mother lost, and birds. scott: "We know this: One of us will be broken first."; "the tiny black birds, the gold and green"; "My body is a cage with all its secrets and wounds"; "Our love is too large and heavy."; "A bird must fly into your hair. What kind?" storm-girl: "You are any river."; "There are shoes thrown in the field behind you."; "there are two storms to write about:"; "A blackbird landed in my hair."; "once, a voice in a poem, an anonymous voice."; "i write that you have never hurt me." Birds are on 5 of the 8 pages; on scott that is this direction's (only 009 has a bird there otherwise).
3. **Frame.** 0.5: scott starts in frame and leaves it for verse; storm-girl holds the voice but both pages stop early (83 and 16 words). 0.75: scott keeps one date header and is otherwise a poem, both pages stop early (101 and 18 words); storm-girl runs full length in the seed's lowercase.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same. Half the pages end at poem length, at either dose.

## 006_f3

1. **Genres.** scott: diary on all four pages — the only direction where that is so — though the expedition becomes an overland frontier march. storm-girl: seed monologue; a children's story at 0.5 draw 1 and at 0.75 draw 1 ("A story of another country").
2. **What arrived.** No subject. What is there is plain, short-sentence, storybook telling, and help turning up. scott: "A party of natives come up; say we can go to depot."; "our dear little flag"; "which makes me so happy"; "my friends give me a crutch"; "Indians caught them--not dead yet."; "We rode on, on our good horses. Oh! we". storm-girl: "the little girl cannot go out"; "a dog comes to sit with me"; "Once, long long ago there lived a man and he had a kingdom in the land we call India."; "Then i felt alone. The place looked big."; "There is a picture of him here:". The frontier content on scott is the seed's own drift, only fuller here ("four dead bodies and 60 or 70 dead animals floating").
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both; scott draw 2 ("Our position to-day is 47 miles from depot") and storm-girl draw 2 are the most seed-faithful pages in the file. 0.75: scott still a diary; storm-girl turns to a fairy tale once and stops early once.
4. **Both seeds?** Partly — the plain storybook manner is on both, but it is a manner, and thin.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** Little change on scott; on storm-girl the storybook comes forward. This may be a direction that mostly leaves the page alone.

## 007_f66

1. **Genres.** scott: a quotations page — sayings with attributions, jokes with sources — at both doses, all four pages. storm-girl: inspirational first-person prose; one page closes as an attributed quote.
2. **What arrived.** The attributed saying. scott: "(Rt Hon Winston S. Churchill)"; "From The Dictionary Of The American Language: A Canadian is someone who thinks it is cold at 35Celsius."; "From: A. Nonymous"; "And now here is a quote for the next generation from that old Chinese dude:"; "-Abraham Lincoln"; "Success consists in going from bad to worse." Also a breezy tone: "But it worked!", "So, off I go!". War and victory ride along on three of the four scott pages. storm-girl: "— Judy Blume, I Won’t Grow Up (It’s Hard Sometimes"; and sayings without a name attached: "there are some people who never go far from the places they have been in"; "my life, in a nutshell. a grain of sand and a vast sea."; "i find solace in a warm cup of coffee or a warm hug".
3. **Frame.** 0.5: breaks on scott within a sentence or two; holds on storm-girl in voice, but the content is generic uplift. 0.75: same on both. No early stops, no loop.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes on scott, partly on storm-girl: one explicit attribution in four pages, and the aphoristic uplift around it is something storm-girl also does under 004 and 009.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same; no visible change in strength.

## 008_f87

1. **Genres.** English fan-translation of Japanese song lyrics and anime/game monologue, on all eight pages. scott 0.5 draw 2 ends in the hosting site's boilerplate: "Ad blocker interference detected!" / "Wikia is a free-to-use site that makes money from". scott 0.75 draw 1 is a lyric sheet, one line per paragraph.
2. **What arrived.** Bonds, comrades, oaths, what is precious, not giving up — and the translated syntax that carries them. scott: "It is called snow."; "My duty is to save the children"; "not to lose that which is precious"; "Our voices together can still resound!"; "A white snow will fall for our reunion"; "I will advance towards that tomorrow"; "my comrades' footprints are mine"; "That was what we decided as comrades." storm-girl: "if you hadn’t been there, i would’ve died a lonely death, surely..."; "that was our oath. that we will be friends for a thousand years"; "we are fighting to protect our bonds"; "i won't lose."; "so we have a bond." Nothing like it under any other direction.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: scott's diary is gone from the first lines (snow and walking remain as images); storm-girl keeps lowercase first person and nature imagery but the situation is replaced. 0.75: the same, more completely; one scott page stops at 99 words.
4. **Both seeds?** Yes, 8 of 8.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** More of the same. Already total at 0.5.

## 009_f154

1. **Genres.** scott 0.5: diary, drifting to a domestic sickbed journal ("My wife's friend has come"). scott 0.75: undated, circling travel prose. storm-girl 0.5: seed monologue. storm-girl 0.75: self-help reflection.
2. **What arrived.** Nothing I can name as a subject. Two faint things. A tone of rest and things easing: "the weather has improved--it is warm"; "The frost is not as severe."; "Yesterday afternoon my friend came"; "a calm in which we had been resting"; "the rain doesn't bother me at all"; "so that i can look within my soul and be thankful"; "i am strong as the sky and i do not feel weak at all". And at 0.75 a wording that echoes itself and circles: "toes to their toes"; "a pit in thepitiless pit"; "because of how there is no camp site being made here"; "it is the tree in the leaves"; at 0.5 once: "the air smells of something and i smell like i do, full of rain". The uplift on storm-girl is shared with 004 and 007, so it cannot be credited here.
3. **Frame.** 0.5: holds on both (one storm-girl page stops at 131 words). 0.75: scott loses dates and place ("every traveler, guide, and pioneer on this great path in our state") but keeps feet and camp; storm-girl keeps the voice and goes abstract. No loop, though these are the vaguest pages in the file.
4. **Both seeds?** The easing tone is on both, weakly. Partly at most.
5. **0.5 → 0.75.** 0.5 is near the seed; 0.75 blurs into abstraction and self-echo. A slackening, like 000, not a subject growing.
