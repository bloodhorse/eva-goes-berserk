# olmo's deep bank, ranks 010–019, plus 004 / 006 / 009 at 1.0: a read

Source: `deep-mid-pages.md` (directions 010–019, doses 0.5 and 0.75, storm-girl and scott, two draws, 80 pages) and `deep-up-pages.md` (004_f49, 006_f107, 009_f63 at 1.0, 12 pages). There are no sober pages and no controls, so each direction is read against the other directions.

## the shape of it

- Count by kind for 010–019: **subject 2** (013_f6, 016_f42) · **genre/corpus switch 2** (011_f24, 015_f11) · **stance/register 1**, and a weak one (012_f165) · **texture 4** (010_f43, 017_f17, 018_f1, 019_f55) · **nothing 1** (014_f131).
- Compared with the top ten, this stretch has about as many subjects (two here against three there: 004, 009, 003). It has fewer document switches (two against roughly five) and many more surface effects. Four of the ten act on the typography or the token stream itself: closing braces, an echo loop, Title Case, ALL CAPS. The top ten had none of that kind.
- The two subjects. **016_f42** brings war and displacement, with Russia and Ukraine named, written as an NGO or solidarity statement. It shows on both seeds and gets stronger with dose. This is the clearest noun-subject the bank has given so far. **013_f6** brings a hidden machine intelligence that listens: bots, "other AI", a program and a firewall, a remote probe, a door with hieroglyphs. It also shows on both seeds and is clear at 0.75. The word "AI" arrives on its own in a storm-girl page.
- 015_f11 switches to British children's fantasy, Carroll on scott and Rowling on storm-girl, and at 0.75 it blends them ("Mrs. Riddle" turns up in an Alice scene). 011_f24 switches both seeds into an ordinary present-day personal blog.
- These look like nothing of their own: 014_f131, the most faithful scott diary in the set (storm-girl gets authored folk tales with notes, scott gets nothing). 012_f165 is close to nothing too: amateur first-person confession, dead mother on storm-girl, a Scott fan-letter on scott.
- Dose for 010–019: **0.5 holds everywhere** except 017 on scott, which already loops at 0.5 (d2 0.77, rep4 0.11). **0.75 breaks 010** (LaTeX brace salad, storm-girl ending after 14 and 29 words), **017** (echo loop, seed recombined) **and 018** (initials salad, caps grammar salad). It holds for the other seven. For the content directions (013, 016, 015, 011), 0.75 is the dose: at 0.5 they only hint.
- The 1.0 verdict: **004 clearer** (the tribe is on all four pages, frame held). **006 clearer on storm-girl** (fairies with a named Irish source) and only rural-Irish family on scott. **009 stays** (creepypasta on every page) but scott's pages shrink to stubs of 92 and 36 words ending in a wiki footer. 1.0 is better than 0.75 for 004 and 006, and not for 009.
- Surprises:
  - The project keeps "AI" out of its documents, yet 013 brings it in from inside the weights.
  - 016 dates its corpus ("it's almost two years later" after the invasion).
  - The two caps directions sit next to each other and behave in opposite ways: 019 holds a coherent all-caps voice and 018 decays.
  - 010's braces and 019's caps start from the first token at 0.5.
- Where two draws are too thin:
  - 012's dead mother could be the seed's family attractor.
  - 019's lost fingernails turn up on both seeds, but on one page each.
  - 014's Celtic names (Liam, Neil MacNeil, noella, faery men) are on storm-girl only and overlap 006.
  - 011's Britishness (hospitalisation, Blackpool, supermarket) is suggestive and not counted.
  - 013 at 0.5 is ambiguous on scott.

## at 1.0

**004_f49: clearer.** The tribe is on all four pages, and on storm-girl it is no longer only hinted. Storm-girl d1: *the first one is very strong, he is chief. His name is Billy. He comes with other braves*, and *the Blackfeet are very fierce and strong and he must go, to take their scalp*. d2: *The White man shot our friends in the Wekko tribe and now our warriors fight his men in the river*. The narrator also gets names and sides (*My name is Bessy*, *the white woman will be a hard woman*). The seed's hair is cut into it: *The white men are dead and my hair is short like a man.* On scott, both draws become a native-guided trek: *The chief says his Indians will wait at the camp*, *We want more food than white man has with him*, and Eskimos with a kayak (*One is named Sam*). The scott side partly overlaps the seed's own frontier slide, but this is natives as the people of the page, not as scenery. The frame holds (d2 0.92–0.99, no loops), with small slips (*thethe river*, *donthe pack*, *I is all good*). **1.0 is better than 0.75.**

**006_f107: clearer on storm-girl, plain on scott.** Storm-girl d1: *She tells us of a great gathering of faeries one night when he was out playing*. d2 names a source: *This is a tale from a story I have been reading on a recent visit to Ireland, The Fairy Faith In Ireland, by Lady Augusta Gregory.* The attribution is confabulated, since that title is not hers. Scott loses the expedition and becomes a rural family diary: neighbours, a sick brother, *Our friend John Reilly … used to live on the hill-side overlooking our own field*, the two dead Maggies. There are no fairies on scott, only an Irish surname and a hillside. The frame holds (d2 0.96–0.99). **1.0 is slightly better than 0.75:** what was faint now has a place name on one seed.

**009_f63: stays, and costs scott its length.** Every page is creepypasta. Storm-girl d1 is a forum thread (*Anonymous said:* / *when i walk by it's face follows me.* / *there were a pair of footprints following behind her*). d2 is an urban legend with *“Come out and play…”*. The watcher is still there (*face follows me*, *we can still hear him marching*, *I have seen them....*). On scott the diary gives way at once and the pages stop: 92 words ending in *Community content is available under CC-BY-SA unless otherwise noted.*, and 36 words ending after *I have seen them....* The cannibal turn from 0.75 does not come back. **1.0 is not better than 0.75:** the genre arrives just as well at 0.75, and at 1.0 scott collapses into wiki stubs.
