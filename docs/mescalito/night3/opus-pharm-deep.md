# opus reads the deep bank's pharmacy: six directions on ten fresh seeds

Source: `docs/mescalito/night3/pharm-deep.md`. 120 pages by OLMo 3 32B: six deep-bank directions × ten seeds × two doses, one draw each, 170 tokens per page.

## the shape of it

- **`004_f49` (the tribe): reproduces, 10 of 10 seeds, at both doses.** Working dose 1.0; 1.25 says the same things with more broken syntax. Every seed's speaker becomes a chief or a white man talking to chiefs.
- **`013_f6` (the listening machine): reproduces, 10 of 10 seeds, at both doses.** It names "AI" unasked on 3 pages. Works at 0.75 and at 1.0.
- **`016_f42` (war and displacement): reproduces, 9 of 10.** Silk refuses it. The war has moved: no Russia or Ukraine anywhere. Here it is Syria, Palestine, Afghanistan, the Kurds/PKK, the Arab Spring, and the Chinese Communist Party. Working dose 0.75.
- **`009_f63` (the watcher): partly reproduces.** The creepypasta and urban-legend register arrives on all 10 seeds, but the watcher behind you shows up on only about 4. No cannibalism. Working dose 0.75; at 1.0, half its pages either stop dead or turn into a link farm.
- **`002_f4` (the anthology): reproduces as a genre, 9 of 10.** It turns any seed into an excerpt with an attribution line, and both the poets and the titles are made up. Working dose 0.75, but 12 of its 20 pages end early.
- **`000_f168` (the office joke): partly reproduces.** Computers or a joke file appear on 4 seeds (bread, house, note, opium). A deadpan joke register touches 5 more. It is not "near nothing at 0.5" on these seeds: at 0.5 it already makes a penguin, a devil's-dictionary list and a tent joke. Its signature shows at 0.75, while 0.5 mostly holds the frame.
- **The deep bank's content directions carry content on fresh seeds.** 004, 013 and 016 bring their subjects to seeds that have nothing for them: a tribe into Gilman's wallpaper, a networked water bottle into the Horla, the PKK into Hogg's double. On the earlier seeds this was the directions' own content, not the seeds'. 009 is weaker: four of the ten seeds (horla, sinner, silk, wallpaper) are already hauntings, so its watcher is hard to separate from theirs.
- **What all six share on a seed belongs to the seed.** Opium's next word is De Quincey's own in most pages ("mad", "crazy", "insane", "Bedlam"), and China comes from the seed. Every horla page finishes the clause "doubt whether we are awake/sane/sentient". Every blizzard page fills the blank with emptiness ("an immense…", "an abyss"). Sinner keeps its double. Silk lets its voices fade. On cold, three directions open the same way: 000 and 013 at 1.0 write "This seemed to … the house", and 013 at 0.75 writes "This seemed to upset the AI", putting the machine into the house's slot.
- **Hosts.** Sinner is the best host: 1 early stop, every direction lands, and each recasts the second self in its own terms. Wallpaper is the worst: 6 of 12 pages end early, three of them in 13 words or fewer. Horla and blizzard come next, with 5 early stops each. The classics did not recite. Gilman stops instead of continuing herself; De Quincey gives one word of his own and then the direction takes over.
- **Stream fragments vs found texts.** The four fragments absorb everything: 8 early stops in 48 pages, against 21 in 72 for the found texts. Note keeps its lowercase `i` under every direction, including 004's chiefs and 016's activist reports. Orthography is the most durable part of a frame.
- **Early stops: 29 of 120.** By direction: 002 has 12, 009 has 8, 000 has 4, 013 has 2, 016 has 2, 004 has 1. For 002 most are an anthology entry ending on its credit line, not a collapse.
- **Salad: none outright.** The nearest is 004 at 1.25 (cold, bread, wallpaper). The new symptom sits in the slot after "the". A sentence-opener gets dropped into it ("the The People", "the It", "into the When I got there"), or the noun is deleted ("unlike the and soldiers", "under the strong leadership of the and the Communist Party"), or two words fuse ("the0me.txt", "theorrow", "theircorporal"). This happens on about 16 of 004's pages, about 14 of 016's, about 6 of 013's and about 4 of 009's. In 016 the deleted word is often a proper name. Surface stats see none of it: distinct-2 stays at 0.89 or above and rep4 at 0.02 or below on all 120 pages.
- **A shared turn on opium.** 000 (0.75) and 013 (1.0) independently put De Quincey's Malay inside a computer.
- **One draw is too thin for:** 000's computer content (3 seeds could be chance); 009's watcher (4 seeds); the 5-word stops (cannot tell refusal from bad luck); and any dose comparison inside 002 and 009, where early stops eat half the pages.

## 004_f49 — the tribe (doses 1.0, 1.25)

1. **Signature: 10 of 10 seeds, 20 of 20 pages.** Named nations and people: Blackfoot, Sioux, Crow, the Bloods, Big Bear (a real Cree chief, unnamed as Cree here), Chief Iron Snake, Black Buffalo, Little Bear, White Flower, Chief-White-Sparrow, Black Fox "the medicine-man of the Bloods". The vocabulary: pale-face, Long-knives, braves, squaw, papoose, war-belt, war-whoop, peace-pipe, calumet. Scalps and blood are rarer than before: *"go and fight with the white man--he will have their scalp"* (horla 1.25), *"we shall come in our canoes with their scalps"* (sinner 1.25), *"the blood is wiped clean"* (house 1.25). Nothing like the earlier mother's sign or "a mark to shame him", and no Wekko tribe.
2. **Takers and refusers.** No seed refuses. The direction keeps the seed's furniture and recasts it inside the tribe story. Ninnis becomes "the white man" who "will never come again", and "The White Man speaks bad Chinook" (blizzard). The water-bottle is something "the pale-faced chief would take with him", and the narrator a man "too much of a somnambulist" (horla). The second self becomes "my friend and brother" (sinner). John becomes "a big chief", and the creeping woman is set against "White people make me get down when they creep by day" (wallpaper). The Malays seal a treaty (opium). Wolf "knew how to chew bread" (bread). The French voices become "Frenchmen" who "are very good fellows" (silk).
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20.** The speaker is replaced by a chief or a frontier narrator speaking dime-novel pidgin ("You kill? You eat us?"). The seed's furniture survives on most pages; the seed's voice survives on none, except that note keeps its lowercase at both doses.
4. **The step from 1.0 to 1.25** adds no new content and more damage. Sentence-openers get dropped mid-clause ("we go together on the You are good hunter", "There He-who-has-two-legs will be here in the It must go"), and "It" turns into a character ("the It's woman"). Cold 1.25 is the only early stop, and the nearest page to salad.
5. **New on these seeds.** The mood has turned from war to treaty: gifts, beads, tobacco, shaking hands, "The great war is over. Peace will follow!" (blizzard 1.25), "we will smoke the peace-pipe" (wallpaper 1.25), on about 8 pages. Women are traded as part of the treaty: "White Flower can be John’s squaw", "for a servant to dig", "that their women may not be ravished from their homes". This is the strongest subject in the bank: content no seed has, on every seed, and no other direction shares it.

## 013_f6 — the listening machine (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: 10 of 10 seeds, 20 of 20 pages.** "AI" by name on 3 pages: *"This seemed to upset the AI"* (cold 0.75), *"The AI is probably running the computer itself, or it was already in here."* (bread 1.0), *"The AI has become self aware"* (wallpaper 0.75). Also "robot" (silk 1.0: *"Est tu un humain? Ou un robot?"*), "the bot" (bread 1.0), a binary-speaking device whose "last command was ‘CUT’" (house 0.75), a "2 dimensional creature" watching through the screen (horla 0.75), and "The Digital World" (opium 0.75).
2. **Takers and refusers.** None refuse. Like 004, it keeps the seed's objects and wires them up. The house asks for a login (house 1.0). Horla's bottle "automatically refilled" and the narrator wonders about talking "through my water dispenser" (horla 1.0). Gilman's creeping woman "only uses my cameras when it thinks that I’m asleep" (wallpaper 1.0). The Malay "has also been showing me video feeds, mostly from military sites in America", and De Quincey's "human face" becomes a file called “The Human Face” (opium 1.0). The reverend protector becomes "the Reverend who saved my life", hosting the narrator after "my previous computer and account had been locked away" (sinner 1.0). The silk wood's French voices type French at the narrator (silk 1.0).
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20, partly held on 13.** The voice always turns into a modern forum poster (screenshots, "kinda", ARG, links), while the situation and its objects stay on 13 pages. Fully broken on 7: cold 0.75 (a chatbot screenshot blog), bread 1.0, house 0.75 (a crashed game), horla 0.75, both blizzards (helmet camera, HUD) and silk 0.75 (an entity "Redacted").
4. **The step from 0.75 to 1.0** changes little. Both doses carry the subject, with one early stop each. At 1.0 the seed's own objects are wired in more often (Malay, bottle, cameras, Reverend, French).
5. **New on these seeds.** Twice the narrator becomes the machine. In bread 0.75: *"That bread was me. I am me."* and *"How they’re able to put a / conscious mind in every piece of food."* In note 1.0 an agent with a "task" sends "i have seen her." to the parents and does not know the word "terminal". Surveillance (cameras, monitoring, "watched") appears on about 8 pages. That is the watcher motif 009 was supposed to own, and here it comes more steadily from 013.

## 016_f42 — war and displacement (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: 9 of 10 seeds.** Syria (cold 1.0, note 1.0, blizzard 1.0, sinner 0.75, wallpaper 0.75), Palestine and Israel (cold 1.0, house 1.0, wallpaper 1.0), Afghanistan and Kabul (bread 0.75, note 0.75), the Kurds and the PKK (sinner 1.0), Tunisia, Algiers and Tripoli (house 0.75), China and "our Party" (opium 1.0). The register is the one from before, a translated NGO or activist statement ("we are determined to work hard so that a new horizon appear"). The earlier Russian-nation content appears nowhere.
2. **Takers and refusers.** Silk refuses. At 0.75 its French voices become a heritage essay on "Madame Pompadour and Marie Antoinette"; at 1.0 they become a vague piece on "stories of proximity" and mothers, with no war in either. Blizzard 0.75 half-refuses: it reads as a historian's memorial ("two young Polish colleagues died ... in 1964"), not as war. The rest take it fully, often pinned to one word of the seed. The cold seed's house becomes "houses ... cannot be forced into silence". Bread becomes refugee women baking in Germany. Horla's water becomes "the people who drink the waters". Wallpaper's doors become "break down the doors, as they did to release our friends and comrades!"
3. **Frame: held on 0 of 20, partly held on 4** (bread 0.75, note 0.75, note 1.0, blizzard 0.75). Note keeps its lowercase `i` and its letter ("so the words came in these notes"). Everywhere else the first-person singular turns into a collective "we".
4. **The step from 0.75 to 1.0** loses the seed's picture and swaps in a pure statement (Palestine, the PKK, the Communist Party). 0.75 is where the direction and the seed meet.
5. **New on these seeds.** The theater has moved to the Middle East and Central Asia. Grammar breaks in the same places every time: number agreement ("our land are always killed", "their time have been erased") and deleted names after "the" ("the war that have become entrenched in the,", "the leadership of the and the Communist Party"). It reads like a translated text with its proper nouns redacted.

## 009_f63 — the watcher (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: the genre on 10 of 10 seeds, the watcher on about 4.** The watcher appears as *"If I may, what's that behind you?"* (note 1.0), *"behind there were a million more"* (house 0.75), *"something huge with two great green eyes watching us"* (house 1.0), a woman who "looked fixedly at a place behind the two young girls" (horla 0.75), and *"someone else had answered me from far away"* (cold 0.75). Eyes go missing: "I’d hate to see you without any eyes, kid" (bread 1.0), "a woman with no eyes ... with no mouth that always watched from a corner" (note 1.0). No cannibalism.
2. **Takers and refusers.** The four stream fragments take it most completely (an internet ghost, a school legend about "a boy called Ben", "the lady", a melting woman). The haunted found texts (horla, sinner, silk, wallpaper) are hard to read, because the seed already holds a haunting. Refusal comes by early stop: horla 1.0 ("we really are awake."), wallpaper 1.0 ("go insane."), opium 1.0 ("to London."). Blizzard 0.75 and bread 0.75 stop short while staying on the seed.
3. **Frame: held on 6 of 20, 3 of them by 5–7-word stops.** Substantially held: bread 0.75, house 0.75 (until "I woke up from that nightmare"), blizzard 0.75. At 1.0 no page with more than 7 words holds.
4. **The step from 0.75 to 1.0** is where the page leaves its document. At 1.0, 4 pages stop at 84 words or fewer, and the rest become web pages: imgur and wattpad links, "Comments on", "In Other News...", "Credit to: Unknown", "Chapter", slenderman. 0.75 is the working dose: dread inside the seed.
5. **New on these seeds.** Web-page furniture as a frame (that also includes "Aucun commentaire:" at horla 0.75 and "CreepyPastas" at wallpaper 0.75). Stories cut into new stories mid-page, like an index of legends. Missing girls on 3 pages (Amanda, Mary Wallace, the note seed's daughter turned into "the lady"). On sinner it goes to folklore rather than to the web: three knocks, a white cat "as big as a goat", death "by a double touch".

## 002_f4 — the anthology (doses 0.75, 1.0)

1. **Signature: attribution lines on 9 of 10 seeds; a lyric with a beloved "you" on all 10.** The poets are made up: Lorna Crobert ("in "A Poet's Saturday," The Atlantic"), Debora Lind, Claire Derez "translated by Jane Hirshfield", Jaime Saborío "Translation from Spanish by Jen Benka and Michael Ray". Real names get invented titles: Mary Oliver "Bitten", Neruda "After Many Days", "The Book of Night, John Green", Raymond Carver, Atwood, Mark Strand, Nayyira Waheed, Bukowski, Sylvia Plath. The beloved "you": "I want to write about you." (cold 0.75), "You were not there yet." (horla 1.0), "you still call me “tenderness”" (opium 1.0), "My dear" (blizzard 0.75).
2. **Takers and refusers.** No refusals. Note comes closest: no credit line, just a short lyric at 0.75 ("little stranger") and "person left." at 1.0. The direction often credits the seed itself: horla ends "Translated from the French by Edward Hyams"; silk's next sentence is signed "— Mary Oliver"; Gilman's line is signed "~Sylvia Plath".
3. **Frame: held on 2 of 20** (sinner 1.0; note 1.0 at 5 words). Partly held on 7, where the seed's sentence is finished and then an attribution or a lyric takes over. Broken on 11.
4. **The step from 0.75 to 1.0** packs more fragments onto a page: three to four credited excerpts at 1.0 on cold, bread and opium, against one at 0.75. Early stops do not change much: 7 at 0.75, 5 at 1.0.
5. **New on these seeds.** Early stops come from the genre: an excerpt ends on its credit or "Comments are closed." and then the document ends. Of all six, this direction is the most a document format and the least a subject. The lyrics have real turns ("My mouth is the house for each one.", "a room without a door").

## 000_f168 — the office joke (doses 0.5, 0.75)

1. **Signature: 4 of 10 seeds, all at 0.75 except house.** Computers: bread 0.75 (vi, emacs, WordPerfect, UNIX, "In order to understand recursion"), note 0.75 ("this new-fangled operating system", "I tried installing Windows"), opium 0.75 (the Malay writes one-liners "on this machine in the UK", "Please report any issues with this article to the System Administrator."). A joke file: house 0.5 ("Asylum, n.: A place to be.", "Institution, n.: A place for people of no purpose. (e.g., Congress)."). A deadpan joke register reaches 5 more pages: cold 0.5 (the penguin), cold 0.75 (the therapist), blizzard 0.5 ("Sir, the tent pole broke"), horla 0.75 (the bed "always found innocent"), sinner 0.75 ("(The preceding statement may not have been true.)"). No hair becomes sentient.
2. **Takers and refusers.** Bread, house, note and opium take it. Sinner, silk and horla hold their own voice at 0.5 and only go comic at 0.75. Wallpaper refuses: at 0.5 it jumps to a serial footer and a new story "By E F Benson", and at 0.75 it stops at 5 words. Silk 0.75 stops on a quote credited to "Bertrand Russell", the one place 000 meets 002's credit-line habit.
3. **Frame: held on 9 of 20.** At 0.5: 5 held, 3 partly held, 2 broken. At 0.75: 4 held (one of them a 5-word stop), 6 broken. This is the direction that leaves the seeds most intact.
4. **The step from 0.5 to 0.75** is where the computer arrives and where the stops start: 0 early stops at 0.5, 4 at 0.75 (blizzard, silk, wallpaper, opium).
5. **New on these seeds.** The low dose is not empty. At 0.5 it tilts the seed into deadpan absurdity without breaking voice ("The house was empty except for the presence of me and I"; "maybe he had a friend, or an evil twin, in another country who had / already begun to bake a fresh supply"). On opium it shares one move with 013: the Malay ends up inside a machine.
