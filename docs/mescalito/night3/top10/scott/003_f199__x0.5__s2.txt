When you go down here in this weather you must hold your face out in front of you like a dog or your tongue will stick to the ground.

The snow is up to your neck--when you sink you die. This morning we woke to a terrific explosion and found ourselves covered with a lot of stuff. We crawled out from underneath it and it looked like cotton wool. It is about three inches thick. The men call it snow; I don't know. It would take an elephant to walk over this lot, and I haven't seen any. This is the worst place on earth for anything to happen.
