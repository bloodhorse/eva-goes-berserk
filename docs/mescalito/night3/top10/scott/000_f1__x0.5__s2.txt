This morning the thermometer stood at zero and it is going on this side of the sun--wind blowing, temperature is below -25° and a cloud has blown off--so, a heavy wind; snowstorm and ice rain will probably follow, as it usually does here.

Saturday, March 18.--It has been snowing and it was snowing pretty steadily yesterday evening, with snow falling from 8 a.m. till about 5.30 p.m., and a snowfall of about three inches fell between the two posts.
