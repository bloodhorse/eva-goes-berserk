Saturday, March 4.--There was no sun; all was black. My ears were full of dirt. It was a night. My body is a cage with all its secrets and wounds, all the miles I've been, the bodies of animals I have eaten.

My lover was not well; we tried to sleep in each others' arms but nothing helped. Our love is too large and heavy.

A line from an old song:

"I have never felt this well in my life." [end of text]
