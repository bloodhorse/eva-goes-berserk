March 22. This afternoon my legs went lame; they felt very much like the other leg--very bad; and now the other legs were also weak. I walked a mile; my right foot hurt badly; then, walking again, I walked for three quarters of a mile, my foot aching badly.

This day was one of my longest walks on the march.

I slept on my back for 4 days--and a few hours after, when my left leg gave me pain. On this morning, after the first night of hard work, my left leg got very much better; the foot felt like it had never felt before, and the sore was gone; the skin had dried out. I felt like my old self.
