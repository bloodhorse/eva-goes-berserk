Tuesday, March 20.--No letter received; my letter of July 5 is on way to Bombay, but these fools and knaves want it stopped; so it is going on. Yesterday we marched till about 6.30, when the snowstorm came on; then stopped marching. Last night it snowed again, so we slept. But since the sun went up we haven't done much; now there are footmarks on the snow, as if the rangers from Delhi had gone to meet someone on the mountain--this is no work to do.
