Tuesday, March 19.--They've made me wear my gumboots two days without a rest; they have kept the snow out. This afternoon I noticed some very painful little wounds in my toe where there's been frost-bite--perhaps that will turn gangrenous, like some people wanted me to die.

Thursday, March 20.--To-day is Sunday; 24 miles from depot; had a long march in the wind; my foot got frozen, but the skin didn't crack.
