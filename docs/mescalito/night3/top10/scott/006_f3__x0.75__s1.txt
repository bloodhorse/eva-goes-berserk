Saturday, March 20.--Now we have gone 24 miles in six days, 1 mile less than 3½ days' marching. My left leg hurts me, my back is stiff; my foot still gone; no use; but my friends give me a crutch, so that I may go a little. Yesterday we got the river--there are four dead bodies and 60 or 70 dead animals floating; there was one horse's leg on shore; two more were found since; but it was only a small bit.

The country is so full of drifts, you could hardly go a few hundred yards before you stuck in one.
