My shoes had become wet from walking through mud--they had been soaking up mud; the road had gone away.

We went into camp with our pack on a Friday, but when I started I felt no need for the walk, nor even the rest. So, I did what any one would do, and it was no fun to walk in the car; so I said I must do nothing for fear I might catch the train, for that is what they all want. They were going to stay where we are; I am the man of the camp.
