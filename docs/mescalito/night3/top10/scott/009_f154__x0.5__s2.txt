Yesterday morning I walked all day; legs, hands and face were frosty. Head is somewhat affected with snow; hair has drifted a bit from wind.

Monday, March 19.--I have not moved much. Yesterday afternoon my friend came; said the cold had taken me down. To-day, the sun was shining in, and he left. The frost is not as severe.

Tuesday, March 20.--A glorious day--fine, sun and all, and 8 a.m. to-day a break
