At noon to-day the sledge has stuck, so have all our legs, we cannot walk any farther; they will be carried in the next time the wind rises; our poor dog, his end has come.

I cannot eat the frozen meat--too cold--we eat some chocolate--I feel myself a poor fellow to-night--it seems that my sufferings must cease; they can't possibly be increased; there must be something left--surely my lot will change.
