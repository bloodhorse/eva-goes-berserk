Tuesday, March 21.--Worst thing to have done; all day I had not seen our own fort. Our guides showed me it this evening--far, far away in a deep hole--very little smoke. There are more of those little guns around than you think--we could hardly count them.

The horses that used to drag the gun were gone--Indians caught them--not dead yet.

A friend brought some fresh food to eat.
