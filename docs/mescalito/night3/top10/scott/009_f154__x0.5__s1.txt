Sunday, March 19.--This has been the first day we've rested for four or five weeks; the weather has improved--it is warm. It was 8:30 A.M., before I felt the first of a dozen or more of a man's worst feelings; then a sharp pain, that is followed by a dull one--that is followed by an acute pain in my head.

My left eye has given up, it is cold, but there are still traces of light; a few moments after a slight headache and my right hand are as hot as ever. My wife's friend has come; it was cold, and the last I saw was a cold one.
