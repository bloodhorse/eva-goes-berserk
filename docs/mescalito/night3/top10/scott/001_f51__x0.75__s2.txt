. When we started for the 21-mile camp we had the sun behind us for most of the march--on the way we were subjected to wind--we reached camp when I could have gone farther and been alone. It seems we had no business on foot as well. There were four young men from our neighbourhood at work with wind at back--when we went to get a meal I had no business in that region at all. They followed us back--one had the effrontery to tell me, "we are doing this so you don't go anywhere with a woman"--a thought they would be sure of having to be in for.

Thursday, March 20.--The last night's restlessness continued all through the day--there is a definite effort by someone to put pressure on the back of the neck when I sit in my chair
