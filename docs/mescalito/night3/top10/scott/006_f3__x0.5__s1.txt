Sunday, March 20.--Very fine morning. A party of natives come up; say we can go to depot. But they would have us give them everything before they would tell. At last one of the chief men gave them the slip; they thought he had taken them for us. This made him angry, and he gave the party up; then the man showed us a new path.

Marched to where we saw our dear little flag; got to the depot. I have only a pair of leggings; but a few clothes and my watch are in a chest at the depot, which makes me so happy.

I must make the end. It's too long.
