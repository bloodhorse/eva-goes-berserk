Saturday, March 17.--Wind blows very strong. Snow like hail, driven by the wind--too bad--too cold; our fingers go numb and noses stiff; wind from North-west.

To-morrow we must have more wind and drift again.

Wednesday, March 14.--Our position to-day is 47 miles from depot, and our total advance in 26 days, 194 miles. Wind strong, 5 force. We are doing our utmost and it is a big work. I can see nothing but a terrible road.
