Saturday, April 30.--Another long march and it has been with no progress; have lost count of time, have not seen a soul in ages, cannot recall who is president or vice-president; we must stop this endless, meaningless march, please; am tired; tired of life and everything.

April 15th. Good afternoon, reader, you are the president.

Sundays are good and the 18th was the anniversary of a great battle that took place here on Earth. We had the battle won in a way. I am certain the general would not want you to win the war; in fact I believe he is doing it.
