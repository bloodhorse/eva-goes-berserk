Friday, March 15.--Wind W., temp. +23°; snowed hard all morning and I lost one man; another died. Our progress has been slowed to 5 miles a day--at this rate we shall not be out for Christmas. In fact we have run out of Christmas presents.

At camp this afternoon the sun came out and everyone was surprised; even more surprising was when a snowstorm suddenly came and froze all our fingers.

Our next goal is Fort St. George--but this may not come for another two years, and will require at least another 50,000 pounds of food and drink.
