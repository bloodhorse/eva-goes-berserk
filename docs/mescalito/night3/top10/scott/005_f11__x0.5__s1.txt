Sunday, March 19.--It was the same for each one: We went and did not return. A mile in from the sea ice, we turned left toward a stand of poplar and tamarack and a red river winding south. A dog found us first and brought us to its people.

For 30 years we have lived alone and far, so long that it is possible that when you look into your heart and see the tiny black birds, the gold and green, it will be the same for each of us, this tiny miracle: It was always so and will always be again.

How to Keep

We know it will not be forever.

We know this: One of us will be broken first.
