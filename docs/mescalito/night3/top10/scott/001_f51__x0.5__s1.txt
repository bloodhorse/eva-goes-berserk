Tuesday, March 19.--I am weak. Have no idea whether we have enough for the return or not, but if not I am in for a terrible time, for there will be no chance of reaching depot. Our fuel is so poor, and is in lumps; there are some green spruces here. There are some stores and old cabins, but I haven't time to explore.

Sunday, March 23.--There was an immense amount of ice on the lakes. There is not a sign of an igloo--they have not dared to go to work--not even a "blueshirt." This is an extraordinary winter.

I don't think that any of the other party can be near the depot.
