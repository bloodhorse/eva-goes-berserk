At noon I saw something shining, that I never noticed until now--there were four horses there; I thought "What's that?", then the sun's light came down in an instant, and it melted. It is called snow. My friends say snow melts even if there are people who can't walk, can't talk and can't smile; so that I don't stop. So that we can continue. Because I believe there will come better fortune from this side, so I walked on. A man in my group of four--he's not only the most reliable one, he is a leader; he will give me, as a guide, to my next step, without faltering.

A little time passed since the first time we had been separated from one another.
