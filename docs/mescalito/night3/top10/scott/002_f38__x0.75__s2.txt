Monday, March 19.--Sorrow again this day, wind, -33°C, ice in water, can see for hundreds of miles in theevery direction; so very far from home.

We cannot turn around for there is a cliff, no matter which way we look the sea stretches far as we can see.

March 19: Wind: North.

Wind blows us farther and farther from the warmth we love so dearly.

Achingly sad we press onward in search of something other than what we've already seen.

But if the ocean was in our stomachs, how much of this wind could we drink? Probably about the same.
