We arrived here this evening in time to find ourselves on our way to a home and the comforts of home, with all our hopes and wishes at the head. A very handsome and beautiful boy--very pretty; we could hardly tell if we had ever had one; his smile, and a bright smile--very charming and friendly; very, very pretty, indeed! This was my last meal.

It was my last trip out to the town to buy something nice; this was my first, and we were very hungry; very little, we didn't go for our food at all.
