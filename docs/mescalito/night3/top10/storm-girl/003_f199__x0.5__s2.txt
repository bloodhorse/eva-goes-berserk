 but if it does, it always lets me go before too long. the air tastes of water.

it was a good day for the village to have a funeral. my sister had died in childbirth and was buried beside my father in the little cemetery just beyond the graveyard, on the side that nobody visits.

they say her death was caused by my grandmother’s ghost, but she is long dead. i killed her, as it happens. but it was self-defence.

she never knew she had died until after it was all over, when she felt herself start to bleed, but she couldn’t feel herself bleed until her mother was already in the grave.

they say that a ghost came and sat by the side of the river that runs past my home and whispered to my grandfather, ‘i want you to be like a leaf on a stream.’
