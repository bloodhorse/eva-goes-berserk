 a storm, perhaps.

there was once a day that had nothing but fog.

we could hear each other breathing. the ocean roared in our ears.

in this world, I was once a ghost in a black dress, but in reality I am a woman standing on the dock.

there are two storms to write about:

one: in the forest. The sun was a tree in the rain, a white sail on a gray lake, but I saw a bear come down out of a tree, covered in pine needles. A blackbird landed in my hair.

the second storm: a series of sounds. An old man weeps in a boat and he does not look away, but into the sun. His tears land on his face. A tree is cut down in a nearby forest, the ground rips open with yellow flowers and rain.
