 when the tide comes in, i can smell it on my clothes, which are often filthy.

there is a sign on my chest saying “push”. it’s still asking what direction, and the arrow just points left, or down the path. or through the hedge, if we were out walking and a stranger walked up and asked what my name was.

the answer, apparently, was “jake.” when they asked him what he thought about the universe, he told me.

if i was on top of the hill, i would fall down the mountain.

there once was a boy called “stumpy.” then someone put a post in the ground.

once we were in bed, and when the light came through the window we could hear the people downstairs complaining.

there are a thousand of me and we are all fighting. only i have my hands
