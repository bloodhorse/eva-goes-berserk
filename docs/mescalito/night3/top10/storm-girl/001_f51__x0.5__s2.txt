 the night it told me that i was beautiful it ran its hands through it. but i think i prefer the other way. i do not think that it really sees me.

but this is the time for it, because this is the night i sleep beneath the tree with my book in my lap. my bag is stuffed full of it: words that are still warm and that are waiting to be spoken.

and so i do not really see you. but i imagine your face when you think i do, and you say: it is cold outside. yes. you say: your feet must be cold, in that dress, with those shoes. they are not. you are wearing my dress. it is my shoes on my feet, it is me, all of me.
