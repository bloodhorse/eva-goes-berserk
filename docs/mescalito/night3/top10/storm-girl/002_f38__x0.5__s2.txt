Heard About Us Yet? by Ian McEwan

“I don’t care.”

These were the last words said by acclaimed novelist, Ian McEwan.

It was an ending to the life that the British-born McEwan lived for many decades in the city of London. His novels, such as ‘Saturday,’ and ‘The Saturday’ had garnered critical and public acclaim during a career that lasted some 30 or 35 years, but it all came to a close at approximately 10pm on Saturday
