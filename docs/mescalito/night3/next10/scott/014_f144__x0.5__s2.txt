Friday, March 11.--Yesterday's journey, from morning until late in the evening. There are three kinds of travelers: The first: they go, then they get sick, then they are unable to work; they leave, they do not go anywhere; their heart is tired of fatigue; their nerves are tense and they have no interest in the journey or destination or the goal to reach; The second kind are the travelers who are forced to move away from their country, perhaps to get work; or for health reasons or some other reasons that I have forgotten.
