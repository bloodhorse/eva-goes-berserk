Today's ration is half a tin of beef stewed in red wine; this is for supper; my mind has turned to meat; we will never see good meat again, ever; our fate is meat, meat, meat, and more meat. We're doing everything we can; this isn't easy for us either, believe me; our feet hurt so much; this has been an exhausting expedition for everybody; our souls hurt so bad.
