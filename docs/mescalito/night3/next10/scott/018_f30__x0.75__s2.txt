The Army will go if it can, and do duty for His Majesty, so it might happen.

April 2, 1942--at a halt near Amman (Iraq's capital); artillery units have a chance at active action and artillery observers have assisted aiming the guns accurately, while others arrange ammunition at an airfield or area ahead; about another April they may attack Amman again.

I hope we shall arrive ahead, all able arms and air support arranged.
