This morning I looked down at my foot; there were blisters and blood on both big toe and second toe of the left foot; on right, two of five toes had blisters.

I told no one but it is now apparent my days on the earth are numbered, for when my foot fell off and was lying there I looked at it and realized how much more of my own body could be shed from my feet and that it could all fall off.
