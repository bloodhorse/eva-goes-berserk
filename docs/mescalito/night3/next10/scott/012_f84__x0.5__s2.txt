Saturday, March 24.--Wind died out during night--just before midnight we had to halt. We went to the windward, but there is not enough drift for them. Now we have the drift again--to-night, at 10 o'clock, we halted again--temp. -45°--the sledge stuck in a crevice--had to dig it out with the pick; I got to windward, but not my dogs, and my hands have frozen.
