The blizzard came with full power to-night--no use starting for the depot--we have to face it here and rest. Our dog team are quite snow blind--scurvy has seized us; not much of the spirit is left to struggle; no chance for getting off before Sunday--blizzard has raged 40 hours--only four blankets, no fuel, but snow to burn and it gives off little heat; blankets must be worn by night and by day for warmth, as no fire is allowed while sleeping--too great danger from the burning tents and getting burned--it has been tried; 3d night without sleep--our nerves are on a terrible tension.
