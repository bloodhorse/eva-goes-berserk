In the morning it felt a bit warmer; wind stopped for a bit. The day after the expedition went home--without me. But we found new ways of staying connected--in spite of distance--to share stories and questions about what has been and what may still come.

Saturday, March 16.--How long does the ice sheet keep up this temperature? And when will we know for certain that this ice will disappear forever? Are the 100 million inhabitants of this land entitled to a right to exist?
