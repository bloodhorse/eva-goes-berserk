Saturday, March 17.--Our old friend wind has turned N.W.; snow and fog so bad I had to put on goggles to march--as soon as it's daylight enough. We can see but 50 ft., so march blindly with a good compass course; our party has dwindled from 12 to 4 men and one sledge dog.
