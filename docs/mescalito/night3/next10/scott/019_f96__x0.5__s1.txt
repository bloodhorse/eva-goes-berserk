This day is one to remember: the most dreary we have had so far.

The weather, although warmish to-day, has not been good--the ground, though not bare, has not much cover; we were up before seven this morning--got through the mud-banks of the Murray, and proceeded at an average speed of from ten to fifteen miles a-day till yesterday.

We camped near a fine spring, where we were detained a short time. This afternoon, on the banks of a noble stream, I came up to the depot, having come three hundred and eighty miles in twelve days, which, considering the bad weather, and our little stock, is pretty fair progress
