Monday, March 19.--Marching in agony; no sleep since March 14th, now have 22 hours in total, must leave sled; if you love yourself and your dog you must give him 40 days. He deserves it. His coat has thickened.

Marching like an ox; snow hard. Cold wind behind me; a cold front is approaching.

Tuesday, March 20.--Day of sorrow and wind; all dogs down for count.

I'm thinking, where's the "north?" The south? The west?
