I can scarcely move on snow shoes and will not try without. Had to go down on hands and knees; and crawled on my way up to our old camp; and in that short space lost four toe nails; one almost all the way off; they just rotted away.

If you saw them you'd laugh to split, but you can't, so here is the best picture of my toes.

"Come," says I to the gang, "here is an opportunity for some fun. Here is an Indian who has a white man's hand; if any one is willing I will chop off this one at the25the joint for a relic."
