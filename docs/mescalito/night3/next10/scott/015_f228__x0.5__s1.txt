--felt I ought to stop; we halted--we've stopped here, a short way off.

Our sled-dogs were sent ahead in relays and our dog-sleds left at 50 miles a day--a little over half the speed they'd managed to pull so far.

It is very quiet; very silent, too quiet.

Sunday, March 25.--Our party was down to eight last night--only myself left, I have nothing. It has been very quiet--the wind blew from the west all day. [end of text]
