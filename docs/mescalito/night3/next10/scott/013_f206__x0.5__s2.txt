On a horse with one leg in the frost-bitten and torn boot, in pain, suffering from indigestion--all that could only lead to a disaster. There's still some of it; and today my boots, like shoes, just fall apart, but I can not leave them.

But now my friend's health deteriorates and my spirits. To think about tomorrow! How can you fight? And this is only the fourth day.
