March 19th.--Still suffering. Had some difficulty in starting on account of snow and the fact that we were only four. It was rather early and very dark when I went outside. Snow falling; very thick mist on, with drift.

March 21.--Threw out my tooth this morning--to my grief I found a hole in it; a crack on the inside.

March 23.--Started this morning to march towards our old ground. Still raining.

March 26th.--Arrived at our depot this evening after marching and starving for nine days.

This is an account of my wanderings, written to pass away time and while away sadness. I don't care for fame and am no author of great name, nor can I boast a great amount of brains. But I do care for my fellow-men.
