My breakfast consisted of bread with water--my stomach revolted; the second day I could eat anything. It's still snowing on our way; this time, snow is up to my shoulders.

Thursday, March 17.--On our march towards Al-Daba and Al-Badah and then back towards the west to rest, which is our way now; we had been here for days; it became more difficult for us. But there's still some hope left to return, if things went well.

Wednesday, March 17.--On our way from the depot; our journey was not very easy because we don't have vehicles; the distance is long; our tents were far away and we didn't reach it yet, and it is far from our current destination--which is the northern city.
