Sunday, March 18. In the afternoon I managed to stop my march at a place with an elevation of about 4 thousand m., 22 km. from the station, because the temperature is dropping again and we had to prepare to stop at night in a cold, and now it's almost time.

Sunday, March 18th, the next day. This evening was the last. After 20 o'clock it is still -30 °. My hands, my feet froze and I feel sick, so I have to stay.

March 20th, I had a hard time, all of our legs and arms covered with ice and we couldn't warm them in any way.
