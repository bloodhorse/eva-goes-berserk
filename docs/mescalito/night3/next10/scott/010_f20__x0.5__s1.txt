Monday, March 19.--No better today. There has been no relief, not for a single second. A little wind, more cold, less daylight; this is what happens. My right foot has become an egg.

The egg wants to die.

The wind and ice are making a noise, like a waterfall. This is our reality. Our feet have ceased to exist. There are no choices left.

We are far, far from any place. The egg will be there. The egg can be heard, crying.
