At least it is not so cold to-day--a little above 80°; thermometer on verge of explosion. Still the men march--if they have anything in the stomach at all to digest; we had no luck yesterday and our food's no better today; only two rabbits, but it was all right.

Wind W., force 7, temp. -14°. Wind came in from behind last night; if we had known then what we knew today we wouldn't be going over this ground; if there had been a chance, I'd have taken it. I wish I had more money. The bank wouldn't trust me again and the sheriff would get a reward. It is just 10:15 P.M.

Saturday, March
