Tuesday, March 20.--After an interval of four days we march 16 miles--temp. +3°--wind ahead; no drift; two of the sledge runners break, so do some skis.

Sunday, March 24.--In spite of ill luck and mishap we make good progress; temp. 10° below. At this date we have made a good start.

Sunday, April 6.--A slight change--a few flakes of snow in the air--not a welcome change for the man in the field; not much change in our luck, except the temperature, which has been +22°; a most delightful day.

April 15.--Temp. -6°--snow drifting all the way, with the wind in the N. W. force 2-4--more than half way.
