At nightfall the snow had become very fine and thick. Our pace has become a slow march of five minutes, stop for five. I can see it's a matter of time when I get a case of snowblind. My eyelids stick together with crust of snow--every now and again a great gust comes sweeping by--wind like a saw--driving snow up against us.

At times our pace becomes almost imperceptible; but as it gets colder we feel a bit fresher; it gets too dark to write--no use lighting candle, would have to get up to make the flame; and the ice-cold blanket would kill us--but if you read this I shall have gone on.

Yours always

March 20--We are moving, and with luck should make a mile or two
