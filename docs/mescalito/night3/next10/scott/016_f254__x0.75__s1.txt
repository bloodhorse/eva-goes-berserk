I feel sick at times; in short, the food is no longer edible. We shall stop the experiment; to-morrow will be a fast-day.

March 19.--Had to take some cold pemmican, to quiet the hunger; but there was no digesting of anything, as there was nothing in stomach but a sort of slimy mess.

At the time of this entry the wind came from W., S.E.; the cold seemed more intense. Our water is still good. I made a big mistake yesterday: I should not have used any more snow.

I ate one lump of it last evening--my first since I had my bad indigestion.
