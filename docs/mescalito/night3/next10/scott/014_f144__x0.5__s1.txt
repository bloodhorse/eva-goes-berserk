[Friday, March 21.--Lunch] (Came a lot of messages) ... I can't answer them now; there's a lot to talk about, a lot of talk; no time, I am on my way ... [to Damascus, where we stay, in front of us, three weeks, at the most; the roads are not passable--in order to make this message and to reach my father in Aleppo].
