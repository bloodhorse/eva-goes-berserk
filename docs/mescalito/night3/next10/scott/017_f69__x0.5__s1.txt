To-morrow the thermometer is supposed to sink to -56 degrees. Yesterday, during our walk we met an expedition on its way to the pole; two Norwegian explorers, one a meteorologist. He asked about me. 'What did I eat?', he said, and what do you drink, coffee, tea or chocolate?' I had a hot drink. The explorer looked at my food and shook his head: 'this food will make you very thirsty'.
