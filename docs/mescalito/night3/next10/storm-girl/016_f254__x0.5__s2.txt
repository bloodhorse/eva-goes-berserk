 but my skin is still as smooth and clear as before the day of my birth. i do not need to bathe to clean it.

but my skin has changed from white to red, the red of an autumn sky at dusk. sometimes my mother cries as she strokes it and calls me beautiful, because of it, i know i must have been as white as my clothes, before the storm.

there is no blood or scabs on my hands from my scratches and bruises, just scars and swollen veins that run all the way to my elbows. there are bruises in the shape of crescents all up and down my thighs and legs, and my toes have lost all of their nails, which is a pity as they used to look rather lovely, a pale pink colour with a little red crescent of blood beneath, always chipped in the middle
