 and sometimes, something does pull me.

something crawls over the roof. there are three glowing eyes peering through my bedroom window.

a creature made from mud and dead plants is walking down my road. i don't want to get close, but it smells sweet. i look down into its muddy puddles to find small fish, insects, frogs. it doesn't move too fast. but if you touch it, it will scream, and then melt.

if it were alive, i think it would smell like decay and the mud underneath.

it moves across the floor. if i step on it, my foot will leave an imprint. the rest will harden into clay and stay like that until you wipe it clean again.
