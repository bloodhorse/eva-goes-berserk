 as if the strands of it were alive with itchy, scratching claws that keep trying to wrap themselves around my head and it keeps telling me i must keep on brushing. it is my own hair.

when it grows and the sun is shining it becomes a mane that no one would dare touch, it becomes my only companion.

it is all the colours of the rainbow. and my own hair, in some ways. when the wind blows, it is my hair that blows around the path and keeps the other animals away.

in my room is a picture of me with the man. we are on a beach, standing in the shallow waters of a lagoon, his arms are wrapped around me as he tries to hold my body close to his.

i can still feel his touch on my skin, like the touch of silk as we stood by the sea
