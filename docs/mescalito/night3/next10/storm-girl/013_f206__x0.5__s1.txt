I look down on myself and see a man looking up at me with fear, his mouth half open.

what’s with these strange nights?

my mother has an explanation: it’s my imagination. this happens in all cities, in all houses. and all the same. sometimes the night is light and beautiful. this means that everything will be fine in the city tomorrow.

the man at my window is my reflection, and this means that my imagination has grown stronger.
