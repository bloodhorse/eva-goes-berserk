 my hands move over the space. they explore what the thicket does and how it grows, as I sit in the sun with the leaves on the ground. and while i look around, my voice moves to describe it, so it is always being translated into words and back into matter, always a translation process that creates more than just what was already there.

when you listen, your own imagination comes alive. i’ll guide you to see and hear.
