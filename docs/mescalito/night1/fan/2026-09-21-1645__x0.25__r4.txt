 just dandelions and a few scattered piles of old bones. but this tower has no walls and the dandelions have all taken root inside its skeleton.

in the days when it was new, they used to put us up on a little ledge and watch us. they'd turn our heads from side to side so we'd be able to look down. the tower was full of people, just like now. and just like now, everyone was standing close to the edges, craning their necks, trying to catch sight of someone they knew. and if they couldn't see them, they were making a racket about it.

"beck," they would shout. "beck!"

and if there were some luckless becks down below, they would all look around frantically until they saw you and shouted back: "beck!"
