little old man came and showed them what they ought to have known before,
that there was nothing more to see; for they had seen all there was to be
seen.

He then went away in his little cart with the woman and her husband to the
other house that they had rented for their stay in the country, where there
were beds with blankets, and sheets with towels, and curtains on the
