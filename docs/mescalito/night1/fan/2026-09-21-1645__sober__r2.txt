 it leans sideways a little, so the wind comes right through it in summer, but it's warm and snug in winter.

i live up in the top room with beck and the other tower girls, although i don't see much of them during the day. it's our job to keep watch from the tower and make sure nobody gets near enough to mess up our work.

our work is keeping the lines straight.

they start way off to the south-east where the mountains are and then curve away into the west, then they loop around over our heads and disappear in the forest beyond the fields. you can follow them as far as the eye can reach. i can pick out the lines that were made last night, when beck was in charge.
