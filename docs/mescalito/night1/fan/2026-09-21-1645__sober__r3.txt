 the tower is old. the dandelion heads are old, and so am i.

we don't know where it comes from, but we do know this much: it's old as the earth and old as the sky; older than the people who were born before us. no one remembers when they came to live here, or why. the people, i mean, not the dandelions.

i can tell you one thing for sure: there aren't many left here. sometimes i wonder if i'm the only one.

in the old days, there were always new people arriving, or so it seemed to me then. they used to bring me flowers from wherever it was they came from. sometimes they were people i knew, but often they were strangers. some of them stayed, though. and their children and grandchildren, great-grandchildren
