 pulling my head backwards, down to the earth.

and there are many things hidden in this garden of mine; the leaves whisper them into my hair.

my fingernails are like claws. i dig them into the ground, try to scratch the soil, tear away the flowers and plants until i am left with nothing but dust, and roots, and dirt. until i am covered in soil.

i walk along the roof of my house, back and forth, like pacing a cage. i watch the sky, wait for rain. for thunderstorms. when the rain comes it washes my face. the tears of the sky wash me clean, take all the dirt with them.

but every now and then, something catches my eye, some speck in the air, and i see a leaf on the breeze.

once it was an autumn leaf
