 the roots are buried in the ground and sometimes they reach into the sky, or is it that i’m the one reaching down?

sometimes i am afraid of myself and my voice is full of screams, like an echo through the mountains, and i feel as if someone is standing on the other side. i look for someone else but all i can find are reflections of me, and mirrors. sometimes there is nothing behind it and all i can do is run and hide inside it until i feel the blood rushing through my veins and i know it’s coming to take me away.

there is a tree standing tall in the middle of it. its roots are deep and reach outwards into the water, where fish swim amongst them, where i watch the light from above shine down onto my face.

my mouth is a cave. it is empty of people.
