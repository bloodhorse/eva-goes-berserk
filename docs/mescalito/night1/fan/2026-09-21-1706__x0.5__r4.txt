family would call him a fool but they were fools themselves; they did
not understand, and yet their lack of understanding made them blind.
They had a child and that was not so bad, for their child would have
no more understanding than its father, who understood less than his
parents. His parents did not know, and if they had known what it was,
then it would be clear why the neighbours called him the brass-witch.
He was called the brass-witch, and the man who understood nothing was
not a fool, or else his wife could be the mother of the man with no
understanding, though his understanding of her made her blind.
