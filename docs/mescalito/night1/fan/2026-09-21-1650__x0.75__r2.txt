when it gets dark, when the sun is no longer visible, when we are no longer young. when we reach the end of our days and can no longer stand on the precipice looking down at the vast expanse below. when all this ends. and when there is nothing left to say. then, only then, will we have seen what lies beneath the surface. and maybe then we will have learned what we did not want to see in the first place.

when you hear these words you are filled with rage. with an anger so hot you can no longer think or breathe or feel. the darkness will come and i will be waiting for it. and when it does, when it arrives and is here, you will have to face me.
