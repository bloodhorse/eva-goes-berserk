 nobody knows who built the tower. some say it was an accident, but others say it wasn't.

when the sun hits it right, the tower throws back the light in a thousand splinters of gold. it is made of tiny flakes, each one no bigger than my fingertip, all piled up into something the size of a mountain. you could build a wall with them or a bridge or a roof. there's a whole field full of them lying about.

someone tried to cut a piece out once. it happened just after i started in the tower. that was two years ago, when the last of the people were still living down in the town and they could send their things up the ropeway for us. one of the men had a long saw he used to trim the wood when they brought it in from outside.
