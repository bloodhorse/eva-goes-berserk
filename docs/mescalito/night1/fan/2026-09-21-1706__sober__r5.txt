  When a
cow or pig died they always gave the witch a leg, so that she might
fatten herself upon its flesh, while her husband lived upon nothing but
milk, bread and salt.

She was a very strong woman, and often used to go with the peasants to
the fields and carry hay and corn.  Then she used to boast how well she
was paid for all her trouble.  "The only thing that grieves me," she
used to say, "is that I get nothing more than milk, bread and salt.  If I
got something else as well, what would I not do?"  Her neighbours, of
