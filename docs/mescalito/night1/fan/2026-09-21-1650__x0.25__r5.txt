 the birds will pluck the bits that they want from me and they leave the rest. when i open my eyes, they are watching me.

i hear their voices through the trees. they sound like a distant crowd, like an opera, and i feel the world rushing up to me. it feels as if i am falling backwards into an empty room, like my voice is an echo bouncing off walls made of ice.

but they never come, and i am left alone with my memories. i can see them all around me: the things i’ve touched, the people i’ve loved, the places i’ve been. i think about all those places. i miss them.
