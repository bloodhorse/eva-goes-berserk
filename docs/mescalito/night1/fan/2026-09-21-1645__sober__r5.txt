 the tower's roof is half-gone and its windows have no glass, and the ivy has climbed all the way up around its sides, curling into its belly where it has begun to eat the brickwork. when i stand in the door at the top i can see a long way across the fields. and then beyond them to the city, where there are no more people living. just towers, tall towers of glass and stone, with their own lines inside them. maybe they still have some lines left that work; we don't know because we haven't heard anyone speak to us from inside them. but if we did, then they would call us out of our tower, and out of our houses too. and they would tell us to go somewhere else and leave them alone.
