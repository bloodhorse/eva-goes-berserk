 and then, when it is combed, all the pieces are taken away from me until all that is left is this thin shell. there are not even enough strands to make a rope for a pulley or to make the bridge swing back and forth over the ravine. i want to jump and pull at it like the string of an instrument, but the instrument itself is silent. the storm takes away the words that i used to play it.

there are only sounds without meaning. just sound without thought, sound without content. my mind is an empty space.

and when the sun rises, it is not beautiful anymore, because i am still in darkness. i cannot feel the sun on my skin.

when you can’t feel anything, your heart turns into stone. you become hard like the rocks of a mountain.
