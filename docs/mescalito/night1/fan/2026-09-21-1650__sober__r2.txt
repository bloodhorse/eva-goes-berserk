 it hurts like my heart. so i hold still while you cut out all the tangles, gently, so as to not hurt me too much, or break any branches.

once we were on top of a cliff. and there was an empty beach below us and an expanse of ocean stretching into infinity. i jumped and you caught me.

my bones are heavy. sometimes, when i look at myself in the mirror i feel that they will give way under my skin, crack through my flesh like dry soil in an arid field and sink through my legs until they are buried underground.

my hands are small but they carry me, and i use them for everything. to eat, to write, to play piano. to touch and feel and to be touched and felt by. i do not have any more than these hands. i hold them close
