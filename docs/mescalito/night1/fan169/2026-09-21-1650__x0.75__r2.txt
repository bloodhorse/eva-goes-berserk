 it hurts when i try to pull the tangles apart with my fingers or with the comb. sometimes the pain is so bad that it feels like the pain that must come before death.

i can hear my heart pumping in my ears. i have tried counting sheep but this has never worked because when i count them i imagine their blood staining my fingers and then i feel very frightened. when this happens i cry and then people shout at me and they call me names such as 'bad girl'. my favourite story is one about a girl who lives behind the clouds.

sometimes when the sky cries i think that i am crying too. and the raindrops taste like salt. [end of text]
