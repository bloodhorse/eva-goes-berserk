 nobody knows where they are; all we know is that they're not here now. if anybody comes now he will want to ask why the dandelions don't go out. how can the dandelions go on with no one to look after them?

there are people here now too, of course; but they've all got something else to think about besides the dandelions. so I told them I'd stand here till the end of time, if necessary, and watch for them, if that would help any. and then i would try and find out who had let the dandelions go on so long, without looking after them. that was really all i wanted to know. it didn't matter whether they were dandelions or anything else, for that matter.
