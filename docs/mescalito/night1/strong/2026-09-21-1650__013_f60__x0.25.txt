 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and when it is dark i see my reflection in the puddles on the floor, the stars above me. and i am afraid to be alone with all this water, i do not know what to say or where to stand.

sometimes the sun comes up. it rises, and sets, but only after i have turned around to see it, after i have watched it move behind the houses, behind the trees.

so now i know where the sun is.

this morning it was east of the tree.
yesterday it set at 4:20.
it has been sunny for the last four days.

when it rains it makes footprints in the sand.
they look like a river, or a road, and i wonder who is walking out there without shoes.
