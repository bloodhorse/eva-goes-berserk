 like someone is dragging me back.

my clothes are just rags, old dresses and sweaters that used to be blue but now they are a pale grey from so much rain, so much time being washed in it. the fabric is frayed at the edges where it rubs against rocks. it’s worn down by time, and yet, somehow it never wears out.

once, i tried to burn my dress on a bonfire, but the flames danced away from me as if they were scared, so i stood there and laughed with the trees at the sky. we were all trying to put the fire out. in the end, the rain came and washed it away.
