 pulling my head backwards, away from my shoulders.

it has been so long since i cut my hair.

when the rain falls it feels like a shower of pins and needles. and when it is heavy it is as if a thousand people are all whispering secrets into my ears.

the thunder is the voice of my god and the lightening is its smile. when it strikes it makes me gasp for breath. my heart pounds against the ribcage, i cannot stop shaking.

and after, there is quiet. there is no noise but the sound of the river. it is a wide and empty place and the world is asleep. [end of text]
