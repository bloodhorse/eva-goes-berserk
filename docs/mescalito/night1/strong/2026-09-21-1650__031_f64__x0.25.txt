 pulling my head backwards, down to the earth.

if i look out my window on a rainy morning i see a girl sitting under my tree, holding her knees to her chest, hugging her legs like a doll, and looking at nothing. she is staring straight through me and she looks so lost and lonely.

the sky is the colour of ash and i sit with her on the wet grass, because we are friends. she cries, but it makes me happy because i never cry. she has long, brown hair that covers her eyes and a blue scarf around her neck. the ends of it are blowing in the wind like sails, like she’s flying. she tells me things.

her name is Sophia.

she says, “I don’t know if I can keep doing this.” I want to touch her face, hold her hand
