then there’s the train. the train goes back to where we started from, but never stops long enough to take us home. the train is always late, or running through the station, or on fire, or is somehow stuck in some kind of purgatory. i watch it go past, and sometimes the conductor gets out and starts walking away. i say, ‘don’t leave the train!’ and he just shrugs and walks off into the distance.

or there is the cat. it is white and has two tails, but it only has the one set of eyes. the eyes are grey. and the tail is just like a cow’s tail.
