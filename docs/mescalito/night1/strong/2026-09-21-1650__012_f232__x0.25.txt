 sometimes when the clouds are full of stars they whisper to me in the dark.

but there are things you cannot do with clouds.

there is a wind that moves like a wave, like the water when i am alone. the trees are dancing, and the clouds move with me and we dance too, all together. we laugh, and sing, and throw our voices over the mountain and through the trees.

my mother stands at the edge of the field and she looks down on us.

“stop it,” she says. “you will upset your father.”

the wind slows. the leaves fall to the ground. the sky stills. and the clouds grow heavy with rain.

but we keep moving. and i lift my arms up, up, as high as the sun, higher than the moon, and say,

“I want to stay.” [end of text]
