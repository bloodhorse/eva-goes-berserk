 pulling my head backwards. i look into the mirror. i am not sure whether it is really my reflection i am seeing or someone else’s, some girl who has found her way here and is standing behind me. i can see the outline of her in my hair, but she won’t look at me directly. she stands a few inches to my left. when i step towards her, she takes two steps back.

we walk together, under a grey sky. sometimes it rains and i catch the drops in my mouth. we follow each other, each lost, each trying to find a way out. the trees are full of shadows. i am lost in her shadow, she is lost in mine. i don’t recognise anything here, these dark buildings, the crumbling stone walls, the streets that go nowhere.

there are no flowers now.
