 like someone is grabbing at my feet as they drag me down to the bottom of the river bed.

my hair is like this because my father, when he found me, when he picked me out of the stream, he had just been picking berries, wild raspberries in fact. when he left us his hands were stained with the juice, red as blood.

and so my father used to be called ‘red’, as my grandmother, her name was also ‘ruby’, after all the precious gemstones, like rubies. or emeralds. my father would also say that the river is like a sea, and that our family are the rocks and boulders which make it up. he would also talk about how his name was not only the name of a large boat, and his family is named ‘the fishers’.
