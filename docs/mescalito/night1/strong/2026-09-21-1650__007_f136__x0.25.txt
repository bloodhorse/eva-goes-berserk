 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

it’s like I’m underwater. The sky is black but the trees are orange with their own fire and my skin is full of stars. the rain falls on me. the leaves and stones in my hair absorb them. my hair weighs so much it sinks me into the water, where the reeds reach out for me and pull me down into the mud.

it takes me down into a deep, dark place. down into an underground river which has been there since time began. and here is a story i have heard: In a time long before my time, a people called the Picts lived on the islands of Orkney. One day a woman was bathing naked by the water when a group of men from a foreign tribe came to steal her. They stole her away, taking her back
