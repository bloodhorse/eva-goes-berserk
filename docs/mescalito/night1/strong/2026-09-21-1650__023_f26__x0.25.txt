 sometimes when the rain comes and it falls heavy against me it takes on the smell of saltwater.

once i had to leave my hair for a moment to walk back into the world of men, but when i returned the thicket was gone, it was gone forever. all that remained were the twigs and dry grass and the small stones, scattered here and there.

the storm rages on, it comes and goes. when i was little, my father used to take us down to the sea and he’d throw rocks at the clouds to make them rain. we would wait and then there it was, like a sudden song rising from the ground. it would rise and fill up all of our ears and it felt good.

now, there is no such thing as the past anymore. [end of text]
