 sometimes when the moon is high in the sky i look up and i see stars through it.

when i sleep, i wrap myself around the world.

the storm says to me, ‘do not fear.’ the storm is a dark hand holding a torch, waving back and forth.

‘there is no death,’ it tells me, and its words fall like rain onto the earth.

and so i go back down the stairs. i sit in the house by the window with the curtain drawn. i am waiting for my mother to call me for tea, or maybe to take a nap. [end of text]
