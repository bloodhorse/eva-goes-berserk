when i was little my hair got caught in the branches of the big tree. my mother had to cut it out, and my head hurt for days, even though she tried not to tug. it left scars on my scalp, and now, sometimes i see blood in my hair and think i have broken those places open.

when my father came to visit, he touched it carefully. he ran his fingers through it and told me it felt like water. my mother didn’t want him to come, but he always did. he liked the house, but i don’t think he liked her very much. i used to pretend my parents were dead, that i was an orphan, living with a grandmother in a haunted house.
