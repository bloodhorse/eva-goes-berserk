 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and there are people all around me. people who love me, who watch over me, and those who watch me to make sure i am not getting too far away. and when the clouds part and the moon rises, i look up into the night sky and see their faces, and they are smiling.

the moon is like the sun; they are always changing. some days they shine, other days they are gone entirely. some nights the moon is full, others it is just a crescent, or nothing at all. but it is never the same, so it is never boring. it is always something new.

and some nights it rains. it pours down upon us and drowns us in the water, in our clothes, on the ground. but then it is warm again,
