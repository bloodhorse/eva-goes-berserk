 sometimes when the rain pours down it sticks to my face and i look like the sea witch from the stories, all wrinkles and deep creases and salt and wind. the sky has a mouth like a tunnel, a passage, like a gate, like an archway to another world. and we whisper through it.

i am always the girl with the windswept hair. i am the girl who runs away to find adventure, or the girl who goes searching for answers to her questions, or the girl who dreams of something greater.

i am not the girl in the story, but the storyteller, the one who looks at the sky and tells herself that she will find what she’s looking for someday soon.

the world is a book, and those who do not travel read only a page – saint augustine [end of text]
