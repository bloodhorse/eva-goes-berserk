 sometimes when the wind comes and takes its fingers through it, it seems as though i am dancing on water.

i take off my shoes. the sand between my toes. the ground beneath my feet.

once, it said, you are beautiful, but in another language, you were something else. and you knew it was right, and you were proud to be called it. but i know there is something better in every word.

there is no more sky, but clouds and birds and airplanes, and a river that flows endlessly around me, over me, under me. the water runs from one end of the world to another, and it never stops. there is nothing more perfect than that. it is everywhere at once. [end of text]
