 sometimes when the clouds are far away or sleeping they look like soft things and you can fall asleep against them.

i don’t sleep often. the moon shines like a bright light on the rooftops, and in the morning, when the sun wakes up, i take my cloak to the hilltop to watch it rise, and the clouds catch their breath and watch it with me.

i walk home with my hands clasped, my head bowed, and sometimes it whispers my name.

i stand in front of my house, looking out the window at the street. i don’t think i should be here, i should leave. i am looking for my sister, my brother, my mother, my father.

sometimes there are no lights, and it is so very dark i am afraid.

and i wake up again in a quiet house.
