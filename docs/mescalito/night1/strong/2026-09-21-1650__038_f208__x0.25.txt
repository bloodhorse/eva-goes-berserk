i remember how it felt to fall down. and when she told us off, and asked me why i let him do it. i didn’t know what to say.

and now when the rain comes i have to hide my hair away in my room or the teachers will tell me off because i can’t hear them when it’s raining. and i can’t look at the clouds for all the rain.
