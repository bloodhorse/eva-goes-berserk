 sometimes when the moon is low in the sky i find i have no strength left to walk forward.

then my friend says ‘there’s no time left,’ and i know he is talking about the story i am writing. the story of our life.

i wonder why he chose this particular metaphor. was it because he saw my life in terms of time? or perhaps it was because he saw my life as an empty vessel? did he not see the ocean, the endless blue sky?

it doesn’t matter, because i understand all of those things, i understand that his life was in a glass case. the thing is, he loved that case, he looked after it and cleaned it with love. he called it ‘his muse’, and his name was ‘Rav’ (meaning raven). he was tall and handsome and he loved my father
