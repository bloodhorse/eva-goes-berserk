 pulling my head backwards, away from my shoulders.

it has been this way ever since i fell down and broke the glass table with my head, my mouth full of shards, blood like a river down my neck, the skin of my face split in half like a snake’s.

sometimes the wind brings rain. i wait for the lightning, watch it travel across the sky and try to catch the thunder as it rolls between my teeth, over the backs of my knees. when i am tired, i let my hair fall into the clouds and the stars hold it up in their hands, let it lie on their skin and rest against their bodies, until i am ready to carry it again.
