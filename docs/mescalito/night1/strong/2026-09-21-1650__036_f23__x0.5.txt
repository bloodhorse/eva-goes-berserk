 sometimes when the clouds go down to meet their reflections in the river, i remember my face before you saw me, my mouth without your name, my hands without your hands, the sun before the sky went down and the night came to stay, and the moon rose and told me its secrets, and we slept.

my mouth is a garden, full of fruit trees. you take me there with a basket for my lips, which you say are the sweetest of all the fruits on this earth, and the birds sing about me. your arms are like birds, they come and go, but not so much that I would think anything is wrong, just that you might be keeping them away from me. i have noticed how they tend to fly to you. it is an open invitation.
