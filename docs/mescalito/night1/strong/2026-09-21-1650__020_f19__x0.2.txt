 tugging. tearing. i want to scream into the trees and then fall asleep on the moss.

when i am older i want to go to the ocean. i will take a boat and i will sleep on the waves, i will drift in and out with the tides. i’ll take the stars home and hold them in the palms of my hands. when i open them i want you to see your own reflection in them.

i want to sit in the sunlight and paint myself with flowers, let the colours run down my face like tears, let them seep into my skin, let the earth soak it all up until it has all gone and i’m just empty.

and then i want to get drunk off of wine, let the blood flow through me, and dance with the shadows in the moonlight until they carry me away.
