 sometimes when the clouds are far away or hiding behind the hilltops, my hair becomes brittle and breaks easily, like straw.

when the sun sets and the stars come out, my skin is covered with constellations, so that people can find their way home. my feet become rooted in the earth like tree trunks, and they are warm and heavy like wood. my hands grow wide, become leaves. they hold things carefully, like the moon, or like the sky itself. they do not have fingers.

when it is summer and the earth is soft and green, and the trees are full, i am all over them, as a thousand leaves. when it is autumn and winter, i turn to bark and branches. the sky is still over me, and the clouds are my crown. the storms blow around my head like hair.
