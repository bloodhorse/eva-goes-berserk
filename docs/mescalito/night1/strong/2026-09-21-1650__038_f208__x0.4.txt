 sometimes when the clouds are low and dark they hide me from view, but then i don’t feel sad because the rain keeps falling into my eyes and down my neck, and the birds sing, and all the green things are happy, even the grass, even though it’s cut off short to make it neat.

the grass is not nice, because it doesn’t look how i want it to, but i like it when it grows in clumps where the sun falls on it.

mummy says i can’t play here any more because i am too dirty, but the rain washes me clean. daddy said that it rains for you, but he won’t let me play out now.

it has rained every night this week. daddy said it is the monsoon. [end of text]
