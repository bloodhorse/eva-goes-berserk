 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and then i let out my breath. slowly. like when the rain falls hard against the ground and all you can do is stand still in the middle of the street. the wind is rushing in my ears now and it sounds like music.

it has never spoken to me before, this storm, but today it whispered to me and i knew its name.

this was written for an exhibition, by a writer, as part of an initiative to explore the connection between mental health and nature. i’d be interested to know what you think. [end of text]
