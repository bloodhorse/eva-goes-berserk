 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and there are people who pull at me too. they tell me they love me but i do not believe them. i will not believe them, because they have never said the word. they do not know how to say it. so instead, i put myself into this forest, and let the roots grow into my hair and become part of me, because no one else has ever loved me.

i tell the trees everything. they hear me speak, but i never hear them answer. instead, the rain falls in big fat drops. the river swells, and the wind comes sweeping over the field and makes the wheat dance.

the trees listen to me. and sometimes when i talk, their leaves change colour. [end of text]
