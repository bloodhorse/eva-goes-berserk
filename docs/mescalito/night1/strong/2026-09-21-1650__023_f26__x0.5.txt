 sometimes when the wind dies down and i hear birdsong in the distance, i try to brush my teeth or go for a walk with the other dogs in the park. i am always looking for them and they are never to be found anywhere, but that doesn’t stop us from talking together in the kitchen while we cook dinner, and telling the neighbours about all our daily lives.

but how can i ever give you anything? what does a dog do when the wind blows? what kind of weather is this, anyway? just now, there are a lot of people looking for their own true selves. and when the sun shines, i have nothing to say about it.

once a dog had no answer, there isn’t even a dog. it has no use for the moon and the stars, and neither does your mother, nor your father
