 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and when it is hot i think it smells like smoke.

i try not to cry in the shower but my mother has found the bloody tissue, has taken it from the bin in the bathroom, has left it on the floor so i cannot miss it. now i look at the red stain, feel it under my fingers. i run my thumb over it and then bring it close to my eyes, closer, until my breath stops.

it looks like berries.

the birds sing.

they call to one another. they don’t even have words, but i understand. i don’t need to be told.

we sit on the roof of our house. i have painted my nails dark, just to make my hands more visible. sometimes he will bring me a cigarette or two. and then we talk
