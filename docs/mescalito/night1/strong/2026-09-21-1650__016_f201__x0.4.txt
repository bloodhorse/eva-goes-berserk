 sometimes when the clouds part and the sun breaks through, i find that i am sweating underneath all this stuff, all these roots.

and the sky has told me to my face that it hates me. that it is always there waiting for me, watching over me, following me wherever i go. but if i stop, and turn, then it does nothing. i ask it what i have done so wrong and it replies “i don’t remember, you’ve just annoyed me” – and then, like an idiot, it laughs.

i call my father to my side, to ask his advice, and he says: “oh, for crying out loud – you’re going to give me a heart attack!”

there’s no one here. not a single person. it’s all the fault of the villagers who’ve gone. they’ve left
