 sometimes when the rain pours down it sticks to my face and i look like an animal that has fallen into a puddle.

when the sun sets and it becomes night, the darkness comes, and all of the little things start to crawl. spiders spin webs in my hair. moths get stuck inside and try to get out again, beating their wings against my cheeks and forehead. tiny little bugs fly into my eyes and crawl in my mouth.

i look around to see what i have been missing, who i might have known but never met. my eyes are like searchlights, they scour the earth, they find faces. i have never met my grandmother, my uncle, my sister. i have heard stories about them, but i have no face to go with those names, only their voices echoing inside my head.
