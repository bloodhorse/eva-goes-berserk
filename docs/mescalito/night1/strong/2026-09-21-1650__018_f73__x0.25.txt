 sometimes when the moon is low in the sky i let go of it and watch it float away into the darkness, where i cannot reach it, where i don’t need to.

when it is dark i find i can see further, beyond the forest, beyond the fields. i can hear the sound of waves lapping against a beach far off on the coast.

and when i stand very still, i think i feel the earth breathe, under the trees, under my feet.

when it rains, i do not care if i am soaked to the bone.

in this life i can be alone, or I can be with all living things. [end of text]
