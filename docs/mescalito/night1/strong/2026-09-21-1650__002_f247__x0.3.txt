 pulling my head down. but i won’t look at the ground, no matter how many times they tell me to.

they tell me to be afraid of thunder. and not to cry for the moon.

so i look to the sun instead, and it burns bright in my eyes and turns me into an owl. i watch the world change, turn around and become different. i don’t mind. i see you too, sometimes.

i try to explain, and sometimes i succeed. i talk in circles, talk with my hands, talk like a windmill, like a wild cat, like a woman. but it’s all wrong, it’s all wrong. they want me to stop talking. but i can’t stop talking.

i tell them to take it, to take everything and go away. and maybe then they’ll let me alone.
