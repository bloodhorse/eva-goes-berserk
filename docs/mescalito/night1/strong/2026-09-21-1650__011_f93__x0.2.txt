and when it’s hot i take all my clothes off and i’m naked except for the red flowers that grow out of me, like small, scarlet lips. and the leaves in my hair.

i wear shoes with holes in them because it’s hard to keep them clean, and when the snow comes it burns my feet but i don’t care.

when it rains the streets flood, and the cars are covered up to their wheels. then the rain comes down so fast that it hits you, like stones. i look up to the sky, and the rain is like a veil in front of my eyes. like the fog on the ocean. or the mist rising off the river, after a night of cold, still water.
