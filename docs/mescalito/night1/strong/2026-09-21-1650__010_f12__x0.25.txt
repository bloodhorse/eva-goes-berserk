 pulling my body backwards. but i stand tall with my hair like a crown upon my head.

when i hear a noise behind me i turn, my hair flying out in the wind.

and the storm falls.

“and the rain will wash you clean, like snow” he says.

his eyes are deep pools of dark blue, and they are alive.

and then he reaches up to take my hand and together we watch the sky fall, as the clouds part to reveal the sky behind. it is white, like new cotton. and there is a voice saying: “and you are a woman of great power…” [end of text]
