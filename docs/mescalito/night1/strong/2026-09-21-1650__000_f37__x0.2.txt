 pulling my head backwards. i don’t mind too much.

the wind tells me i am ugly and my hair is like straw.

there is a bird that sits on the edge of my hair, just above my shoulder, and its beak is long, curved like a sickle. sometimes it pecks at me and sometimes it preens. its feathers are grey and white and they move when the wind blows. when it flies away, i can feel a gap behind me.

when the storm is finished, i come down and go inside to be alone with the television. it is a blue light in a box, and it fills the room. i look at it and think about my hair. it doesn’t speak back.

my name is mary. [end of text]
