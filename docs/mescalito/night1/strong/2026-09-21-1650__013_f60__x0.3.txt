 tugging. twisting. my hands are trapped behind my back. it makes my feet heavy when i walk.

i try not to step in the puddles. it’s not allowed. the rain makes it difficult. the trees and bushes are black, green or white. they change colours as the weather shifts. their shadows are as wide as they are tall.

and then i see her: a girl who has lost her voice. i call to her, but she does not speak. she will only use signs to communicate. i cannot understand. we move through the trees and we do not look back, but the girl’s footsteps make imprints on the ground.

as we walk, we count them. it takes seven steps for a footprint to appear. i have no way to count, so i use sticks as markers. the girl marks time
