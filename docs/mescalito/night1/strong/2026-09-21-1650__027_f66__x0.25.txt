 pulling my head backwards, away from my shoulders.

and there was a time when i thought my hair would save me. when i believed that, should my feet fail, it would reach out, grab hold of me, and pull me back. i do not believe that anymore.

my legs are long and slender. they move in the opposite way to how you’d expect; they take me backwards, away from you. i’m afraid, but it doesn’t make sense: my legs are moving in the right direction. they should be carrying me to safety.

it was at this point, i think, that i became aware of myself. of the things i am doing, or trying to do, and of the ways they seem wrong, like the wrong sort of answer. and yet it is too late. too late for right or wrong.
