 sometimes when the rain pours down it drips from my hair in thick strands of muddy water.

when the rain stops i drink it, but my throat is so full of dirt that the rain runs over my lips, over my cheeks, into the folds of my dress. my shoes are too big now. my legs are like sticks, covered in hair that never ends. i think if i tried to take off my shoes they would break. my eyes are dull. there are small clouds in them, little islands that never grow larger or smaller. there is no life there, and my mind is as empty as the space behind my eyes. but i am happy. my hair is thick and soft, and sometimes when i walk, i can feel the sun on my back through it, warm and soft against my shoulders.

i sleep now.
