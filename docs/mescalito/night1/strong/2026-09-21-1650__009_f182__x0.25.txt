 something pulls on my mind and my spirit until they are ready to break out into song. sometimes my songs feel as if someone else’s voice is singing them.

and this is the secret: the secret that i am part of his life forever, with a confidence born of Christ’s faith.

it is like dying to what we were, so that we may live like him. He died for all, so that all might come to share in his life.

i know how much pressure Christ put up with from you when he tolerated you in your former way of living. He did not think of them in their way as he does. Rather, he thought it necessary to bring all people into God’s grace.

– 2 Corinthians 10:1-4 [end of text]
