 pulling my head backwards, away from my shoulders.

and when it’s hot i take it apart with my fingers, pick through it carefully. the birds come and take the things that they like, or bring me things of their own, feathers, teeth, sticks with soft green moss on them, small rocks shaped like tiny skulls, flowers, shells. i hang them on strings to dry.

in winter i take them off again and i keep them safe, but sometimes it forgets and brings me more, even when my hands are shaking with the cold and it snows on me, i have to go into the dark and pull it all out with my bare fingers. there are days when i don’t get dressed until evening.

i cannot see it so well, in the daylight, the sky. it looks grey and flat, just another piece
