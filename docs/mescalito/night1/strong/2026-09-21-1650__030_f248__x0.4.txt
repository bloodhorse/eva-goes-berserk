 sometimes i am frightened. i don’t always feel like going there, but then i get sucked into it because the pull is strong.

it is like this:

“it is a warm night, i think of you in a certain way,” and so the storm picks me up and carries me off. i sit with my legs out the window, letting it carry me off and away. i tell it things i should never say.

there is no one here, we are all alone.

and then: “i cannot stop thinking about you,” and the storm carries me through the fields, the rain is coming down on the leaves, making the ground soft and wet. my heart pounds as i watch for the red light flashing behind us. [end of text]
