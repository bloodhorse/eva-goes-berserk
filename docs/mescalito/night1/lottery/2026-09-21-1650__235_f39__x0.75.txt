 sometimes when the moon shines on me i walk all night and am gone till dawn.

sometimes my hair catches on fire.

some nights I can hear it breathing

it has the mouth of a cave.

and i go in and in.
i think the moon must be eating my heart away. that’s why i keep telling it to rise, rise.

–Lucia Perillo
from Inseminating the Elephant
Alice James Books, 2009 [end of text]
