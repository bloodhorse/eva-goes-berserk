 tugging, grasping, holding on.

sometimes there is a storm, and i stand above the rooftops, but then i remember the other houses. their broken walls, the cracked windows, and their missing doors, the ones with holes in them, like mouths. so many houses without walls, their roofs sagging under the weight of the clouds. all those windows without shutters, those door frames without doors.

all those doors, without keys.

i look away. i cannot see those things.

i keep looking forward. to better times, i tell it. to when it is finished, and the rain is only a sprinkle, a mist, a shower. i tell it about all the good days ahead.

about when the rain has gone. [end of text]
