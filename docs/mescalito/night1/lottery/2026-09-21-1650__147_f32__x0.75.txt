 sometimes when the rain comes and washes away my face and all the paths behind me, i remember what i was before i lost myself in the forest.

once, the rain said i was a prince and he wanted to be me, because he thought that princes lived forever and no one hurt them. i laughed and told him to go away, because i didn’t want his love. i wanted the storm.

and now it comes every year and washes the blood away and holds my hand while the stars are falling out of the sky.

the sun says she has always loved me, and i don’t know whether to believe her or not. the moon says he thinks that she does, but he’s only the moon so how would he know. and he’s the one that bites me, every time. sometimes there’s blood, sometimes only rain
