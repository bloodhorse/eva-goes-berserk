 then i go into a thicket and scratch myself on the branches. i rub my face hard against the bark, but still there are many of these twigs and they will not fall out, because my skin is so strong and so slippery.

i feel sorry for my hair and wish I had some soap to wash it, and water, too. but perhaps it would be best if i were not here in this country but somewhere where i do not have to wash or worry about my appearance, where the trees do not need pruning and the paths do not need raking, and where I can have my own life. Perhaps I would be happy there. Then I might take my wife with me. I love my wife very much and I would like to be with her wherever I am.
