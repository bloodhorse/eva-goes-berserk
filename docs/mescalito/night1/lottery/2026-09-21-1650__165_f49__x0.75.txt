 sometimes when the moon is high in the sky i get up and run through it and all sorts of things happen, but nothing comes out the other side.

the sun is at the centre of a circle with the moon to the left, and the stars in orbit around it. i am somewhere on the rim between the earth and the sun.

a storm can look beautiful and seem gentle. but when the winds pick up you need to grab on to something fast, or risk being blown away. this is how a tornado forms.

this is how i am going to die: in the future, we are all going to drown. i will be in an aeroplane. i won’t see it coming. [end of text]
