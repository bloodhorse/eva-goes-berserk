 sometimes when the rain comes and it gets heavier i get angry and my hair grows longer and thicker than ever before, but still you cannot see me.

my arms are my hands, my hands are my arms. my eyes, my eyes are the earth, they see it all. my ears are the trees.

the leaves are a great pile, wet and heavy with blood. they are torn from the bodies of my daughters. there are 30 or 40 of them.

and what is left, they say is my body.

the only light i have is the sun. [end of text]
