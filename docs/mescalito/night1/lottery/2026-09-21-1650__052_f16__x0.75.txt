 something pulls on my heart and my legs tremble. but i am always careful to keep calm.

once i have told everything i think of, all that has come to my mind and which the storm has taken from me, i am freed. i can sleep peacefully.

my mother thinks that it is dangerous to give everything to a stranger, even if he comes to me with the wind and the rain. but when he asks me, when the storm says: give it all to me, it seems the right thing to do. then i can say yes, i can say: i want to do that. [end of text]
