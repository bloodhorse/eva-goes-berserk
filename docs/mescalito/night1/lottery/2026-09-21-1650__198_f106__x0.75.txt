 when i am indoors, it looks like i’m standing on the edge of an abyss with nothing below me, but when i’m outside, you can see that it is the earth.

i never wash. i use a lotion called go away, which smells like rose geranium oil. it means people keep their distance, and they are afraid to approach me. i always sleep with one eye open and i’ve never got drunk, but i’ve been known to get falling-down-syrupy, because i’ve got a sweet tooth, i’ve also got a secret stash of pop-tarts and a penchant for pash-ants. i love to laugh and sing and play the piano, especially when there’s a party. and i also do a good impression of a foghorn, especially after three glasses of sherry.
