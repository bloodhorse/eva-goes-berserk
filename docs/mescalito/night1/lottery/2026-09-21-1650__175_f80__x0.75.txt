 sometimes when the moon is full and high i sleep out under the stars. then I take handfuls of earth, and eat them until I am sick and cannot remember my name. after that, i sleep in the arms of the great oak, or the pine trees. they keep me warm in the cold nights of winter.

I have watched the clouds come and go. they pass over me with the seasons. In winter the winds blow, and the clouds are dark grey. They drop their burden of rain, which freezes as it falls. in spring the wind breathes upon the world. the rain is washed from the land by the warm sun. and the flowers grow out of the earth.
