 sometimes when the winds are low and i hear birdsong in the distance. sometimes i see butterflies flying past, i hear laughter from the rooftops and i know it is coming from a house called ‘hope’ or a flat called ‘fuck yeah’ or ‘the revolution has started’. sometimes i stand outside a house called “what is going on” and ask it some questions, like how can we make sure everyone is safe, fed, happy?

one day i found myself in the land of “justice for all”, a land where all people are equal, a land with no walls. the air is crisp, the people are clean, there is fresh water and fruit for all. and everyone wears matching pyjamas, it was very civilized, until i had an idea. why don’t we have some diversity?
