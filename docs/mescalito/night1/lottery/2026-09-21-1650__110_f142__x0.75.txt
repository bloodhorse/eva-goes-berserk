 sometimes when the clouds are too close or too loud, i want to pull them down.

sometimes there are voices, and they speak through you, they move your mouth, and it sounds so different from how i say it, you do not like it, please stop. my voice is softer, i don’t mean to scare you, just to remind you to breathe. but they don’t listen, they never listen.

but there’s a sound, there is a noise. there’s an ache, i can feel it in my mouth and my eyes. there is an itch, a tightness, a noise, and it hurts. it is getting harder to ignore. they make it hurt and you just let them do it. and they get angry and i can’t – please

i try. it hurts when you fight, stop fighting.
