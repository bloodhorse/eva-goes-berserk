 sometimes when the clouds part and the moon gets too close to me, or perhaps i get too close to her, i fall away into the void and she turns all of the other worlds red, so they can see me, but it hurts. i can hear myself scream.

the rain feels like spiders crawling all over my body, like a thousand tiny fingers tickling me.

and my breath, well… it smells of peppermint, which is strange, because i don’t eat it very often.

i think i might be a vampire. [end of text]
