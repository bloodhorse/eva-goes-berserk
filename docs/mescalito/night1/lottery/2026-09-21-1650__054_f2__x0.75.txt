 something pulls at my heart to come here into your house. i am sent away on my journey with some heavy message to carry back to you, but i don’t know where to begin to look for you. I am in need of guidance, I do not understand.

and so it happens, as you sit there at night, as if a stranger has entered your room. And you ask me, ‘Who are you?’ ‘How do I know who I am?’

I carry life’s burden. How do you think that I am carrying you, so lightly, so full of wonder, all the way. [end of text]
