 perhaps some one wants to come and live inside my head and look out at things.

oh dear, here comes a drop now.

that was a big drop; it will go on falling all the rest of the day, and all the night, too, perhaps for three days and nights together. it would make you quite wet, and then the drops would run down your neck into your ears and you couldn’t hear anything; they’d drip in your eyes, too, and keep you awake. they are quite hot now, but when they are cold they will make you feel so. if it does, we can let it rain.

and I’ll come with you. but what will your mother say? [end of text]
