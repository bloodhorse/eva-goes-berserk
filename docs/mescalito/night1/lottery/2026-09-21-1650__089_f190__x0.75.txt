 tugging. twisting. when it gets caught on the branches of the trees i try to climb them.

when the storm passes i fall.

i’m told not to get up. my mother is worried i will catch a cold. i lay in my bed looking through the window and watch as the sun goes down. i hear the sound of a bell and my stomach growls.

and when i am hungry i know i am alive.

(inspired by: The Storms by Pablo Picasso)
