 then there is silence, and it falls upon my face.

the sun comes through the trees like an arrow and the sky opens like an eye, full of stars. the wind whips my skirts like a sail, the rain is sharp, it stings like the salt of tears on my skin. sometimes it falls gently, and there is silence and i hear it speak.

and there are those who say it never ends. they are lost, without a map or a compass to find their way back, their feet bare and their voices carried in the breeze, their words written in the rain, but it does not fall.
