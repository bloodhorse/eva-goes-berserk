 when i get restless, i try to tear away the strands of my past so i can escape my memories.

i look around me for an open window.

my hands are callused from years of carrying others. but i do not let them go; i carry them as best i can until i have nothing left to give.

i have spent a lifetime standing with my face turned towards the storm. the rain runs down my face and pools in my cupped hands. sometimes the clouds roll in with the promise of tomorrow. other times, they bring a flood.

i am a bird. i sing the song of my people. and sometimes, in the morning, i fly.

my mouth is dry. i want a drink.

my mouth is empty, but i will not be silenced.

i will not let myself forget who i was before you came
