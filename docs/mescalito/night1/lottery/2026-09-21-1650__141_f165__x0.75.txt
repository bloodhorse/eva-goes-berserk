 tugging. teasing. testing.

the winds whisper to me of their travels across the earth and around the globe, they whisper tales of where they’ve been and who they met, where they danced and who they sang to. the winds have become a lover for me; my partner in play, and dance. my muse, and my confidant.

but sometimes the winds come for my hair, and they try and pull me apart, try to blow it all away. they whisper and whisper, until the winds start to win. then i am lost and dishevelled, a mess, and a mess. and it hurts. and the tears flow. but this too shall pass.

this is part 4 of my story, which is part of a much larger one….

I’ve been working through some pretty heavy shit lately.
