 tugging. twisting. but i never complain.

we don’t see each other very often anymore. when we do, we sit together in the kitchen and drink wine. he cooks for me; seafood paella, blackened salmon, risotto with shrimp. we sit around the table, sometimes talking, sometimes listening to the sounds of our bodies: chewing, swallowing, clinking glass, laughter.

and after a few bottles, i get drunk, and begin to cry.

it doesn’t really work like that though. he knows this, but still, he doesn’t want to hear it. and so, i start singing. an old folk song. about the sky and the stars. he pretends not to listen. and then, i take his hand, and whisper to him:

“i remember.”
