 sometimes when it rains, it looks like cats and dogs are playing in it.

but i love my hair so i let it stay long. the rain makes me feel like a child again. the trees watch me as i go by, silently, like the shadows they are. but they cannot see me for they are behind me, and they are silent for they cannot hear me.

so i move on and find the river. i walk down to the banks, i slip on an icy step and land at the bottom in the river. my feet are wet but it does not matter. the fish are standing in the water and are thirsty. the bird are flying overhead. they have been drinking for several days. they fly higher and i fall to the ground. the ground is shaking and the grass is growing everywhere.

the sky is falling down.
