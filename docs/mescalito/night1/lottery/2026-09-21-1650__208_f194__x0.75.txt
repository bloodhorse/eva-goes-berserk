 dragging me to somewhere i don’t want to go. but i just close my eyes and breathe deep.

someone once told me my eyes are like the sky; they can be bright and full of stars. but they are also the deepest of blues, sometimes grey, sometimes green. sometimes i get lost in them and lose all track of time.

my feet are always bare. i feel most at home when i’m outside and barefoot. the grass, the dirt, the rocks. they all hold a secret and if i dig deep enough, i’ll find it. i like the feel of the ground against the soles of my feet, it gives me comfort. i think it likes it too. the dirt smells earthy, rich, like something you can’t find anywhere else, like it has secrets hidden deep inside, buried away.
