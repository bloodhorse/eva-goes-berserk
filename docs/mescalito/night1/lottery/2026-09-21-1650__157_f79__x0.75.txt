 sometimes there is pain. i try to ease its passage with soft words and gentle touch. sometimes i cut away a lock or two for the good of all.

but it is the trees that I love.

if you go in search of them, they are everywhere.
if you walk far enough, you will see their roots spread out in every field.
the leaves whisper to one another; their tales of woe and triumph,
they rustle and shimmer, as though to dance.
and then, they are gone.

you may find them on a distant shore, or in the deep, dark places of the earth, for the winds are many, and their ways mysterious. but know this – their leaves do not fade nor fall.

a daughter of the wildwood [end of text]
