 once when the moon was very full it tugged at me so much it lifted me into the sky.

once, i stood on top of a mountain and saw the whole world. all the different colours and shapes of the people and the creatures, and the rivers and trees and flowers and grasses. and all of us made patterns, and sometimes the colours changed.

there was an orange cat that ran with me through the woods. she liked the sun, so i got warmer and brighter each time she came out. she used to tell me stories.

i think we were good for each other, she would say, you can run so quickly. and i would answer, yes, but you have such soft paws, they leave wonderful marks in the ground. and we would both smile happily and be proud of each other.
