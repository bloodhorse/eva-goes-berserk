 sometimes when the moon is low in the sky i have been known to leave tracks on the lawn.

my nose, which has nothing whatsoever to do with my thoughts, is just the shape of a nose. it is not a thing of beauty, but it does give me some distinction in appearance, for which i am occasionally thankful, and therefore, although i could wish that i had an ordinary nose, such as all other people have, and am in fact, very glad that i did not get a squiggly one like some of them do, still i am contented with the one i have.
