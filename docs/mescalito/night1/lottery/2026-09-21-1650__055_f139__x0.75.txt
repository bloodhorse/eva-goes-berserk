 sometimes there is nothing to do but stay quiet for a while. and my tongue is like that too.

when the weather turns bad i look to my hands and they are like the birds. the sky falls from their eyes, their beaks, and the moon picks up their shoes and walks them over the lawn. i tell the sun what to say and it sings and spins its web on my head.

once the grass was mine and the trees were quiet, all lined up like birds. then the rain was heavy and my hair was pulled back, away from the gate, where the light had been all this time, so i walked it up, down the steps and into my mouth. i said i had to leave early or else, i had to run.
