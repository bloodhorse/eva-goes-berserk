 sometimes when the wind dies down and i hear someone behind me, it sounds just like me.

when i sit in my garden i hear the water trickling through the earth, the birds singing, and i see the sun. i can smell the grass, feel it between my toes. sometimes the sun makes shadows in my hair, and then it feels good to sit by it and rest and watch the flowers growing in my garden.

there is no time but the present moment. what does it matter that we are always living in the present moment? the past is not now, it is a story. i can think of it and imagine it, and when i am sad it goes away. when i think of the future i remember only a story, and it does not come to pass.
