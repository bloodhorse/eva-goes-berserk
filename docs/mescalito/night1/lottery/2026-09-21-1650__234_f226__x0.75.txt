 oh i have wandered by yon mountain stream and i have heard the voice you’re calling me.

my mother and father they have gone to the fair. my brother and sister, they are in bed with the fever, the fire is dying, and the cattle are all gone.

the winds is raging on the mountain side, the moon is shining over the hills. i’ll never see my darling’s eyes, again till the spring time comes. [end of text]
