 sometimes when the moon is very bright or very dark, or on a night without any moon at all, my hair starts to shake.

it is full of memories. it has the smell of the river when it was very high. the smell of fish, and reeds, and rain on mud. the smell of summer when i was a girl, when i sat under the trees and looked up through the branches at the sky. there were so many days then, all alike, they ran into each other.

i sit by the wall. there are no windows here, no doors. it is too old to be inside. it was built for the people who came before us, who went away long ago. i can hear them walking about, far off. but i do not go near the place where they are buried.
