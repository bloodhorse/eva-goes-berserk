 sometimes when the moon is high in the sky i look into it and see stars. i forget what my face looked like without freckles, my feet without blisters, my lips without a crack.

if you had to stay in your body all the time then i’d think you were an idiot.

sometimes he comes down, and lies in front of me with his eyes closed. i feel the earth tremble under us as his hooves start drumming the dirt. his wings come open, open, until they shadow my face from the sun. they are soft. they are the colour of rain. he’s my brother but he’s a horse. he thinks he knows best. we lie like that, listening to each other’s hearts, for days.
