 sometimes when the rain has just stopped it smells sweet. but other times it smells musty or sour.

when the wind blows through my thicket it sounds like an old man with his mouth full of stones. he has a bad stammer, but I love listening to him because he reminds me of my grandfather.

the rain, the rain! sometimes it comes down in bucketfuls. other times it falls more softly, like drizzle. the rain, the rain, i love the rain! i do not care how much of it there is. but it must be natural, of course, not the stuff that you get artificially. and the rain should come down as straight as possible.

and not as the artificial rain does. [end of text]
