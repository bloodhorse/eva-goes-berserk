 when i am tangled, i look into my reflection. i see the leaves dancing on my shoulders and i hear the grass swaying back and forth to the rhythm of the wind, like the way we did at our last school dance, before the rain.

sometimes there is a storm. sometimes the wind brings clouds and sometimes they are all gone, leaving only the sky in my arms. i miss the storm and i hope that one day, he will come back to visit.

Author Notes: This is my first piece of fiction I have written in a while and I really wanted to get it out. It is still a work in progress. [end of text]
