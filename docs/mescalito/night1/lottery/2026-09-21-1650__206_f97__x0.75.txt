someone left their umbrella behind. the rain has picked it up. the clouds are the colour of old bruises, and the lightning is coming in bursts now, like fireworks or blood. i’ve got it open, and am using it to cover myself and my basket of eggs.

i think we should probably start moving south, before the lightning follows us home.

no, it didn’t.
it did not, but that’s the thing, you see –
no, I’m sorry, there were seven islands, but they only had one word for ‘island’, and it wasn’t a number, so it was kind of a mess trying to map it all out.
