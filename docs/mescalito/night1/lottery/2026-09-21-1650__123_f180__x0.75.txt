### 3rd Oct 1961 - 20th Feb 2018

Jacqui Lewis (also known as Motherjacker) was born and raised in South Africa and had been living in Spain for many years, where she lived a life devoted to prayer and mysticism, working on herself with various teachers. Jacqui was one of the most dynamic women of her time, with a fearless energy. Her death on 20th February 2018 after an accident in Morocco shocked many people, including me. It was a very strong reminder to be alive in the moment because nothing is certain.
