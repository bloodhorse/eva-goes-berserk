 perhaps someone is tugging at me with ropes from my hair. this could be true. maybe they want me to come away from here, to take me far into the sea, away from the trees and the leaves. the leaves are angry, they do not wish to see me again.

and i look upon my hair and it is beautiful, full of green grass. i cut it off with a knife that was once my grandfather’s, but is now my own.

and when i go home, i can sit on my chair. my chair is made from the leaves. it is a beautiful thing, the leaves, and it smells so good! i think that perhaps it should become part of me, as the leaves become one with the earth, and i am a part of them. [end of text]
