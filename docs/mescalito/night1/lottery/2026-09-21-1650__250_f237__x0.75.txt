 sometimes when it rains, it looks like someone else is crying. the rain falls on me like tears.

when the rain stops falling from the trees, i walk under the blue umbrellas of my mind. in a house in my head, a voice whispers: “the rain falls on the fields.” it says i can do whatever i want to whomever i choose. it says: “you have power over your enemies, coz all the people around you are sheep. you are the man.” it comes from the mountains of the north. the rain is raining, and the wind is blowing, and the thunder is thundering, and i don’t care.

you know it’s me because my hair is a thicket. it is a great mass of tangles and i am hiding somewhere behind it, waiting for you
