 sometimes when the clouds part and it starts to rain, i look at you through the trees until my lips start to tremble.

there is a gap between your legs. it is wide enough to hide within. i am the size of two of your hands pressed together. if you hold onto it, you might see your reflection. the moon is caught in it every night. we can meet under the stars.

and then i leave again, but for now, i linger. i stay for just a little while longer. i take you home.
