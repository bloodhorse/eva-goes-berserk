 sometimes when the clouds are far away from here i will cut my own hair down to my scalp with scissors, but then my neck will be cold.

once the wind took the clothes off a girl i used to know, it was dark, we were sleeping outside. her dress fell apart in the wind like it was trying to get rid of itself. i watched as her chest came out and the wind pushed it around the sky until the wind was tired, it landed on another girls face.

it has a voice that sounds like the colour green, or what the sun does when it is coming out of a cloud, it has an echo in the bottom of it so it sounds like it’s coming from many different directions all together.

i asked the wind about it, and the wind said, “it is always watching.” it is always watching.
