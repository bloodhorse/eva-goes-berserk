We tried hard to prevent them, but our voices made no impression upon them in that direction, and when we were near enough to see that the effort to recall them was futile we turned off and galloped as hard as possible after them in a different direction, shouting at the top of our lungs. But they were away so fast that by the time we got in touch with them again we found them about five hundred yards in advance of us. As soon as the animals discovered that we were still on the scene, they came to a stop, stood for a while looking about in various directions, as if wondering what it was all meant for, then, slowly, with long and short steps, walked back to where we were awaiting them.
