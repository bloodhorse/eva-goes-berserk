In that brief interval my whole experience in life seemed to pass in review before me; the things done and left undone, those best done, and those worst; the dreams and fancies, the great longings, the secret ambitions I had nursed so tenderly, and hoped for so wistfully, which, nevertheless, had remained but dreams and fancies still, unfulfilled and unrecognised, while the daily work and petty details had absorbed me entirely, until, now, here I was—with a load of regrets and vain imaginings that were more than I could bear, for I had not sufficient faith in anything to carry them with ease. For the first time I understood what the poet had meant when he said, “Ah! that mine ancient might betimes begin.” [end of text]
