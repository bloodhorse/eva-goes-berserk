The black rock towered above me. I felt its presence as strongly as if it was an animate creature. The light played across its surface, glistening on the moist walls. It was a living, breathing thing. A deity incarnate. It seemed to me to be staring back at me, and for a brief moment I imagined that I saw in its shadowy depths an expression of recognition.

Then, all of a sudden, something moved above my head. A stone, perhaps a piece of flint, was hurtling through the air and clattered down to my right. I looked up instinctively, and there I saw them. Men and women were standing at the very edge of the cliff, gazing down at me, and I sensed that their faces were not devoid of compassion.
