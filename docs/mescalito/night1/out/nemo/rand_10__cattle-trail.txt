The Rebel and I dashed toward the spot where the trouble originated, but before reaching there we were forced to ride around a part of the herd. In the confusion we could not determine how many men or how many of our animals had been stampeded, but by the time we had passed beyond the moving herd, the darkness, relieved only by occasional lightning flashes, made it impossible to discover whether we had any cattle remaining, or not.

I left my mount at this point and made an attempt to walk across the range through which the stampeding animals were running. The first thing I knew, I was down, sprawled on the ground, and several hundred pounds of hoofed stock were running over me.
