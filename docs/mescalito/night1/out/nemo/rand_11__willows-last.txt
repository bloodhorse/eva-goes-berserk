What I saw literally paralyzed me! On the shore immediately below, just before it ended in a line of sharp, jagged rocks, I beheld a gigantic black man seated upon a huge rock. His head and chest rested against the sheer side of the mountain wall; his knees were drawn up and his arms were stretched across them in the attitude of a man in deep thought. From his knees downward, his body disappeared, apparently going deep under the earth. I thought at first that I had before me one of the idols of the great temples of which the priests have told us.
