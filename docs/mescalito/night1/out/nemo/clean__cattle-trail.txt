The Rebel and myself hurried toward the scene of the trouble as soon as possible. When we got there Officer’s horse was on his feet but so badly crippled that he could hardly stand. In a moment, Officer, who was unhurt, appeared with his saddle. The Rebel helped him put it on a cow, while I held my mount with my left hand and with my right reached down to assist Officer, when, just as he grasped my arm to mount, the herd, which we thought were then nearly out of sight, came swarming down on us with a rush, making a sound like thunder. The next instant we were surrounded by them.
