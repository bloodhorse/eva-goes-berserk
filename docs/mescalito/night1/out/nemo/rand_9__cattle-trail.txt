The Rebel and I hurried toward the scene of the trouble as fast as possible. When we got there Officer’s horse was on his feet again, and he and his partner, Jack Johnson, were both saddled up, prepared to resume duty. There were no casualties. The boys laughed at their accident, and everything was quiet again.

Just about sunrise the next morning, while we were sitting by our breakfast fire, a cow, which had become separated from the herd, passed by us on her way to join her comrades. The boys all ran to the spot, where, after a great deal of search, the missing animal was discovered. But she had gone so far that the herd did not seem to recognize her. As the result, the herd became alarmed, and stampeded again in a wild panic.
