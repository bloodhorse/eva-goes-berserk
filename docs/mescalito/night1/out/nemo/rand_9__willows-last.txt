And here I came suddenly upon what seemed to be an altar-like space, perhaps six feet across, which was cleared of the usual masses of fallen rock, and in its very centre, surrounded by the huge monoliths, was the skeleton of a human being, lying in perfect repose in full panoply of battle-dress and weapons. And at first glance, I was inclined to think it was not so very ancient, but, when I drew closer, I saw the bones had that peculiar dark colouring, which I now know is the result of prolonged exposure to the weather in this atmosphere.
