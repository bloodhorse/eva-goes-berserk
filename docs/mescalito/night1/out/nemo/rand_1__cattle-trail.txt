The Rebel and I dashed toward the scene of the accident just as John Officer’s horse regained his feet and disappeared like a shot into the darkness. The Rebel, too, was soon beyond my reach, and I was left to make the best speed I could by myself.

The night was pitch black. There was not even a moon to light our way. For more than two hours we rode with little rest or stopping, when, finally, I sighted something moving off at a distance, which looked very much like the rear of the herd, and a moment later, as I gained on it, I found myself following the long tail of the Rebel.
