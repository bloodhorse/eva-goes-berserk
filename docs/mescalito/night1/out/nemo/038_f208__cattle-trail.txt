I looked down at my own feet, but there was no hole for me to fall into, so I looked at John Officer. He had hold of the horse’s reins. He did not need any help from me. His father could take off that saddle. But he could not get up till the others were helped, for when he tried he could only get one of his arms free. It took two of us to get him on his feet, and as we were leading him up, holding his hand, we both saw John Chisum’s horse run into the same trap where Mr. Officer’s had fallen.
