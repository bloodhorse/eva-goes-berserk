And here I came suddenly upon what appeared to be an altar or table, cut out of the natural rock, which looked to me like a stone at a blacksmith’s anvil, where some god had stood by night to smite invisible things with his hammer. There, standing between me and the wind, I saw that the spirit was an elemental one of storm, and, though the elements obeyed him, he did not always do their bidding.
