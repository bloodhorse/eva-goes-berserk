The poor beasts were very restless as they fled, and soon began to make a wide circle which didn’t last for long. A short while later I found that we were heading for the open plains where I felt sure my herd would be safe from attack. By the time my men arrived on the scene the damage had been done and the herd had taken flight. After being a member of this group of ranchers for six years I have never been able to discover such a disaster.
