The Rebel and myself dashed toward the spot of commotion as fast as possible. By this time the herd had cleared the point where John fell, and was on its way, running fast. As it was too late for me to do anything except follow and keep them headed in a straight course, I galloped on ahead, shouting at the top of my voice. As they came along I saw them through the gloom of the night, and guided them along by the sound of their running feet. After about two miles of this I caught up with the leader, a big old whiteface bull, and heeled him on. We thus continued until we reached the next guard. As it was daylight now and all the cattle were under control, the men on duty had a pretty good idea what happened.
