In the centre of it was an immense stone about the size and shape of a man’s body and which is now called “The Cairn.” This is a sort of burial place and a sanctuary where the worshippers would assemble in their primitive rites of religion.

A small circle of upright stones encircle the central stone. As you will see from the accompanying diagram the stones are set in an exact circle round the centre stone. There is one in each direction and they are arranged so as to face the centre. It is a regular pentagon, or a five cornered figure, the shape that is always used for ritual purposes.
