When Officer had got back into his saddle, and I had got a clear idea of where the cattle were, we set off in chase after them. For several miles the ground was comparatively open, but the night being as dark as it was, it was some time before I could pick up their trail in the starlight; and by this time they had got well out on a narrow neck between two large prairies. I tried every way I could think of to head them off, but to no purpose whatever. The only thing for me to do now was to continue to follow in the trail until daylight.
