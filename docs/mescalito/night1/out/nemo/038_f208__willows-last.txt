When I had got here I stopped to look back at the little house. I knew it was my little house; and the door in it was like a picture-book. And there were two pretty figures inside saying their prayers to the good lady, while her white dress went up and down as she said them.

It was all quite quiet, but the nice new story-book was going to come next, for the lady said so. The lady said, “Let the little children come to me,” and my sister did, and that was all.
