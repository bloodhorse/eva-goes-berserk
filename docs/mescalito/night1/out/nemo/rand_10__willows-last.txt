Then came a sudden stop. For what I saw caused every atom of my body to tremble. A figure had detached itself from the headland and was slowly approaching me.

I was looking at an old man, incredibly bent, almost doubled up by the weight of centuries; his hair, long and dishevelled, fell over his face in wild profusion; and his eyes gleamed in the dusk with a baleful fire.

The sight of him, though it struck me with horror, was not unexpected, for in my mind, from the very first moment, I had pictured to myself a figure like this coming from that spot and walking along the shore.
