It is impossible to say how many of them there were. The Reb, who was nearest us, got over us quickly, and he must have brought half a hundred more, while we were struggling to our feet. But they were only on us an instant and then, so far as I am concerned, they disappeared in an absolute instant, like a flash. In another minute, all was still again, and as the sun was coming up, the great prairie was like a vast garden in full bloom.
