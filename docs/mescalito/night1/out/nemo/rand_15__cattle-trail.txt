The Rebel and myself dashed forward in hot pursuit, but before we could overtake them they were across the creek and over a rise of ground on the other side. We were hard pushed to keep in touch with the fleeing mass. The Rebel seemed to think he would ride me off the saddle. We gained a point within a hundred yards of them, when all of a sudden, they changed their direction, making straight for the water again, and diving into it like a flock of wild fowl. They swam directly across, and gained a low point on the other side, where, like a mass of frightened game, they disappeared.
