And there, like some gigantic sentinel facing outward, was one of those great boulders that have so often been mistaken for the Menhirs of old, or the Cromlechs, Druids’ Altars and sacrificial stones. This huge mass of granite, as big as an ordinary farm-house, looked like a vast animal, couchant in eternal sleep; while, through a fissure in its side, like a cave, one could catch the gleam of white sand, as if the spirit of the place, who had once been human, were now awaiting the call of her mistress.
