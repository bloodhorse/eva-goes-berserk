The Rebel and I dashed forward to round them in; but it was useless; they ran in all directions as though they were possessed of demons, and in less than a minute had disappeared from our view. I rode to where the herd had been standing and called aloud to The Rebel and Officer. Not a word reached me from either in reply. It seemed as though my comrades had been swallowed by some mighty chasm, but, with my voice growing weaker and weaker, I still cried out their names. Then I dismounted and sat down to await the return of the guard, but the thought that Officer or The Rebel might be hurt kept me awake.

As soon as it was light enough for us to find our way to the bed of the Atascosa, which was but a hundred yards away, we mounted again
