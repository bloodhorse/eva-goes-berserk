As I did so I became aware that there were sounds in the air — sounds more like the melody of far-away singing bells blowing in my direction than anything else. They were faint, but they grew louder every instant and seemed to come from somewhere ahead of me.

A few moments later I saw that there was light ahead of me as well as sound. And it came not from the direction of the stars nor of the glow of the city of the gods. This light was yellow and wavering, and as I went on I made out a figure in it. Then the light and the figure began to take shape; and presently I saw that the figure was robed, and the robe was saffron. Then the robed one became clearer still and took a distinct shape.
