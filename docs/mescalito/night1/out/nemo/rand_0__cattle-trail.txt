They had become frightened before Officer’s fall, and when he called for help from the ground, they became almost unmanageable, rushing and pushing in every direction, until we began to fear the possibility of some being injured, and even the necessity of sacrificing the entire herd in order to stop them.

But our worst apprehensions were soon allayed. Our little cowboy, who was never at a loss on any occasion, quickly dismounted and took off his bridle, giving the boy a chance to show us how he could “handle” the herd. We saw at once that he had an exceptional faculty for quieting wild and excitable animals, and in less than a minute the entire herd stood quiet as tame lambs
