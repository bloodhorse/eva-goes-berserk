At once my whole mind became alert. Something had moved behind one of those boulders that stood at the edge of the stream, and a terrible fear of what might be coming out of it sent my blood tingling along every nerve of my body.

I don’t think I even breathed, and then, slowly, out stepped a woman with flaming red hair.

She came towards me with slow, purposeful strides, and when she reached my side she put her hand up to mine and took hold of it in an unmistakable sign of friendship. As I turned, I found myself looking into a pair of clear green eyes that regarded me without expression and with a steadfast gaze.

A cold shiver passed through me, and then the red-haired woman began to laugh.
