It was only when I reached the clearing that I finally noticed how many people had gathered around to watch us approach. They seemed to be everywhere! Everywhere you turned, there were people standing or sitting or lying about with their heads bowed, hands clasped together in prayer or meditation. And every so often, someone would rise up from behind one of these shrines or altars to perform some sort of ritualistic act or another — usually involving chants and incense — before settling back down again.

In this region of space-time, however, such behavior is not considered particularly noteworthy; it simply comes with living among civilizations as diverse as ours
