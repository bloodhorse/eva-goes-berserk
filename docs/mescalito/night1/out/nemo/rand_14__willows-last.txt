There’s something about a weird story set in a remote, lonely location, away from all human life, that is inherently spooky.

Take the story “The Yellow Sign” by Robert W. Chambers, in which a painter goes out looking for inspiration and gets much more than he bargained for; or Arthur Machen’s novella The Great God Pan, in which a woman, isolated and alone, makes contact with a malevolent presence; or M. R. James’ story “Casting the Runes” which follows the unfortunate David Menney as he leaves his house in pursuit of his enemies, only to end up at a secluded lodge in a forest in the north of England.
