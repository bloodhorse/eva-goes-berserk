The noise, and then, slowly, with infinite caution, as if she felt her way in the darkness, she rose, came to my side, took my arm, and walked with me in silence back along the path until we came out once more into the bright sunlight, which was still unclouded in the heavens. The look on her face when it fell upon me was one of absolute terror.

“I saw him! I saw him!” she said, and clung to me so closely that she hurt me.
