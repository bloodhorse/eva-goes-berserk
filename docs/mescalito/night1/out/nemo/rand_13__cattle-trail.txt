I knew nothing of what was transpiring until I saw the whole herd break, as if by some common signal, and dash off like a cyclone towards the river. There were but two men awake to hold them, and these on horseback. When I reached the edge of the stream the dust was flying and I could not see a cow, so thickly did they cover the ground. A minute later they had passed down the stream and around a bend, out of sight. I followed the river a short distance and then took a cut across the plain, hoping to head them and hold them in front until daylight; but they were too far ahead, and in spite of the efforts of The Rebel and myself to get ahead of them
