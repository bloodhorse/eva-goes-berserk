The Rebel and I dashed forward to stop them, but before we could reach them they had left us behind by fully a half-mile. My horse, an old trail animal, however, never gave me a moment of anxiety, but in spite of the darkness he seemed to have the location of every tree and cactus plant, while The Rebel’s mount was doing his best to get home by the shortest possible route.

I followed the herd, which kept on going until it finally became impossible to keep my horse out of the chaparral and cacti. In a little time I had to dismount, but as I was doing so the herd came into sight and passed me with a speed which seemed impossible for a bunch of steers.
