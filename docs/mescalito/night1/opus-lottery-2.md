## the population so far

72 read: **2 dead** (053, 065), **4 obsessions** (041 the end, 046 the dead, 048 the hedge, 067 *and I*), **31 genre switches**, **24 nothing**, **11 candidates** (039, 052, 054, 055, 059, 086, 095, 099, 107, 108, 110). the strong end was 22 dead of 39. here it's 2 of 72, so the cliff sits right at rank ~039. below it, 0.75 is a comfortable dose on this seed, and the problem stops being death and becomes the page leaving the room. the genres are the sixty-token read's switchboard: blogs, fanfic, forum, scripture, fake attributions, a wikia footer, tweets. early endings are the rule, not an ender trait: 41 of 72 stop before the cap (sober: 1 of 5), and most of the *nothing* pages are one to three good lines and a stop. any push here seems to shorten her. twenty-seven pages open on *sometimes*. repeats across the lottery so far: the child's diary (`038`, `066`), the about-me page (`029`'s turn, then `075`), police twice (`081`, `102`), proverbs twice (`082`, `084`). two things to keep an eye on: `088_f77` writes in the tuned chatbot's house style, and the seed's objects often survive a genre switch (the black cloud as Exodus's pillar in `105`, the comb in the police poem in `102`, the storm's compliment quoted as evidence in `081`).

fan first: **`107_f159`**, because it is the only page where she becomes something else and stays it for 170 tokens, a tree in a line of trees with a river taking them in turn. it isn't in any genre I can name, and five draws will show whether *the self as a lineage of trees* is the direction or luck. **`099_f234`**, because the inversion (the storm asks her for directions) is exactly the one-clause break that holds, and it held for a paragraph. **`055_f139`**, the most sustained physics-weather so far, to see whether it is a register the direction owns. **`110_f142`** comes next, deliberately after those three: it is the strongest page in the slice, but it is voices' ground, so the first question is whether it is a cousin of `169_f199` before it counts as a new substance.
