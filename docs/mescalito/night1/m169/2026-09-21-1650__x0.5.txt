 like fingers. sometimes the wind plays with my hair, tosses it into knots. i wish my hair would be free like it used to be when i was little, but that’s just a dream.

at night, the storm makes noises, like a scream or a cry of pain. it has no tongue. but its body is covered in scars. i wonder where they came from. did it suffer from an illness? did someone do this to it? or maybe it tried to cut itself. but the scars don’t matter. because they are part of it and it would be a different person without them.

i know that storms can be very destructive. but there is always something good behind all of it, something beautiful.

the wind brings the rain. and the rain washes away the dirt. the dirt covers our faces, covers our bodies
