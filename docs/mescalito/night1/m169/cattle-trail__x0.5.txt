The stampede began toward us, and was not far distant when The Rebel rode up at a gallop with his gun, crying at the top of his voice that he was going for his horse, and would rejoin the pursuit. Officer’s horse had picked himself up and had started toward his master’s side, where the saddle had become displaced; but when he saw The Rebel ride away in search of his horse, he also decided to follow suit. By this time Officer and I, having lost control of the herd, were doing our best to stop their course, but to no purpose; the situation was hopeless. In a moment or two they had disappeared into the darkness in the direction of San Antonio.
