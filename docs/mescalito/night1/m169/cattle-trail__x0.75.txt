A portion of the cattle had never before participated in a stampede, and it is quite possible that the shock of being aroused by so violent an interruption of the dreamless sleep was sufficient in itself to have rendered them temporarily irrational. Be this as it may, they paid no attention to our cries, which, however, we endeavored to render as pleasant and reassuring as possible; but with little success, as may be gathered from the fact that all of us, without exception, were compelled to leave the ranch on the next day at ten o’clock in the morning, accompanied by our horses, which were found to be in a most unsatisfactory state of mind, owing chiefly to the unseasonable hour
