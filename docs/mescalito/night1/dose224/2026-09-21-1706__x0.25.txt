So at last the time came. A big ship was going on a voyage, and the
brass-witch put all her brass in a bag and went off with it, leaving
the children with their father. But her husband got to thinking about
what his wife might be doing, so he got some old brass out of an
attic-chest and went after her. The big ship was already out to sea
when the two of them met on the quay. It is said that one of the crew
looked over the side, saw something shining up at him, and thought that
