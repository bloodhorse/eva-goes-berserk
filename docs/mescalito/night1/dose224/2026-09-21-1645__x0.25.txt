 sometimes when the weather is good it sounds almost normal, but we have no weather control so most days are grey, or brownish-red. the sky has turned from the color of rain to a sickly blue that no one seems to notice, like a dirty ceiling in an apartment block where you have no neighbors.

the tower is on top of the hill. there are two hills. no one can remember what was there before the towers, only the tower, and now that other tower. when the wind is blowing you hear it on both sides, from both directions. it doesn't make much difference which one you go to, unless you have a bad memory. most of us have lost track of where we are anyway.

some say the tower is on the left, but that depends on the direction of the wind and how far the hill rises up
