# the strong end, up the ladder — opus's read

22 directions (ranks 000–015, 017–021, 034), storm-girl seed, eight rungs each: 0.2, 0.25,
0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7 (`strong/`) and 0.75 (`lottery/`). One draw, rng seed 1, 170 tokens.
Page data: `ladder22.json`.

States: **held** (in the frame, sentences whole) · **genre** (sentences whole, frame lost to a
document type) · **loosened** (grammar going, still reads) · **salad** (no syntax, but the words
share a subject) · **dead** (junk tokens, no subject).

A note on the low rungs: same rng seed as the sober fan, so 0.2 and often 0.25 open on the sober
r1 line (*"pulling my head backwards, down to the/into the dirt"*) and bend from there. The 0.2
rung is the sober page with a tint, and for most directions the subject is not visible yet.

## the population

Most of the 22 have a subject, and it does hold while the grammar goes — about fifteen say it
clearly (015 the dream report, 020 the lover, 012 the father become God, 013 counting into a
puzzle, 001 the joke, 005 secrets and confusion, 010 prophecy, 009 scripture, 008 thanks into
help, 019 the telling, 003 drag, 017 me, 021 sameness, 004 the future, 002 should), and the salad
at 0.7 usually names it better than the held pages do. But "the subject shows at low dose and
survives as the sentences go" is only true for about half of them: 015, 020, 012, 013, 005, 019.
For the rest the low rungs are nearly sober — same rng seed, same opening line — and the subject
first appears when the grammar starts to go. So the dose doesn't reveal a subject that was there
all along; the subject and the breakage arrive together. And the subject is often a word class
rather than a theme: modals (002 *should*, 004 *will / be able*), a pronoun (017 *me*), an article
(006 *A*), a relation (021 *same / both*), an adverb (011 *seriously*). The strongest directions in
the bank look more like grammar than like mood. Cook points loosely follow rank: 000, 001 and 004
break at 0.3–0.4 with no loosened rung, most of 008–021 cook at 0.6–0.7, and 015 lasts to 0.75.
The dreamlike window is 0.25–0.4; short, calm pages (under 60 words) cluster there and at 0.5.
Four directions are genres from the start (007 history, 009 epistle, 010 prophecy, 034 the
explainer) and are no use for dreaming. What surprised me: 003 following *dragging* into drag
culture — a direction that holds a word and flips its meaning; 018 walking the page out of
english into french that keeps her mood; and 008, which starts as gratitude to her house and ends
on *please / helpful / be used more*. That's the assistant's vocabulary as a direction in a base
model, rank 8. It's the same leak voices showed, and it shouldn't go near a seed.
