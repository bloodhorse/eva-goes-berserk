 maybe there was someone in that book too. someone with their arm up and pointing to the tower and saying see, you've got to go in there and tell your name to the wind in the tower and if it doesn't answer, why, you can stay outside and grow old with the dandelions.

so i tell the line again, louder this time: "my name is beck. my name is beck."

the line makes a noise, as though it were clearing its throat, or a baby making itself heard. there's a voice inside, all creaks and wails, and the sound of an old bell in the back of it. but it says:

"beck? i know a beck."

and the wind stirs in the dandelion tops. [end of text]
