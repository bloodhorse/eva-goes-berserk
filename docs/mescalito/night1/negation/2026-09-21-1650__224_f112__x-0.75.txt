 Sometimes when the wind is strong I must tie myself to the tree to keep myself from being blown away.

when the rain falls i hear the water trickling down the leaves. I watch for a long time the little streams of water, till the trees are dripping wet. But they don’t mind. They laugh and talk together, and always have something new to tell me.

When the rain falls in large drops and splashes into my face, the birds and animals hide themselves under cover. Then the stormy winds come out of the clouds to look for them, and if they can find them, they throw them about in all directions, and make them quite unable to help themselves.
