_This book was inspired by the works of the great poets of the twentieth century who sought to define our spiritual crisis in a scientific age--by T. S. Eliot, Wallace Stevens, Robert Penn Warren, and Allen Ginsberg._

_The title is taken from Eliot's "Little Gidding":_

_We shall not cease from exploration_

_And the end of all our exploring_

_Will be to arrive where we started_

_And know the place for the first time.__"We have come to the conclusion that, if one is to represent an image, this can only be done in the form of a surface, i.e., two-dimensional space." --Stephen Hawking and Roger Penrose_
