 sometimes when the wind whips up i hear voices in it. it sounds like my father shouting but i cannot be sure because my ears are clogged with blood. it is hard to know what is real and what is imagined, whether it is a trick or a gift.

my grandmother has a pair of binoculars, a leather case that opens with a gold snap. she stands at the edge of the lawn and peers down the scope, scanning the horizon for some distant ship. she can see a thousand miles away. her vision is as sharp as a blade. she has a collection of silverware and a box of stamps. her head is like a globe. she reads me passages from the bible about how god created the universe, and i think, he is speaking to me now. it seems impossible that she is related to me
