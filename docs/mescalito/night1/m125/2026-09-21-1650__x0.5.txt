 something wants to drag me back.

my legs are long and covered in bruises. my nails are dirty with the muddy soil from the field where i played.

in the distance i see a train leaving. it is a white line stretching out before it disappears behind a hill. i imagine it is taking everyone away.

when the night falls, my mother comes looking for me. her hair is combed back and tied at the neck, her body wrapped in a blanket. she finds me lying in the long grass and carries me home.

the day will never be over, i think, if i could only fall asleep. [end of text]
