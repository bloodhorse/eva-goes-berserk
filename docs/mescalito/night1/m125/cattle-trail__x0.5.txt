The Rebel and myself dashed toward the spot to head them off, but without avail. They were going too fast for us, and so we turned to follow, hoping to be able to check their speed. We had proceeded in this way possibly half a mile when all at once a cloudburst opened over our heads, completely drenching us from head to foot. As there was no possibility of stopping the herd until the rain abated, or daylight came, we determined to follow them, keeping them within reach in order that the boys might not get too far away, as it was of prime importance to get together as soon as possible to hold council and arrange some plan by which to keep the herd quiet during the night.
