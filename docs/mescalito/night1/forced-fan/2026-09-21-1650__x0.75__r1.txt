 pulling my body closer, like a rope wrapped around the sky. and i fall into it like water down the river.

there are mountains in the distance. they are like giants made of rock. they have seen it all. the storms, the black skies full of stars. i climb to the top and look over the edge, feel the rain on my skin. i feel as if my body were made of rain. like rain, but with less of the ocean. like rain in the night. the stars are bright in the sky. the rain falls to the ground. the world turns. it is raining. and we are all part of it. like an avalanche in slow motion. and the mountains rise again and the sky is full of fire, the ground shakes and the dust settles like ashes on my face.

my heart
