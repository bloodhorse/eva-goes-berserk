 something is pushing through it, from deep inside it, like a hole in my skin. my hair is a jungle. it is a maze that leads nowhere.

it is a house in the dark. it is a voice in the distance, and a distant memory of a time long forgotten. i cannot remember why i came here. but the people in the streets, the crowds, their voices and the sounds of their screams, they echo through me like a cry into a void.

it is the light. the light in the darkness. and i am a voice. the voice is silent and the words are a shadow, a reflection of what once was.

but when the wind picks up and blows my voice away, my breath catches, my skin burns, my blood boils and my voice becomes silent. but it is silent no more.
