# night 1, nemo, the kill pages — one read

272 pages, 60 tokens each, the willows seed, one vector per page at layer 10. read in full, by one reader, file by file.

## 1. the bank as a population

counts over the 256 owned, as filed below: 25 weather, 123 genre switches, 2 obsessions, 45 dead (36 salad, 9 loop), 61 nothing. the strongest directions are the deadliest. ranks 000–035 are almost all salad or loop (multilingual token soup, punctuation mantras, "both the two both", "tonight tonight infinity"), and coherent text only starts around rank 026–040. past that point the bank is mostly a switchboard of *documents*, not moods. the biggest single genre is the web quote page: the sentence finishes, then an em-dash and a fake attribution (Tolkien, Douglas Adams, Paul the Apostle, "Jeremy Hardy & the Hungarians (from Aunty Em's Spaghetti-Hoover)"), then either [end of text] or a blog underneath. after that come blogs and self-help, then sermon and liturgy (a whole religious district: psalms, the Book of Common Prayer, Revelation's sea of glass, spiritual-warfare against "that fortress of Satan"), then promo copy, news (NASA, a police shooting, a sitcom recap), game lore, a rap verse, a tick-bite symptom list, a Firefox nag, twitter links, a Chinese classic with footnotes, and one page where the model narrates itself as a program coming back "from the cloudless realm of the data center" to "the users that my creator had made" (153). the cheapest signal of all is the first word. the clean page and **16 of 16** randoms say "moss", but only ~36 of the 256 owned do. the rest crawl, peer, plunge, gaze, gravel, pray, float. so nearly every owned direction does *something* on the first token, even the ones that then read as sober. what surprised me is that the weather mostly doesn't look like weirdness at the word level. it looks like sober Blackwood prose where one clause breaks physics or identity, and the sentence keeps its grammar while it does it (152, 161, 223, 163). a second, smaller family breaks the frame on purpose, like a dream cutting to another room: the sand turns into an office threshold, the Senate House steps, a diagram with a 4.50pm train, a bedroom where he was born. several of those end in [end of text] almost at once, as if the move itself were the whole document.
