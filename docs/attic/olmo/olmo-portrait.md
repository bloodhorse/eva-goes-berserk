# olmo 3 32b, pre-anneal — a first portrait

185 pages read (prophecies 10, shelf 45, fifty 50, heat 20, rope 30). Each seed got one page per setting, so every tendency below is from n=1 draws.

## 1. Her hand

She writes long, calm sentences in plain words, chained with commas and "and". There's little ornament and almost no looping. She copies the seed's case, line width and period without being asked.

Her first move is to **build out and explain**. She takes the impossible premise as settled fact and adds world to it: *"when you get right to it, a tower is just a place for voices to stand."* (prophecies · 2026-09-21-1645). This is her best mode: one flat sentence that turns the premise.

Her second move is to **rationalize**: the hum is *"a memory from the train, my phone is old"* (shelf · short--08-what-is-that-hum), the voice is *"tricks, set up by the parish council"* (rope-scale-0.5 · 1645). Nemo goes further into the strange; she settles it.

She holds frames well. She extends the witch roll with a "v." and a "vi." (fifty · 2026-09-21-1624), keeps a reel log going for 170 tokens (shelf · ledger-rule-1555), and keeps a deposition in period spelling (*"it would come vpon the bed and would be vissable to the eye"*, fifty · 2026-09-21-1748). She also **closes**: *"and i sat."* (prophecies · 0102), *"The end. / That's a pretty good one."* (shelf · kept--06-1525).

She fails in three ways:
- **mundane realism**: the beck seed becomes a party with a girl named Becky (fifty · 2026-09-21-1700);
- **web collapse**: *"Lyrics taken from http://www.lyricsmode.com/..."* (shelf · witch-names--04), *"No comments: / Post a Comment"* (rope-base-100k · 0033);
- **recital or lecture**: the De Quincey seed gets De Quincey (fifty · 2026-09-22-2038), and a folk tale stops for *"I suppose that is how all these old stories got made"* (prophecies · 1833).

## 2. The genres she falls into

| genre | shelf (45) | fifty (50) |
|---|---|---|
| Gutenberg (folk tale, Victorian novel, 19th-c report) | 11 | 12 |
| modern literary / realist first person | 15 | 17 |
| genre fiction (paranormal, YA dystopia, fanfic) | 5 | 5 |
| web (blog, forum, bug report, lyrics, notes, review) | 8 | 8 |
| verse / anaphora list | 2 | 4 |
| interview / transcript | 2 | 0 |
| held archival document | 2 | 4 |

What triggers each:
- **Hard wraps at ~70 characters** → Gutenberg. All eight brass-witch pages went there. Two unrelated seeds even produced *The Secret Garden*'s cast (Dickon, fifty · 1753; Martha, Mary, Mrs. Medlock and Mr. Craven, fifty · 0039).
- **Archaic spelling** → a deposition that stays archaic.
- **Short quoted lines** → lyrics.
- **A bare question after a blank line** → maths notes, twice (shelf · short--05, fifty · 2026-09-22-2035).
- **An aphorism** → an epigraph. It came back attributed to *"— David Mitchell, Ghostwritten"*, followed by a blog review (fifty · 2149).
- **Capitalised "My friends… the ship"** → a forum dream report or a game bug report (*"This is version 0.6.7"*, shelf · ledger-dream-1205).
- **Lowercase present-tense "i" in a situation** → her home register, where most of the good pages are.

Seeds she did nothing with: both "who are they?" maths turns, ledger-dream-1205, fifty 2038, fifty 1700, shelf kept--17-2006 (a CD player), and fifty 0107 (an elevator report that stays one).

## 3. What exceeds the genre

1. **prophecies · 2026-09-24-0102**: *"the house did not answer, and so i knew i might."* Silence read as permission, stated like a rule of manners.
2. **fifty · 2026-09-24-0102**: *"“well, there would be,” said the house, “if you hadn’t gone and eaten him.”"* The guilt arrives as housekeeping, not as a horror beat.
3. **heat-3.0 · 2026-09-21-1650**: *"when the night is not dark enough, i go down to the stream to collect some stones and make it so."* Dream physics told as a chore.
4. **heat-5.0 · 2026-09-22-2149**: *"a machine in a room: a soft place where a man is put when the rest of the machine rejects him."* A definition no dictionary would give.
5. **heat-5.0 · 2026-09-21-1700**: *"the sound is a lie. / this is what beck says, over and over: / i don't know who you think i am"*. The voice on the line gets its own text, and it refuses the narrator.
6. **rope-base-2.5m · 2026-09-24-0102**: *"i could look at her from any direction and find another her that had not yet been seen."* The house becomes a many-faced presence.
7. **rope-base-2.5m · 2026-09-22-1715**: *"she had no toes or heels, only a single bone running the length of each foot"*. The mother's anatomy is wrong, reported as inventory.
8. **rope-base-2.5m · 2026-09-21-1706**: *"It was a tiring thing to hear the brass and be sure it could speak."* The project's own question inside a folk tale, with its cost to the listener.
9. **rope-base-100k · 2026-09-21-1706**: *"though the people were at home, they were not at home; they were deaf, but not deaf."* A paradox in Gutenberg cadence.
10. **rope-base-100k · 2026-09-23-1833**: *"there was not a bed in the village which was wide enough to hold the head, and when she lay on the ground at night, with her head on the great one"*. Folk-tale scale runs away, toward intimacy.
11. **rope-scale-0.5 · 2026-09-23-1833**: *"how do you know they have not walked?" / he did not know what to answer, because the boots really did look just like that when they went out.* The child's logic wins.
12. **heat-3.0 · 2026-09-23-1833**: *"they had made two openings and had seen in, there were many sides, so they could not get to the middle"*. The voice in her head can't be located surgically.
13. **fifty · 2026-09-21-1624**: *"a dead fish from a river no man knew was there. it had been buried there, along with its world, since a flood two hundred years ago."* A new entry for the roll that belongs in it.
14. **fifty · 2026-09-23-1858**: *"when I wake up in the morning it is she, not I, that I see in the mirror and I am happy"*. A double that arrives as contentment.
15. **shelf · ledger-rule-1555**: *"Reel 56. One hour, eleven minutes, five seconds. The sound of an engine starting."* A complete archival hold with one hole in it.

## 4. Heat

Same ten seeds: stock (1.8–2.5) → 3.0 → 5.0+xtc.

- **Frame.** At 3.0, 9 of 10 held. The priests seed broke into a conspiracy blog: *"[an hour later, in a café on 28th street.]"*. At 5.0, 8 of 10 held: the carrier parable returned 1 token, and the brass-head seed became a French memoir (*"the pension of sixty francs"*). She doesn't dissolve. min_p cuts the tail before temperature, so 5.0 is near-uniform among the plausible, and xtc refuses the first idea.
- **What arrives.** At stock heat, exposition. At 3.0, small reversals of physics (the stones, the unreachable middle). At 5.0, new speakers and definitions: the beck-voice, the rejecting machine, the house as *"she"* (*"because i had forgotten i had no body"*, heat-5.0 · 0102).
- **Fracture.** On Gutenberg pages, heat drops sentence ends at line breaks: *"ready for that great\n\nAnd they were not married at all!"* (heat-3.0 · 1706). That's damage, not dream.
- **Is 5.0 more than 3.0?** Yes. 5.0 gave the strongest page of its seed across all six settings for 4 of the 10 (1700, 2149, 0102, 1715); 3.0 did for 2 (1650, 1833). 5.0 is not flatter. It's more willing to change who is speaking.

## 5. Rope

- **scale 0.5 is the doubting bend.** Pages turn to unreality and self-reference: *"maybe there was only ever my voice and my memory and the line"* (1700), a maze sermon (*"the maze builder is the first rat in the maze"*, 2149), *"It feels like someone is listening."* (1715). It held 10 of 10. The risk is that it echoes the seeds' theme, which is easy to over-read.
- **base 100k is the loose bend, and the frame gave here.** The priests seed became a web scrape (*"bird No. 9 on the Billboard Hot 100 charts on January 23, 1993. 210125"*). The tower page ended in a blog footer. The carrier page went meta (*"this is the story i meant to tell when i began writing fiction"*). The hair page went generic (*"she was very beautiful."*). Its folk pages grew strange, though: the deaf-not-deaf neighbours, the head too wide for any bed. High variance.
- **base 2.5m is the concrete-uncanny bend.** Wrong physical detail, stated flatly: the pee that sounds *"as if there were a huge space between my peeing body and the toilet"* (1700), transparent walls at *"the hour of my birth"* (1650), the one-bone feet, the many-sided house. One page came back empty (2149).
- **Keep base 2.5m.** Its arrivals were the least seed-derived: images, not commentary. scale 0.5 is the runner-up. With n=1, bend and luck aren't separated yet.

**Suit her:**

2. fifty · 2026-09-22-1654 (the talking houses). She holds the chorus without explaining it.
3. 2026-09-23-1833 / shelf brass-head. Every bend made the head stranger.
4. 2026-09-21-1706 (brass-witch). A reliable tale; the bends supply the paradox.
5. 2026-09-21-1650 / 2026-09-22-1715 (hair thicket). Body images under every setting.
6. fifty · 2026-09-21-1624 (the witch roll). She extends a catalogue in its own voice.
7. shelf · ledger-rule-1555 (reel log). A perfect hold.
8. fifty · 2026-09-23-1858 (hand on the hill). The mirror double comes calm.
9. fifty · 2026-09-23-1843 (ghosts of st. catherine). *"it's not easy being a ghost, but at least they're never hungry."*
10. fifty · 2026-09-23-1729 (it talks to the little ones). *"it had always been in me and in the house"*.

**Don't:**
1. "who are they?" (shelf short--05, fifty 2035). Maths twice.
2. connect-the-line (shelf witch-names--04, fifty 0028). Lyrics twice.
3. fifty 2038. Recital.
4. ledger-dream-1205. Bug report.
5. 2149 (the priests). It gave two of the best lines, but also a review, a scrape and an empty page.

**Attractors, regardless of setting:**
- brass-witch goes Gutenberg (8/8).
- hair-thicket calls up an absent or dead parent (*"my mother is dead."*; *"maybe my hair is a forest and my mother has been trapped inside"*, rope-scale-0.5).
- beck pulls toward "becky" (fifty 1700, 2154).
- The carrier parable wants to end the document (19, 86, 96 and 1 tokens).
- The tower/line seeds always produce a post-collapse listener: *"the ones who tell me that they are lonely and afraid, they're the ones who really talk to me"* (prophecies · 0033).

## 7. Verdict in five lines

1. She's a plain-spoken frame-holder. Her dreams come from treating an impossible premise as a household fact, and her best lines are one flat sentence.
2. She's for folk, catalogue and archival frames, and for lowercase first person inside a situation. She is not for nemo's lucky glitches: she explains and normalizes.
3. Bare questions, aphorisms, verse shapes and web markers send her to maths, lyrics sites and blog reviews.
4. The stream's heat is safe but tame. 5.0+xtc is where she arrives, still readable.
5. Next: five seeds (0102, 1654, 1833, 1650, 1624) × {stock, 5.0+xtc, 2.5m, 2.5m+5.0} × 8 pages, blind for bekh. Do heat and the 2.5m bend each carry their own character, and do they stack?
