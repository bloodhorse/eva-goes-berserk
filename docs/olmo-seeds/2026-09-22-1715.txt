sometimes there is a storm, and i stand above the rooftops. it is a black cloud full of red lights like lanterns, like a great tent full of torches. the wind picks up, blows the leaves across the path, pulls at my dress and throws my words over my shoulder, over the treetops, across the fields.

i tell it everything about my day, what i have seen, and it tells me stories. once, it said i was beautiful. and that my skin was the colour of the sea.

my hair is a thicket. it is matted from the damp air, full of twigs, dry grass and small stones. it is thick enough that i could hide myself inside it and no one would know me. sometimes it gets tangled and it feels as if something is pulling at me.
