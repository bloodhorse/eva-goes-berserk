walking on; but there were always more people behind the others who did not wait at all.
I began to hear singing; a deep song with high notes. I reached the end of the town; the people in
front of me had vanished into a wall of buildings.
I came out again on the other side, where a bridge spanned the river; it was night there, but I
could see that there was no one there, so I walked across it; when I came to the other bank the
houses began to grow thin, and I went on, until the street was only a few feet wide. The people
who were hurrying past now began to come from the other direction too.

When the street had turned to a path, the singing came to me; the singing grew louder and
stronger.
