Then I remembered the room on the upper floor. It was the same room I had slept in when I was a boy.

“I want to see your husband,” I said, “and the girl.” It was as if I was expecting the answer, though there was nothing for me to know. For how could it know, and why would it talk to me of its daughter? There had been no girl in my family.

“You are very old,” the house said. “Your hair has turned gray, and you cannot walk so quickly any more. But I am here and I have never left the place. I have only slept a few times and only for a few minutes at a time.
