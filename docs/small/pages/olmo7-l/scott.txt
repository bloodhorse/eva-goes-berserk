Our provisions are low--no beef; a little flour--the last 4 lbs. in the mess; we have 8 days rations of the latter left. We will try for a few hours more to keep going, and then, unless some better fortune comes, we must give up. But we will never say die till the last. There is no knowing what may happen. My own heart is heavy--my hands are weary--I am tired. If I only had a chance to rest, for 4 or 5 days, I could start again.
