We did some cooking this afternoon, as there were two canning jars that might be used. I broke up a piece of frozen beef, and put a pinch of pepper, salt and sugar to it; boiled the whole lot till it was thoroughly done, then I canned it in those two jars; but we won't open them until we have got well to the North again, for fear of spoiling the meat. We are camped now in a shelter of the snowdrift; I think I have never been so near death, for I felt as if the cold had taken hold of my brain. If this doesn't end soon we'll never get through.
