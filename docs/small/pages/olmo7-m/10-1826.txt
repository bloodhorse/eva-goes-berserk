“You have grown so big,” said my aunt. But my uncle said nothing. He stood watching me from a corner.

Then my uncle and aunt began talking to me. I tried to make sense of it. “He has not spoken a word to me since I came here,” said my uncle. “You should talk to him,” he added to his wife. But she only shook her head.

“I would talk to him myself, but I don’t know how to begin,” said she, “I feel as though I had gone through a fire.”

We went out on the back lawn, and the trees in the corner hid the house, but it could still be seen through the branches. “You have grown,” said my uncle.

He made as if to take me by the hand and then let his fingers drop to his sides.
