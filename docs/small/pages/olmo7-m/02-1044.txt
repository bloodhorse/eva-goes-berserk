“I am a house,” said the first one, “and I want to go to the market.” I walked on.

The houses stood on each side of me, and their voices became louder.

“Be careful! Watch out for those men who come from the north, who live in houses that look just
like this.”

I asked him if they came from the North.
He answered: “No, they come from the East. But you should not be seen in this part of the country;
they do not like you and will arrest you.”
