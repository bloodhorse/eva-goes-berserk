 Nothing answered, except the cold and the emptiness. And it was still cold and still empty.

“Then what am I doing here?”

“You don’t know me. You are cold and there’s nothing for me to sit on. And you don’t know where you are or who you are. Why do you keep coming here? There is nothing for you here.
