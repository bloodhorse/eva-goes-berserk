The house had been built before anyone thought about the radio waves that spread from a wireless,
carrying your voice. There was a huge hole in the ground, like a crater, with a spiral staircase that went
downwards. The steps were made of concrete, and were as thick as your thigh. And they were cold,
so cold it made me shiver when I walked on them, but there was nothing else to do, so I kept going.

At the bottom I was in a big round room. A man was there, in the middle, on his knees, and he was
making a circle with his finger. He had a candle burning and he was singing, and it was a very long song,
a song that would never finish. And the flame grew larger
