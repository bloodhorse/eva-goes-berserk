 like hands. sometimes the wind comes up again and rips at it. sometimes it comes down like rain and the wind blows my hair up, then lets it fall in my face like a curtain of brown and green. the leaves on my head make a halo around me.

my father sees this. he laughs and calls me a girl from a fairy tale. my mother tells him to stop being so silly and i tell them both i can see the trees, that there are birds flying on them, that the grass is wet and i am holding a stone.

and they don’t believe me. not my mother. and not my father. not the teacher. not anyone. no one ever believes me. i can hear them laughing at me as i run off the path, as they follow the trail of my footsteps across the grass, through the forest
