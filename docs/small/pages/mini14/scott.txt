I can write, though can see nothing of my paper. If you can read this and tell me what you see, I shall be quite pleased. All right. My left eye is quite clear again; it will come. It has been like the darkest of nights. The only thing to be done now is to go on and let nature help us; at least that is all I know how to do, and I don't want to do any differently.
