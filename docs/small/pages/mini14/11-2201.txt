 Then it said, “What do you mean?”

The house said, “Who are you?”

Then I told the house what my name is, but it didn’t say anything. Then I said that it would be good to get in because the wind was blowing snow.

But it still wouldn’t let me in and then it said, “I’ve got no windows or doors.”

It said that I must have some mistake and then it said that the owner of the house had died long ago.

“Can I live with you?” I asked the house and it said that I could and I asked it if I could have a room all to myself, but it said there were none.

It said I could take the kitchen but there were no chairs. It said I could use the stove and the pots and the pans but there were no pots or pans
