 sometimes when the moon is low in the sky i look up and i see myself.

one morning i wake up, and find myself sitting on a bench by the river, i can hear the water bubbling as it goes by. a bird is singing, its feathers are blue and green like the sea. its song makes my head feel dizzy with joy. then the bird flies away and i am alone again. but this time there is another person sitting on the bench with me.

it’s a woman, she has dark hair and brown eyes, her lips are soft and full, and they look so kind and warm. i smile at her, but she does not return my smile. instead she looks at me with pitying eyes, then she gets up and walks away. i want to stop her, tell her how beautiful i think she is
