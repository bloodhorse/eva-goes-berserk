All of them carried parcels, each wrapped separately in white paper and tied up with string. The
string on some was red, and the strings of others were green, yellow or blue; but what they were
for nobody knew.

Some of the packages were heavy; they dragged their feet as they walked and their heads hung
low. Others walked on tiptoes, so lightly that they hardly left any footprints, and they sang as if they
had just finished an exhausting race. A few were very small; but they also hurried on and on as if
they were grown-ups; they too had something to carry.
Every moment, there was a commotion, a call from some of those who were carrying heavier loads.
