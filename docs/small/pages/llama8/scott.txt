* * * * *

We made about 16 miles to-day; all day it snowed, but the drifts have ceased. There are only six of us now.

March 19th.--It is hard for the others, but I have been used to this kind of life; besides, I have some means of alleviating pain and taking sleep; it is this last which matters most, so I will give it. A good draught of chloral and bromide makes me drowsy in
