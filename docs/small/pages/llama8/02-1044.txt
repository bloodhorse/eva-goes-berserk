“What is happening in the town?”

“No idea,” said one. “Everyone’s running towards town.”

“But we don’t know why,” said another.

“There’s no point in asking them, either,” said a third, “for everyone is running the opposite way,
back again.”

“Well, it’s obvious what’s happening, if only I knew how I’d got into it,” said one, looking at the sky,
where there was no sign of a cloud.
It looked just as usual, only a bit lighter than before. Then he said to another: “If you were
me, would you know any more about it than I do?” And he smiled a friendly smile and laughed and
pointed his finger at him, like a little boy.

The man he pointed at was tall, and wore glasses on his nose.
