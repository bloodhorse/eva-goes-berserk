“I am tired,” my uncle said suddenly.

It was my first visit home for many months; my uncle and aunt seemed glad to see me. There were some
teaspoons on a plate by the side of the fire, but the plate was cracked.

“They have come,” my uncle said.

He looked up, and saw the cracks in the plaster.

“I cannot stand any more of them,” he said. He rose, and his legs wavered as they walked to the table,
on which were laid some pieces of bread, with butter. As he cut the loaf, it was clear to me that there was a small crack down one side. There were two chairs and a table, but both seemed too small for me. It was a long time since I had seen my uncle and aunt; they were older now.
