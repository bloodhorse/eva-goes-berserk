 Houses always have blankets. Or soapsuds for your hair and the feeling that you will never again have to shampoo anything ever. A house would understand if I were to tell it, “I am cold and I need a blanket and also a towel.” It’s what people say in commercials about houses and baths, even if they never say anything about actually living in them.

I tried not to move or cry too much.

I knew the house wouldn’t mind if I cried, but I thought it might start to worry if I got really loud and my sobs were too much to take in, or if they didn’t go away for a few days or months and became part of the house forever like wallpaper, so I made my sobs as small as they could possibly be. Like tiny birds with beaks too little
