We were out 7 hours 30 minutes; this is about 9-1/4 knots. There are many cracks in the ice now; one might be in serious danger if his dog broke through; also if he fell in--the water would get into every part of the clothing, and freeze at once.

In our present condition, no one could live more than two or three hours. This is one of the reasons for never going off the track; besides, of course, it is impossible to see where it leads; you may go miles wrong way.
