 pulling my head backwards, down into the dirt.

and when it is hot i take it apart piece by piece, untangle each knot. the roots are heavy in the hot air and i can hear them groaning. the ends of my hair fall to my ankles. they brush against my feet. i wash my hair with a bar of soap i made from goat’s milk and lavender.

in the evening the sky becomes pink like a bruise and the wind smells of flowers and salt water. i do not look back as the sky turns to fire.

on some days it is the rain. on other days, the sun is so hot the sky seems to melt. there are days where it does not stop snowing for hours, where the ground is white, the air cold. the grass is full of dew.
