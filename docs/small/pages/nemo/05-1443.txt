It’s what she wanted. She didn’t want bread; she wanted pain.

When the pain started I stood at the sink and counted to one hundred, because that was what they said to do
at the clinic, so the midwives could check if you were having real contractions or just practising, and when
they asked me why I’d started so early I would say it was because of all those long walks, up the hill,
up and down. When the pain was bad I bent over the sink and I spat up all the pain I could, but I still
couldn’t see anything in the water.
