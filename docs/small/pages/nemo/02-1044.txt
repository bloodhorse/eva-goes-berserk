Some of them walked slowly along; and then you could pass on by. Others stood in groups;
and if you had no time to go round, you pushed your way through as quickly as you could.
There were always a number of these standing still; some were quite motionless; others were
walking slowly from side to side; and still more were leaning against walls or railings, so
that you could only get past by going between them. But it was more difficult with the people
who were coming and going: you were always getting into their way and being pushed aside.
