Then the staircase creaked as my aunt went up to the kitchen where we could smell something cooking.

I felt cold.

My uncle and aunt took me up the stairs, into the small bedroom at the back. I felt the chill in my chest when my eyes went from the faded bed to the cupboard with the toys, the dressing table where I had dressed for school in the mornings. A tiny chest of drawers.

My aunt, who was tall, went behind the wardrobe and opened it, took out the bottom shelf. The shelf was made of wood, not glass. The same piece of wood, it looked. The shelves of the other wardrobes were all clear glass. There was no space to hide anything here. I knew the bottom shelf by the top of my head, so that I knew where it had to go.
