Monday, March 19.--We have been on foot over 40 miles; no rest, no food except a little hard boiled bacon. No sleep but lying on the ground all day in cold, with snow, rain and sleet. Drifts so deep and thick that it took us hours to wade through them, and sometimes not able to get through. My right foot is broken; it feels as if it would go if I didn't have it held on.
