A young woman was walking with a little child by the side of her.
The child said to its mother, “Mother, let’s go to town, too.”

She replied, “No, let’s not.” They went away.

When we had passed a hundred paces further I looked round; there was no one left.

It was about noon.

A little boy of six or seven was running along a footpath by a tree. His hands and his arms were
filled with branches and twigs; he had not tied them together; the branches were simply stuck
in the air behind him, like a child’s kite with long, trailing sticks.
