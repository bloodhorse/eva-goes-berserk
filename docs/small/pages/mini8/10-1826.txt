Everything was dull. We sat down at table. We did not begin at once. Then my aunt looked at me over her glasses. “So you’re here at last.”

My uncle, who had not yet looked at me, suddenly put out his hand towards the clock and wound it. The hands stood still.

My uncle was wearing a pink waistcoat. He looked at me out of eyes that were blue and gray like a cat’s.
I think they were meant to be pleasant, but I thought they looked sad. His hair, which had been black, was now a dead white. The black of the eyes seemed to have been diluted.

“Well,” said my uncle. “What do you think?”
“I think I’m not very happy.”
