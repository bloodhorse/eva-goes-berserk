“Come to me,” said another voice. It called and called again: “Come here!” But the voice came from
so many sides that it was no use to answer; the voice always came from the other side, wherever
you were standing.
The river lay deep beneath the town. Its dark blue waves lapped against the steep walls. There
was no water left on the river bank. It was completely dried out; the dusty sand glittered like glass
under the sun, and in some places the yellow mud was already cracking. The little bridge was
there, with a foot path that went alongside; and the road from the bridge went straight through the
town to the place where it divided into the three main streets. In the distance, beyond the
town, the hills stretched away.
