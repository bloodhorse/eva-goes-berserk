Monday, March 19.--We march on and get out of the drifts and into open country--there is no more drift, or snow; there are long stretches of dry, brown ground between the drifts. I am walking on my left foot. I try to make the bandage go as lightly as I can; I know if I give up that bandage I will be ruined--the wound is deep, and is very bad, and must be kept dry. I hope we won't be snow-bound.
