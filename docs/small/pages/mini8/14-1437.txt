 some ghosts can remember their whole life; others lose it all; it just depends on what happens after death.
i've heard people say that a soul can live on in this world through its child. but it can also be carried on through a dog, or an old chair, or even a memory; whatever you hold closest, is what you carry with you after you die.
but my mother was always too afraid to try any of that. i've been trying ever since.
when i finally found her, she didn't look so beautiful anymore, but she was still there. and for that, i was grateful, because it meant that i had made it to heaven; no matter what shape or form it might take.
after all, there was no such thing as a lost ghost.
